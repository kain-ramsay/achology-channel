## Body

CBT course levels and diplomas can look like a secret code. Nobody explains what the numbers mean.

They're not really about CBT at all. They're the UK's general qualification levels. The same numbers apply to any subject a course teaches.

A higher number simply means a more advanced qualification. Each one roughly matches something you already know from school or university.

Think of it like exam grades. A GCSE and a degree are both real, they just sit at very different points on the same ladder.

Nobody thinks a GCSE is fake just because a degree sits higher up. Both are real. They just measure different things, at different points in time.

## What Do the Levels Actually Mean?

Level 3 sits next to A levels. That's the usual post-school qualification.

Level 5 sits next to a foundation degree, or an HND. That's roughly two years of university study.

Level 6 sits next to a full bachelor's degree. Level 7 sits next to a master's degree, or a postgraduate diploma. Most accredited CBT therapist training runs at Level 7.

## What Does "Advanced Diploma" Mean?

It usually means a course around Level 3 or Level 4. That's more than an introduction, but well short of postgraduate level.

It's a real qualification. It just isn't an especially high one on the ladder.

The word "advanced" is doing some marketing work here, not just describing the course. Always check the actual level number behind it.

It's a bit like a shop calling a jumper "premium". That word alone doesn't tell you much until you check what it's actually made of.

The same goes for a lot of course names. A nice-sounding title tells you very little on its own. The number next to it tells you far more.

## Why Does the Level Matter to Anyone Hiring?

Because it's a quick, trusted shorthand. An employer looking at a CV can tell roughly how much study a Level 7 qualification took, without asking any further questions.

That shorthand only works for regulated qualifications, the ones with an official level attached. It doesn't tell an employer anything about a course that sits outside the framework entirely.

## Where Do Achology's CBT Certificates Sit?

Nowhere on this ladder, and that's on purpose, not an accident. Achology's certificates don't carry a level number at all.

They prove something else instead. They prove you've done the course and can actually use CBT well.

That's a different promise from a Level 6 or Level 7 qualification. It's labelled honestly as exactly that, nothing more.

## So, What Do CBT Course Levels and Diplomas Mean for You?

If you want a regulated qualification for a clinical career, you need one with a real level attached. Achology has never claimed to offer that.

If you want to genuinely understand and use CBT yourself, a level number won't tell you much either way. What matters there is whether you can actually apply it. That's what competency means, and it's what an Achology course is built to give you.

## Do You Actually Need to Know Any of This?

Only if you're comparing courses side by side. If you're picking one for its own sake, the number matters far less than what you'll actually be able to do once you finish.

Most people asking this question just want a clear, honest answer. Not a maze of letters and digits. That's what this page has tried to give you, in plain words rather than jargon.
