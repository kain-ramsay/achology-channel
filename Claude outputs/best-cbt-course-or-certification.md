## Body

What is the best CBT course or certification? It depends entirely on why you're asking, because "best" means something different to three different kinds of buyer.

There's the licensed clinician who needs continuing professional development hours. There's the curious beginner who wants a cheap taste of the ideas. And there's the person who wants to actually use CBT on themselves and the people around them, without becoming a clinician.

Each of those three needs a genuinely different course, and no single list can honestly rank all three against each other.

## What's the Best CBT Course If You're Already a Clinician?

If you're a licensed mental health professional needing CPD, look at providers built specifically for that: the [Beck Institute](https://beckinstitute.org/certification/), PESI, and Psychwire. All three exist to serve clinicians who already hold a professional licence.

The Beck Institute, founded on Aaron Beck's own work, only certifies clinicians who already hold a mental health degree and have completed 2,000 hours of supervised client work. That's a genuinely high bar, and it's the right one for that audience.

None of these providers are built for someone without a clinical licence already, and none of them should be. If that's not you, the "best" course for you is somewhere else on this page, not here.

## What's the Best CBT Course If You Just Want a Cheap Taste of the Ideas?

Generic marketplace courses, the kind you'll find in bulk on platforms like Udemy, are honestly fine for this. They're cheap, they're short, and they'll give you the basic vocabulary of CBT.

What they rarely give you is structure, practice exercises that build on each other, or anyone checking whether the ideas actually landed. For a first taste at low cost, that trade-off is a reasonable one to make, as long as you go in knowing that's what you're buying.

If you finish one of these and want to go further, that's usually the moment to look at a fuller course rather than another cheap one covering the same ground again.

## What's the Best CBT Course If You Want to Actually Use CBT Yourself?

This is the aim Achology's own courses are built for. The [CBT Practitioner Course](/academy/cognitive-behavioural-psychology/cbt-practitioner/) teaches you to use CBT on your own thinking first, and then on the people you work with, whether that's as a coach, a manager, a teacher, or a parent.

It is not clinical training, and it was never meant to replace the Beck Institute route for someone who wants to treat patients. Achology's own position is competence before certification: you learn to use the method well before anyone hands you a certificate for it.

That's a genuinely different promise from either a clinical CPD provider or a cheap marketplace course, and it's the one that fits most people who ask this question in the first place.

## So, What Is the Best CBT Course or Certification, Really?

There isn't one single winner here, only the right course for what you're actually trying to do. Work out which of the three buyers above you are, and the choice mostly makes itself.

If you're still unsure, ask yourself honestly what you'll actually do with the course once it's finished. That single question usually settles it faster than any comparison chart ever could.
