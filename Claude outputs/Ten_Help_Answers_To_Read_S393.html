What do the main schools of psychology study?

  • The psychoanalytic school: the unconscious mind, and how it shapes what we think and do.

  • The developmental school: how people grow and change across the whole of their lives.

  • The cognitive school: mental processes such as attention and memory.

  • The humanistic school: individual potential and personal growth.

  • The school of social-psychological thought: how people deal with one another, and how society shapes what they think and do.

Can you become a psychologist online in the United States?

In the United States, the American Psychological Association (APA) says that each state makes its own laws on the education a psychologist needs to get a licence. Some states ask for a degree from an APA-accredited programme. The APA also says to check with the licensing body in the state where you'd like to practise.

Texas shows what one state asks for. Its State Board of Examiners of Psychologists requires a doctoral degree in psychology, including an internship. It also asks for 1,750 supervised hours during that degree and 1,750 more after it, and applicants have to pass an exam as well.

What does the road look like in the United Kingdom?

The British Psychological Society (BPS) says an accredited psychology degree is the first step. That degree should lead to the Graduate Basis for Chartered Membership (GBC), which the BPS says you need for accredited postgraduate training. If your degree isn't accredited, an accredited conversion course can still get you there.

The BPS says it takes several years to become a fully qualified Chartered Psychologist. That means a three-year degree and then three more years of postgraduate training. The kind of training depends on the field you choose.

The law adds a second layer, and the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC) explains it. In the United Kingdom, nine titles are protected, such as clinical psychologist, and using one without being on the HCPC Register is a criminal offence. It adds that the word psychologist on its own isn't protected by law.

Does studying online change any of this?

None of these pages says whether a degree taken online meets the rules, so that's a question for the body that sets them, not a course provider. The BPS has a "Find an accredited course" search, and the APA says to ask your state's licensing body. It's worth making those checks before you pay, however you'd like to study.

Where can you study behavioural psychology online?

So where can you study behavioural psychology online, and does it have to mean a degree? It doesn't. You can begin with a free university taster or go as far as an online degree, and Achology's diploma is one more way in.

Behavioural psychology is the study of how behaviour is learned. It looks at how a response gets linked to a trigger. Any adult who's curious about it is welcome to begin.

Can you learn behavioural psychology online for free?

You can start for free. Yale's Introduction to Psychology, taught by Paul Bloom on Coursera, lets you enrol at no cost. A certificate is optional and costs extra.

The course is listed at two weeks of about ten hours a week. Its second module covers conditioning and the work of B.F. Skinner. That gives you a first taste of the behavioural side.

The Open University has a free course too, called Starting with Psychology, on its OpenLearn site. It's a beginner course of about five hours, with a free statement of participation at the end. Its sections cover wider topics, such as group pressure and what makes us who we are.

How might this show up in your own day?

Picture the moment you sit on the sofa and reach for your phone. You haven't decided anything yet. Behavioural psychology asks what that habit is tied to, and what happens straight after it.

Behavioural psychology says a reward after a behaviour can make it more likely to happen again. You don't need a lab to try this. A favourite habit and a small notebook will do.

Say you tidy one drawer and it feels good. So you tidy another the next day. In this field, that small reward is one way a habit gets started.
