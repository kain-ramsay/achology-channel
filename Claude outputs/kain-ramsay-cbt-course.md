## Body

What is Kain Ramsay's CBT course? There isn't just one. The Kain Ramsay CBT course question actually covers three separate courses at Achology, each built for a different starting point.

Achology itself was founded in 2017, in Scotland, by Kain and Karen Ramsay. All three of its CBT courses carry his name as the teacher.

Think of it a bit like an author with several books on the same subject. Each one covers real ground of its own, and none of them is a lesser or padded-out version of another.

## What Are the Three Courses, Exactly?

The main one is the [Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) Practitioner Course](/academy/cognitive-behavioural-psychology/cbt-practitioner/): 169,171 students, more than 31 hours, across 119 lessons. It's the full practitioner-level training, and by far the largest of the three.

The easiest start is [The CBT Toolkit: Core Principles and Real-World Applications](/academy/cognitive-behavioural-psychology/cbt-toolkit/): 4,853 students, more than 14 hours, across 46 lessons. It covers the core ideas without the full practitioner depth.

The third is [Cognitive Behavioural Therapy for Mental Health and Wellness](/academy/cognitive-behavioural-psychology/cbt-mental-health/): 5,898 students, more than 16 hours, across 52 lessons. It applies CBT specifically to everyday mental health and wellness.

## Which One Should You Actually Start With?

If you want the full practitioner training in one course, start with the Practitioner Course. It's the biggest, the most complete, and the one most people mean when they ask about his CBT course by name.

If you want a shorter first look before committing to that, the Toolkit is built for exactly that. You can always move to the Practitioner Course afterwards, since neither locks you out of the other.

Your interest might be narrower than either of those, just using CBT for your own mental health and wellness day to day. The third course is built precisely for that, rather than the broader practitioner training.

## Is This Clinical Training?

No, and it's honest to be clear about that from the start. These are practitioner-level courses, built for using CBT well, not clinical courses that qualify you to treat patients.

That distinction matters, so this page won't blur it just to sound more impressive. Anyone wanting the clinical, NHS-recognised route needs a different path entirely, one this page doesn't offer.

None of that makes these courses less real. It just means they're honestly labelled for what they actually are, rather than stretched to sound like something they aren't.

## Are These Courses Accredited?

They're not on the UK's regulated qualifications ladder, and that's a deliberate choice, not an oversight. Achology is listed on the UK Register of Learning Providers (UKRLP) with the UK Provider Number [10099815](https://ukrlp.education.gov.uk/search/provider/10099815?query=Achology&page=1&ukprnStatus=active), which is a different thing from a regulated qualification.

Each course still awards its own Achology certificate on completion, proving you finished the training and can use what it teaches. It's a fair, honest claim, not an inflated one.

## So, What Is the Kain Ramsay CBT Course, in Short?

Three courses, not one: a flagship Practitioner Course with 169,171 students, a shorter Toolkit for a first look, and a Mental Health and Wellness course for everyday use. All three carry his name, and all three are real, published figures, not estimates.

Whichever one someone means when they search for it, all three sit under the same answer. That's the honest shape of the question, even when the search itself expects a single result.
