## Body

Can you study CBT online? Yes, easily, and it's actually the most common way people learn it today.

There are a few real kinds of course, though, and they're not interchangeable. Picking the right one depends on what you actually want from it.

## Self-Paced Online Practitioner Courses

This is Achology's own format. You work through it on your own schedule, with no fixed class dates or cohort to keep up with.

You get real structure, built lessons in order, but you set the pace. That suits people fitting learning around a job or a family, rather than a fixed timetable.

## Accredited University Diplomas, Part-Time and Online

Universities including Oxford offer postgraduate diplomas in CBT, and many run part-time with online components. These ask for a relevant degree and prior clinical experience already in hand.

They lead somewhere specific: a clinical qualification, on the road to becoming an accredited CBT therapist. That's years of commitment, not a weekend.

## Short, Cheap Marketplace Courses

Generic platforms sell short CBT introductions, often under a few hours long. They're fine for a first taste of the vocabulary, at low cost.

What they rarely give you is depth, or anyone checking whether the ideas actually landed before you move on. Treat them as a starting point, not a destination.

## Live, Cohort-Based Online Training

Some providers run CBT training as live online sessions, with fixed start dates and a group moving through it together. This suits someone who wants a set schedule and other people to learn alongside.

The trade-off is flexibility. If a session falls at a bad time, you miss it, unlike a self-paced course you can pick up whenever suits you.

## So, Which Kind of Online CBT Course Is Right for You?

If you want a clinical qualification, the university route is the honest answer, whatever the wait and cost. If you want to understand and use CBT well yourself, without treating patients, a self-paced practitioner course does that properly.

Neither is wrong. They're simply built for different aims, and knowing which one is yours makes the choice a lot easier.
