Q1 On your own

Opening, second sentence onward

Yes, you can learn psychology on your own, at home. You don't need to enrol anywhere or ask anyone's permission, and you can begin tonight. What studying at home won't do is make you a registered psychologist, because in many countries, including the United States, Canada, Australia, India, the United Kingdom and France, a title like that comes with rules of its own.

Section: Will learning psychology at home make you a registered psychologist?

In British Columbia, Canada, the BC College of Health and Care Professionals says the title psychologist can only be used by people it licenses, unless an exemption applies. In Australia, the Psychology Board of Australia says psychologists must be registered to practise.

In France, the Ministry of Higher Education says access to the title psychologist is regulated, and a licence plus a master's in psychology allows its professional use. In India, the Rehabilitation Council of India lists clinical psychologists on its Central Rehabilitation Register. In the United States, the American Psychological Association says each state makes its own laws on the education a psychologist needs.

In the United Kingdom, the Health and Care Professions Council says the job title psychologist isn't protected by law on its own, though nine longer titles are. Wherever you live, your own country's regulator or professional body is the place to ask what its titles need.
