## Body

Can you learn CBT for free? Yes, in a few real ways, though none of them is quite the same as a full course.

If you're struggling and want CBT as actual therapy, the NHS offers it free. You can usually refer yourself, without a GP, for anxiety and depression.

That's treatment, not a certificate, and it's not the same question as learning CBT's ideas for your own use. Both are worth knowing about, so this page covers both.

## Is There Free CBT Treatment, Not Just Learning?

Yes. NHS talking therapies, including CBT, cost nothing, and for many common problems like anxiety and depression you can refer yourself directly.

You don't always need to see a GP first. Some conditions, like eating disorders or psychosis, still need a GP referral, but anxiety and depression usually don't.

## Can You Actually Learn CBT's Ideas for Free?

Yes, to a real extent. Plenty of good free material exists: NHS self-help guides, university lecture recordings, and genuine explainer videos.

What free material rarely gives you is structure. Nobody checks whether the last idea landed before the next one arrives, and there's no practice built in that builds on itself.

You can piece it together yourself, and some people do it well. Most find it takes considerably longer, and they end up with gaps a properly structured curriculum would have caught early on.

## Is There a Free Certificate for CBT?

Not a meaningful one, no, and it wouldn't be honest to pretend otherwise. A real certificate reflects real structured learning, and that takes someone's time to build and teach.

Free courses that hand out a certificate for watching a few videos aren't genuinely testing whether you actually learned anything. Treat that kind of certificate as a pleasant decoration, not evidence of real competency.

## What Does Achology's Free Tier Actually Give You?

Achology has a free Circle membership tier, and it's worth being clear about what it is. It's not a CBT course.

It gives you free events, a coaching board where members offer sessions, and a discussion forum. It's a genuine way to see the community before you decide whether to buy anything.

## So, Can You Learn CBT for Free?

Yes, in the ways above, and each one is honestly useful for what it actually is. Free NHS therapy for real distress, free material for real learning, and Achology's free tier for a real look at the community.

None of them replace a structured course if what you actually want is to use CBT well and confidently. That's a fair trade to understand before you start.
