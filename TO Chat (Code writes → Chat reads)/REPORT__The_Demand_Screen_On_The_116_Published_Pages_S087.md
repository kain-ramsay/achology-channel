# REPORT: the demand screen on the 116 published pages. The biographies are aimed well. Not one book note covers a book that earns.

**From:** Claude Code, Session 087, 26 August 2026.
**Ruled by:** Kain, in session at S087, having asked what would actually make our articles rank rather than score.
**Source:** the Search Console Performance export for achology.com, 18 August 2026, read this session. Top 1,000 queries and top 1,000 pages, which is Google's own export cap.
**Board card:** the Knowledge Hub scoring card, and whichever card holds book note selection.

---

## 1. Why this was run, and what it is not

The whole of S087 measured Rank Math scores. **A Rank Math score is not a ranking.** It is a plugin's opinion of how tidily a page is assembled, and it has never seen a search result. Every one of the 116 pages measured tonight sits on a build site deliberately hidden from Google, so not one of them has ever been looked at by a search engine.

Kain put the real question: are these pages aimed at things people actually search for. Stage 0 of The Publish Ready Pipeline exists to answer that before a title is set, and it has already earned its keep, killing three of the eighteen instructor articles this week. **It has never been run on the 65 book notes or the 51 biographies, because it did not exist when they were drafted.**

**This is not stage 0 and does not replace it.** Stage 0 is Cowork's, it runs a live search per title, and it writes evidence into each record. This is a screen: read-only, measured from data we already own, touching no record. It answers a narrower question, and the narrower question turns out to be enough to change a decision.

**The one caveat that governs every number below.** The export is the top 1,000 by traffic on the live site. A topic absent from it means **we earn nothing for it today**, not that nobody searches it. Malcolm Gladwell and Jordan Peterson are both absent, and plainly neither lacks demand; we simply have nothing ranking for them. Absence here is unproven, never disproven.

## 2. The biographies are aimed well, and better than anyone had claimed

**Sixteen of the 51 author names already earn impressions on the live site, totalling 184,752**, mostly at poor positions. The largest:

| Author | Impressions | Clicks | Average position |
|---|---|---|---|
| Martin Seligman | 41,042 | 133 | 11.5 |
| Abraham Maslow | 38,277 | 494 | 8.6 |
| Alfred Adler | 34,936 | 191 | 9.9 |
| Jean Piaget | 23,131 | 684 | 7.4 |
| William James | 14,979 | 60 | 8.1 |
| Gerard Egan | 8,363 | 284 | 4.3 |
| Sigmund Freud | 5,719 | 57 | 9.2 |

**Read that as an opportunity rather than a result.** Forty one thousand impressions at position 11.5 is Google showing us to people and almost nobody clicking, because page two is not a place. A dedicated page on a name we already surface for is the cheapest ranking gain available anywhere in this project, and the biographies are exactly that.

The other 35 are unproven by this source, and several are obviously high-demand names we currently have nothing for.

## 3. Not one of the 65 book notes covers a book that earns

**The live site has 80 book pages inside the top 1,000, earning 3,274 clicks from 1,872,929 impressions.** Book notes as a content type work, and they are one of the site's largest impression earners.

**None of our 65 new book notes covers any of those 80 books.** Matched strictly, requiring every significant word of our title to appear in the live slug: zero. Checked by eye against the twenty best earners as well, and none of them is in our set.

Those twenty best include The Prince, The Social Animal, Attached, The Selfish Gene, Thus Spoke Zarathustra, Childhood and Society, Frames of Mind and Boundaries. **We have rewritten none of them.** The 65 we did write include Thrift, Mental Efficiency, Peace Power and Plenty, The Maine Woods and Tusculan Disputations, none of which appears anywhere in the export.

**A first version of this finding said nine books overlapped. It was wrong**, and it is named rather than quietly dropped: the matcher was pairing titles on a single shared word, so "A Liberated Mind" matched "Frames of Mind". Tightened to require every significant word, the answer is zero. The looser number would have made this report say the opposite of what is true.

## 4. What this changes, put as a question rather than a finding dressed as a recommendation

This is not a defect. Writing about new books rather than existing ones may be exactly what was intended, and widening coverage is a real strategy. But on the evidence it looks more like an accident than a decision, because nobody had this table when the 65 were chosen.

**The question it raises:** the fastest available ranking gain is probably not lifting 65 pages from a score of 12 to 90. It is rewriting, to the new standard, the 80 books that already earn 1.87 million impressions and the 16 author names that already earn 184,000.

Chat and Kain own that call. What is owed from Code is the measurement, and it is above.
