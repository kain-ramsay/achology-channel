## How it was ruled, and this part matters more than the face

**Literata held for one day and Kain overturned it himself.** He ruled it at S084 against the old face alone, then reopened it the next morning and said the true thing about how it had been put to him: one candidate shown beside the current face is a recommendation wearing a comparison's clothes.

**He then named the axis himself**: the answer sits between Poppins, which the site used to pair with Como and which he liked but found not mature enough, and Lora. He also said two things that decided which faces went on it. That the current face reads **sharp, dry and lifeless**. And that he finds it **hard to focus on the text** on the article pages.

**That second sentence is a specification, not a mood.** Focus is bought with a large x-height, low contrast between thick and thin, and open counters: the letters fill the line, the page does not sparkle, and the eye stops working to hold its place. Every candidate in the middle of the spread was chosen on those three properties first and its personality second.

**The sitting he ruled from carried nine faces**, the live face first as the control, four whole pages of his own copy, one face on screen at a time in the identical position, and the chosen face following him between pages. He asked for the control and for the page switcher himself, mid-sitting, and both were missing when he asked. Recorded because the instrument is now right and the next face decision should use it as it stands.
