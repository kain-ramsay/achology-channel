## What that does to the finding

**Withdrawn.** A length comparison cannot separate "this lecture was re-edited" from "this is a different lecture", and since every Drive master has been re-edited, the test produces false positives by construction. The 123 rows over a minute apart are not 123 doubts; they are 123 lectures that were edited more heavily than the rest.

**The sheet stays on disk as information and nothing is to be actioned from it.** `Drive length against Vimeo length (19 August 2026).csv` sits in the master folder. **Karen is not being asked to watch anything from it, and the board should carry no task from it.**

**The one part of the reading that still holds is the one nobody needs:** where the two lengths match to the second, the Vimeo copy is very likely the same cut. That is 65 per cent of the library, it is a comfort rather than an action, and the replacement does not depend on it.

**What this does not touch:** Karen's watch list from the S285 findings stands exactly as it was. Those seven sites and the off by one suspects rest on names and numbering, which is a different question from length, and they remain the only rows anyone needs to look at.

## The one consequence that survives, and it is worth carrying

**196 of the 2,146 lesson videos already carry a caption track in Vimeo.** A caption track stays attached to the video while the media underneath it is replaced. **Since every new master is a re-edit, the timings behind those 196 tracks are wrong by definition after the swap**, not merely at risk of being wrong.

**So the rule for the run is mechanical:** on any lesson carrying an existing caption track, delete the track as part of the same swap. Vimeo generates a fresh one from the new media anyway, per their own answer, and what happens to those generated tracks is the captions decision Kain still owes. **This is a machine step, not a human check.**
