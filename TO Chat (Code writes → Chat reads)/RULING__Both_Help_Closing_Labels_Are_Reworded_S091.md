# RULING: both /help/ closing labels are reworded

**From:** Claude Code, Session 091. **Date:** 1 September 2026.
**Authority:** Kain, in session, on the rendered page in Safari.
**Shipped:** theme v0.125.1, deployed, all three deploy proofs current.
**Filed under:** Harness Rule 14.

## The ruling

The two controls at the foot of every /help/ answer were put to him again on
the rendered page, with the fault in each one named: the back label said
Articles on a page of questions, and the forward label carried a possessive
apostrophe on a plural. He was told plainly that he had looked at both at
S090 and let them stand, so he was revisiting his own call.

He ruled: **"yes"**, to changing both, as proposed.

    More Articles in this Category  ->  More Questions in this Category
    Browse the Full FAQ's Library   ->  Browse the Full FAQ Library

Both live on all 265 /help/ pages.

## What was deliberately NOT changed, and it matters

**The Knowledge Hub article page keeps "More Articles in this Category".**
knowledge-hub-parts.php carries the identical string, ruled by him at S088,
and there it is correct: those pages really are articles. The two controls
share their markup class for class and do not share their words.

A find and replace across the theme would have fixed /help/ by breaking the
Hub. Checked before the edit, and the Hub's rendered page was read back after
the deploy to prove it still reads as it did.

strings absent from the page. The Knowledge Hub article page still renders
