# RULING: 880 is the container width for every Knowledge Hub content page, and it is never asked again

**From:** Claude Code, Session 85. **Date:** 26 August 2026.
**Authority:** Kain, ruled in session, on the rendered book note sitting at `achologytest.com/book-note/bn-sitting.html`, choosing Route C.
**Filed under Harness Rule 14**, in the same session the ruling was given.

## His words

> "C, the article measure - we must have consistency across all knowledge hub article pages Claude - do you understand - container width cannot be questioned again across the other quote and workbook pages that we havent worked on yet - 880 is the standard - exactly the same as what the help articles and policy pages should be."

## What he has ruled, stated so it cannot be read two ways

**880 is the container width for every content page on the site.** Not a preference per page and not a starting point. **It is not put to him again on any page**, including the quote page, the workbook page and every other template not yet built.

This closes a question that three documents currently answer differently, which is why it is worth more than the one page it was ruled on:

| Document | What it says today |
|---|---|
| DSRD 9 sections 22.2 and 27 | the article page reading column is 880 |
| DSRD 7 section 4 | a 620px prose column |
| The book note page as built | 792, which the S083 audit found was never in any document |

**All three are now superseded by one number.** Chat writes 880 into DSRD 7 section 4 as the container standard, and the two other rows agree with it rather than competing with it.

## What is true on the site today, measured this session rather than recalled

Every figure below was read off the rendered live page in a browser at a 1400px viewport, not from a stylesheet.

| Page type | Container today | Against the ruling |
|---|---|---|
| Help answers, all 250 | **880** | already correct, nothing to do |
| The book note page | **792** | moves to 880 |
| The seven policy pages | **620** | move to 880 |
| Quote, workbook, and the rest of the eleven | not built | built at 880 from the start |

So the ruling costs two changes and settles everything after them.

## One consequence Chat should carry into DSRD 7 section 4, because it is not a rename

**The 620px prose column was not an accident.** It is a readability standard for dense prose, and the policy pages are the densest writing on the site. Moving them to 880 makes their lines about forty per cent longer.

That matters more than usual this week, because Kain's own words the same morning were that he finds it hard to focus on the text, and line length is one of the two things that governs that. A help answer at 880 works because the writing is short. A privacy policy at 880 is a long line of dense text held for several screens.

**This is not a challenge to the ruling and it is not being put back to him as a question.** The ruling stands and 880 is the standard. It is named here so that DSRD 7 section 4 records what changed and why, rather than reading as though 620 was simply wrong. Code is showing him one policy page at 880 on the rendered page before sweeping all seven, which is the normal practice for a visible change rather than a re-opening.

3. **Every unbuilt page template's spec** written with 880 rather than leaving the width open, because Kain has ruled that the question does not travel again.
