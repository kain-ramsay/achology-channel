## The ruling

**A biography's Rank Math focus keyword is the person's name, on its own.** `Carl Jung`, not `who is Carl Jung`. Applied to all 51 records and all 51 drafts on the install.

Nothing else changed. No article body was touched, no title, no description, no address.

## What it was before, and why that was wrong

Every one of the 51 carried `who is {Name}`. As a statement of search intent that is right, and it is the same instinct behind Kain's S082 title pattern. As the field Rank Math actually checks, it was wrong, and measurably so.

Rank Math tests the focus keyword against the address, the opening paragraph, the subheadings and the body. **The literal phrase "who is Carl Jung" appeared in none of them, because nobody writing a decent biography repeats it.** Measured across the 51: zero occurrences in the body, zero in any heading, absent from every address. Six of the eight keyword checks failed on every page at once, which is why the scores clustered so tightly that the pattern was visible on sight.

The name alone is already in the address, the title, the headings and the body several times, so the same checks pass without a word of copy changing.

The run was done twice on the same pages with nothing else altered between them, so the lift is attributable and not inferred.

**Verified before writing, on all 51:** the new keyword appears in the post slug, in the Rank Math SEO title, and at least three times in the article body. Nothing was written that failed that check, and nothing did.
