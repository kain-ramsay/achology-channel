## What he asked, in his own words

> "How do we reframe all of the descriptions so that they loosely explain what is discussed in the lesson. We must frame each description in a way that tells the reader what is discussed in the video. Does this make sense?"

## How it was settled, and why it was not settled in words

Five lessons from across course 028 were rendered three ways in one page and opened in Safari: Karen's original, the shape that went live an hour earlier, and the reframe. **He picked the reframe by eye.** He was not asked to agree to a description of a shape, which is the failure mode this project keeps hitting.

## What the reframe actually changes

**Only the answer paragraph, and only its stance.** It now says what the lecture works through, and names who is speaking where that matters, instead of teaching the point with the recording out of view.

**Before**, 028-044: *"A company's financial statements have four parts: the balance sheet, the income statement..."*

**After**, 028-044: *"Kain puts the question to Ian Blackie, a chartered accountant, and the two go through the four parts of a company's financial statements in turn. A balance sheet gives a snapshot of assets and liabilities..."*

The answer still stands on its own, so the citeability mechanism the S288 commission rests on is intact: a reader who never watches the video still learns the thing.

## What is unchanged, so V2 is edited rather than rewritten

Three parts with a blank line between. Question line ending in a question mark. Answer of 60 to 85 words. Closing line of one or two sentences on what the learner does with it. **90 to 120 words in total.** Tier 1 and Tier 2 word standards. Nothing invented. The lesson title never repeated. `Standardised Description` only. `Lesson Description` never edited.

**The three named clichés stay banned.** "In this video", "In this lesson" and "Kain discusses" appear nowhere in the fifty. Kain asked for the reader to be told what is covered, not for those particular openings, and the machine check still refuses them.
