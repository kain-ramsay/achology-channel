## The pattern is supplied as an asset rather than described

`contour-mask.png`, beside the spec. White on transparency at full strength, already shaped to the block including behind the lettering, one file for all seven because the field depends only on the block's dimensions and those are identical across the pages.

Nobody can redraw a mathematical contour field by hand and nobody should try. The designer places it, clips it, and sets it to 5.5 per cent.

**One correction worth recording, because it is the kind that hides.** The first export had the lettering punched out of it, since it was drawn only on the block's coloured pixels. That looks identical in use and breaks the moment anything moves. It was found by compositing the mask over a dark ground and looking at it: a white-on-transparent asset is invisible against a white page, so opening it proves nothing.
