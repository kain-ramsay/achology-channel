## The ruling, in his words

Asked whether he wanted one footer everywhere or the two-footer split kept: **"yes, standardise all 250 on the trial panel."** Then, told the 15 category pages were a separate surface: **"yes, bring those 15 into line as well please."**

## What this supersedes, and it is a ruling of his own

**DSRD 1 section 8 is superseded in full for the /help/ section.** It carries the two-footer split by category type: the course routes on pre-purchase categories, the support routes on support categories. That was his, ruled 2 July 2026, with the wording ruled on 16 July.

**His reason for ending it:** a reader who has just had a question answered is in the same place whichever category they landed in.

**`achology_help_pair_panel()` in help-parts.php now has no callers anywhere on the site.** It is deliberately not deleted yet: section 8 still describes it as the /help/ close, and a function removed before its document is corrected leaves the document describing something that no longer exists. **Correct section 8 and the function goes with it.**

## The second change in the same template

The Related Questions block was reworked to read the way the article page's Related Further Reading block reads, on his instruction and in his words:

- the heading is **"Other Related Questions"**
- under it, **"Click on the links below for other commonly asked questions."**
- beneath the questions, the article page's own back and forward controls: **"More Articles in this Category"** and **"Browse the Full FAQ's Library"**
- a hairline under those, 48 above, his standing rule

The controls are the article page's markup class for class. Neither destination is written anywhere: the back one is the answer's own category term, the forward one is the help landing page.

**Two labels are his words and were not adjusted.** "More Articles in this Category" says Articles on a page of questions, and "FAQ's" carries a possessive apostrophe on a plural. Both were put to him in the sitting and both stand as he wrote them.

## ONE THING CARRIED TO THE NEXT SESSION, AND IT IS A REAL TRADE

**The nine support categories have lost their support routes.** The old support close offered "Contact Our Support Team" (mailto support@achology.com) and "Ask an Achologist Anything". Both went with the block.

**Measured on a live support page after the change:** no mailto link of any kind, no link to the enquiries form. Someone arriving with a billing problem is offered a $7 trial.

The help desk link is still in the footer, so nobody is stranded. But a support route was traded for an offer on 9 categories' worth of pages, and Kain should decide that knowingly.

**He has ruled how it gets decided:** carried to the next session, and **he wants both page types rendered for his eye.** In his words: "i need a visual of both types of page so i can make a visual decision, not just one from reading words on a screen." Nothing about it is settled by prose.
