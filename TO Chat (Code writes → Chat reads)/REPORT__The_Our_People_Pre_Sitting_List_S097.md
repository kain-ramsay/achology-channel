## The five, worst first

**1. The bio paragraphs have no space between them, at any width.** Every one carries `margin-top: 0` and `margin-bottom: 0`, so they run together as a single slab separated only by line height. On Evelyn's page that is three paragraphs reading as one. It is the thing the eye catches first and it is one declaration.

**2. The reading measure is 1,104px at desktop, about 130 characters a line.** Every other reading surface on the site sits at 880; that was measured on Our People's own closing panel this session. 130 characters is roughly double a comfortable measure, and it is most of why the bio reads as a slab. **Desktop only:** tablet comes out at 704 and 83 characters, which is fine, and the phone is fine.

**3. The one boundary on the page carries 56 above and 24 below, at every width.** The ruled standard is 48 and 48, everywhere, on every page. It is not only wrong, it is unequal, so the heading sits closer to what follows it than to what it divides from.

**4. That boundary is drawn by the help pages' class.** The line above "EVELYN'S WRITING AND ARTICLES" is `.help-group__label::after`, a 1px rule in the hairline colour, and the writing list's wrapper is `.help-articles`. **people.css already documents exactly this problem for this page family**, in its own words: the page "was wearing two other pages' clothes ... Both looked right and neither was this page's, so this page could never be corrected without changing pages nobody had asked to change." Three borrowed styles were corrected at S062. These two were not, which is why item 3 cannot be fixed without either changing the help pages or giving this page its own class. **That is the decision the sitting takes, and it is the same shape as the hairline decision Kain took earlier tonight.**

**5. The profile pages have no closing panel.** The hub carries the enquiries panel; the profiles carry nothing. A reader who lands on a pen name from a search result reaches the bottom of a short page and then the footer, with no route onward. Evelyn's page is 1,275px in total and ends on the line "Evelyn's articles and reflections will appear here as each new piece is published."
