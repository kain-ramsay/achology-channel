## Three things changed, and the direction did not

**One: the film has seventeen cuts, not twelve.** He split four beats. Beat 7, born equal, he split three ways so the equality line carries three different faces instead of one, which is a better reading of the line than the storyboard's. Beats 8, 10 and 12 he split two ways. Beat 12's split is cut on the words: the first clip runs until "We are Achology" begins, at 178.92 seconds, and the second carries the closing line.

**Two: beat 5's search terms were wrong and I replaced them.** The storyboard asked for `apprentice watching craftsman` and `adult teaching child practical task`, so the hunt returned tradespeople. Kain rejected the whole set: they "reflect tradespeople rather than people leaders, or rolemodel, like social rolemodels and mentors". He was right, and the fault was in the brief rather than in the footage. I re-searched on mentors, elders and community, and he chose from that. **The lesson is general:** a search term is a guess about what a belief looks like, written by somebody who has not seen the results, so some will be wrong. Re-running one beat is cheap, and it beats asking him to pick the least bad of six.

**Three: the film runs four seconds past the narration.** Your direction ends on the mark alone, in silence after the last line. There is under one second between his last word and the end of the recording, which is not an ending. So the film is 190.8 seconds against 187.45 of voice. That is a decision about the film's length, taken by me, and it is on the record here rather than left as an accident of arithmetic.

## What I did not do, and why

**Beat 12's ending keeps the footage.** Your direction had the picture go on "We are Achology" and the screen become the brand for the final seconds. Kain chose footage for exactly that stretch. So the film keeps his footage under the closing line and dissolves to the mark as the last word lands, which honours both.

**No third party video toolkit.** Kain asked tonight whether any of five open repositories would improve it. My answer was no: the pipeline was already built on real ffmpeg, his own Como files and his own brand mark, and using a stranger's repository means running their code on his machine. Remotion is the one worth revisiting if we ever want animated titles rather than still type.
