## The short answer I gave him

**Yes for the picture files, no for the master, and the gap between those two is the whole risk.**

## What is ready, the day he says yes

Seven rebuilt lockups, rendered from the vector master at 3x, on our own values throughout: brand orange into its hover, soft grey into brand dark, each school's primary into its secondary, and a warm bulb tuned per school. They beat the files they would replace in every respect that matters, and they can retire those PNGs as soon as he approves them.

They now live in `_proposed_S063`, beside the artwork in Website-Wide Assets, rather than in temporary space. That is a correction rather than a preference: the first build was lost when the scratchpad was wiped and had to be rebuilt from scratch. **Work Kain is going to judge does not belong anywhere that can be cleared.**

## THE DEPENDENCY, and it is why this note exists

**`Know_Your_Psychology_Graphic.ai` still holds the 2023 colours.**

Replace the PNGs and leave the master, and the next export from that file quietly reinstates the old palette with nobody able to say which set is right. **Two truths, and the wrong one holding the position of source.**

So the order is: he approves the seven, the master is recoloured in Illustrator to the same values, the shipping PNGs are exported from THAT, and only then do the 2023 files go to an archive. Recoloured pixels are the right way to judge and the wrong way to ship, and the recolour of the master is a job for whoever holds it rather than for me.

## Two smaller things for the same pass

**The filenames carry 2023 school names that no longer match ours.** `KYP_Modern Applied Mindfullness.png` has two Ls, and `KYP_Person Centred Psychology.png` drops the hyphen our canonical name uses. Whoever renames them should take the names from DSRD 7 section 2 rather than tidying them by eye.

**One shape, one size, no variants.** There is no reversed version for dark grounds and no SVG. Neither is owed; they simply do not exist, and if either is wanted it should be drawn during the recolour rather than months later off a different source.

## One thing that makes this cheaper than it looks

**These logos are used nowhere on the website.** Nothing in the theme references them and nothing resembling them is on the server, which I checked rather than assumed. So it is a brand asset job, not a site job: it can be settled and shipped without touching a live page, and it queues behind no build.

## And the entanglement to avoid

**The school colours in these renders are the LIFTED set, not the current seven.** Kain said the palette feels too muted and too alike, the measurement agreed at 0.31 average saturation against brand orange's 0.85, and these are rendered on chroma raised to 0.55 with the plum held at 0.45 on his word.

So approving the logos and approving the palette are **two different approvals**, and the logos cannot ship on the lifted colours while the site still runs the current ones without the two disagreeing in public.

**Worth putting to him that way round:** if he wants the logos sooner than the palette question resolves, render them on the CURRENT seven and ship those. It costs one run. Letting a brand asset wait on a site-wide colour decision is the more expensive of the two mistakes available here.
