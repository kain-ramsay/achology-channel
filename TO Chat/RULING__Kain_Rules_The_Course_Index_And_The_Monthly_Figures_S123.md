## Ruling 2: every course shows what it costs a month

He raised it himself and said what it is for. **His words: "many people shop on price ... I think what we need to be transparent about, especially on this page, is without giving people sentences to read ... show people that there's options to pay over three months or five months for every single course and also for every single school ... Chosen from four rendered treatments. **His words: "we go for both figures."** Both the five month and the three month figure stand on every row, always, with no control and nothing to click.

**The figures are division, not a claim.** DSRD 4 section 1.1 states instalments carry "no interest and no surcharge" and that a single course is "three or five monthly instalments", so the figure is the price divided, rounded up to the cent so it never understates a charge.

## What ruling 2 changes in DSRD 4, and it needs writing home

**DSRD 4 section 1.1 currently says no page names an instalment number**, in these words: "Because the counts differ by product, no page names a number: the one line true everywhere is 'Pay in full or spread it over monthly instalments, shown at checkout'." The pricing page's course index now names two, by Kain's ruling above.

The reason the line was written still holds for the other products and is not overturned: a school bundle is two, four or six payments and the Access All Areas Pass is three, six or twelve, so a page naming a number must name the right one for the product it is describing. **What is lifted is the blanket, not the caution.**

The closing line under the list is untouched and still section 1.1's own words.
