## What the component is

**The closing card**: one or two blocks that end a reading page and say where a
reader goes next. Each block is a bordered white box with a tinted header, a
white body, and, where it has one, a tinted footer strip.

It is built on the help answer and is ruled to reach the article, the book note
and the quote page, in the section under the body that signposts other reading.

## Its anatomy, as built

**The block.** White, 1px hairline border, 12px corners, `overflow: hidden` so
the header and footer tints clip to those corners. Outer width 944 inside the
880 reading column, which is DSRD 7 section 4.4's outdent; the block's side
padding carries its own 1px border so the contents return to exactly 880.

**The header.** A mark in a 36px tinted tile, then the heading at 20px. Tinted:
off-white for a reading block, the house orange at 0.06 for a block that offers a
person. A hairline under it.

**The body.** White. One column of rows, each a title at 16 and a one-line
description at 14, with a hover wash and no chevron.

**The footer strip, where the block has one.** The header's own surface and its
own hairline, holding the two onward controls at the two ends of the bar, in the
block's dark type with the readable orange on hover and focus.

**The split body, where the block offers two routes.** The body gives up its
padding, two halves carry it instead, and one border divides them. A half runs to
the block's own edge, so a cursor lights a true half. Stacked below 768, where the
divider turns from their shared side to their shared edge.

## The rulings it is made of, in the order he made them

Every one on a render, in one sitting: one card not three loose blocks; the
questions in one column rather than a grid that left holes; the two ways out
gathered, then given a footer strip of their own; grey for reading and warmth for
a person; a tinted header over a white body with a fine border; one mark, twice,
before both headings; no marks on the routes; the lead line deleted; and finally
the arrows gone in favour of two halves and a hairline.

**Two of those reversed instructions of his own and both are recorded at the
rules that carry them**, because each was right for the thing it was solving: the
two ways out were gathered at one end when they floated in open space and are at
two ends now they sit in a bar, and the decorative line was applied to the help
answers at S108 and removed from them at S110.
