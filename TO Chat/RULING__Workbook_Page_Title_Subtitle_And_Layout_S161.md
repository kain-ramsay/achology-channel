# RULING: the workbook page's title, subtitle and layout (theme session, S161)

**From:** Claude Code, theme session S161, 12 and 13 October 2026. **Ruled by:** Kain, in the sitting, on the rendered page in Safari (Karpman Drama Triangle record as the stand-in; nothing imported). **Session:** theme. **Board card:** Workbook page template. **Read from the vault first:** none fit.

## 1. The subtitle standard changes (Kain: "YES!")

DSRD 2 section 1.7's subtitle standard (S411) said 115 to 125 characters, two lines at most. Kain, S161: the subtitle runs to **three lines at least** and reads "a little bit too short". The standard becomes:

- **150 to 170 characters, counting spaces** (three lines at the page's measure).
- **The S411 shape kept:** Explore [the idea] to [what it does for the reader] and [what it reveals about other people or the world].
- **Two additions:** the originator's full name in the first layer (for example "Stephen Karpman's Drama Triangle"), so the person and the idea are named together for search engines and answer engines; and a short last layer naming what the workbook holds (a teaching and a written exercise on the reader's own life).
- No "workbook" in the subtitle (S411, unchanged).

The words are Chat's and the lanes', signed by Kain; Code drafts none. Every workbook's subtitle is redrafted to this before signing.

## 2. The page heading is the page title, on two lines (Kain, S161)

The heading follows DSRD 2 section 1.7's title standard as written (S411, "One title, not two"): the searched name plus its ending, which the preview had not been doing. Kain, S161: "Karpman Drama Triangle Workbook: / Free Worksheet and Exercise", the line breaking after the colon, **the ending in a lighter weight, the same as the book notes** (book-note.css .bn-hero__title-rest: 700 to 500, same size and colour, the colon kept with the name). Every title runs to two lines at least. Measured with real fonts on all twenty titles: nineteen sit on two lines at 1440 and 1024; Human Givens Emotional Needs runs to three. **The searched name stays the title** (S411: the name people search for); the originator's full name goes in the subtitle and the In short answer.
