## The one fault

The phone gutter is 20px (DSRD 7 section 4.1), so at 375 each row's box measures 383px wide and starts at minus 4: four pixels off the left edge and four off the right. The page clips it, so nothing scrolls sideways, but the rounded corners of the fill are cut and the rows are the only thing on the page wider than the screen. At 768 the same bleed leaves the fill 8px from the screen edge (gutter 32, bleed 24), which is not a fault but is tight enough to show Kain beside the phone case. Both are one declaration in `people.css` and neither was touched.

## Five things that are not faults, checked so nobody chases them

1. **The header picture at phone width sits behind the intro copy at 12 per cent.** It looks like a picture that failed to stack. It is the phone treatment `people.css` writes deliberately (the figure goes absolute and the image drops to opacity 0.12 behind the text). Designed, and worth Kain's eye once because it reads as a faint smudge behind the words, but not broken.
2. **The site header appears mid-page in the full-page captures.** A capture artefact of a sticky header, not a page fault; the S079 artefact tool's own read-me warns of the same.
3. **The cards' role line is 12px against the rows' 16px.** Section 33.8 states no size for the card's role; 12 is a step on the scale and the volume falls as the page descends by Kain's own ruling. A judgement for the sitting if he wants one, not a defect.
4. **The eldership cards now link.** The commission says the six cards link nowhere and are reviewed on the hub; since S097 the six elder pages exist and are published, and every card, row and the guest panel is one anchor to its profile. The sitting reviews them as links.
5. **Three pictures show as not loaded at 375** in the capture: two card portraits and the trial panel's picture, all lazy loading, below the fold at the moment the capture ran. All 19 load at the two other widths.
