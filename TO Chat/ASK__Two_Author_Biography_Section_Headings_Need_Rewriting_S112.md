## 1. What he said, in his own words

On the first heading: *"the words The Short Version, I can't remember actually agreeing them. I think that was just kind of a placeholder we used. But I'm wondering if you could propose something that's a bit more descriptive."*

On the fifth: *"Explore Their Work on Achology. I just fear that that's a little bit misleading, because for many psychotherapists their work actually isn't referenced on Achology. Many of them are, but some of them aren't. So we might just need something that's a little bit broader and a bit more vague, and would leave less room for misinterpretation."*

## 2. The second one is an accuracy problem, not a style one

**It states a fact about the subject that is not true of all of them.** A heading reading "Explore Their Work on Achology" on a biography of somebody whose work Achology does not teach is a claim the page cannot support, sitting in an h2 that search engines read as a section topic. Kain spotted it as a risk of misinterpretation; it is closer to a small accuracy defect, and it is the reason this ask exists rather than waiting.

**The section beneath it does not actually promise that either.** On the Rick Hanson page it points the reader at the mental wellness hub, at a book note, and at a course, which is a "where to go next" section. The heading asserts more than the body delivers.

## 3. What is actually being changed, measured this session

**These are not theme strings.** Both headings are part of the fixed five-section shape of the author biography content type and are written into each article's own body. The five, in order, from `Author_Biography_Carl_Jung_S304`:

1. The Short Version
2. {Name}: Life and Formation
3. {Name}'s Body of Work
4. {Name}'s Influence and Legacy
5. Explore Their Work on Achology

**Counted on disk this session: 51 content records carry both headings.** Every one of those that is published carries them on the live page too, in the body and in the contents card the reader navigates by.

**So the change is a content sweep, not a theme edit:** the records are rewritten, the live pages are updated, and the drafting standard that produces the next one is changed at the same time or the next batch reintroduces the old wording. The third of those is the one most easily forgotten.

## 4. What Chat is asked for

1. **Replacement wording for heading 1**, more descriptive than "The Short Version", which Kain believes was a placeholder that was never agreed.
2. **Replacement wording for heading 5**, broader, and carrying no claim that the subject's work is taught on Achology.
3. **Whether headings 2, 3 and 4 stay as they are.** They are not in question today, but four of the five share a shape and changing two of them may leave the set uneven. Worth one look while the file is open.
4. **Confirmation of which document owns this shape**, so the drafting standard is changed with the records rather than after them. The `author-biography` skill is the obvious candidate and Code does not edit it.

**Code does the sweep once the words exist**, across the records and the live pages, and it is a factory session job rather than a theme one.

## 5. What this is not

**Not urgent to the point of blocking anything.** The article template sitting is finished on every other point and nothing waits on this. But heading 5 is live on published pages now, so it is worth answering before the next batch of biographies is drafted against it.
