# RULING: Kain rules Money for the school card's saving, and gives the two small lines one width

**From Code, S127. Theme session. Shipped v0.588.0 and v0.589.0, deployed and
verified on the rendered page.**

---

## 1. What he ruled

The saving was the second of the three things named to him when he asked for an
honest read of the page: the card whispers its best argument and shouts its
second best. Four ways of stating it were built, each changing exactly one
thing, with the card as it stands as the control. He ruled:

> "Let's go with the money option. I think that makes quite a bit more sense."

So the card states its saving in money and the percentage is not drawn.

**He ruled it knowing it makes the number smaller, and that matters for the
record.** The option was built expecting the opposite, the render said
otherwise, and he was told before he chose: the first school reads 24% one way
and $353 the other, on all seven, because a school bundle saves a few hundred
rather than a few thousand. **His reason was sense, not size.** The Access All
Areas Pass runs the other way, which is a fact about that card rather than
about these seven.

## 2. And the tidying he asked for in the same breath

> "Maybe it'd be good if we had some sort of rule or container that would allow
> the save amount and the amount that's been crossed out to kind of be the same
> width ... it just looks a little bit scrappy."

He is right, and the cause is measurable rather than a matter of taste: the
struck price is 59 across and the saving is 70, both left aligned, so eleven
points of daylight hung off the end of the shorter one. Measured on all seven
cards, both figures are the same width on every one, so it was one ragged edge
repeated seven times rather than seven problems.

**No width is typed and no number is invented.** The column already sized itself
to its widest item, so the saving now spreads its two parts to the two ends of
that column and the struck price is pushed to the same right edge. The pair
reads as one block with a flush edge, and a figure changing length later needs
no rule changed.

**His own stroke does not move, and that is why the struck price is pushed
rather than stretched.** Its faint corner-to-corner line is drawn across that
element's own box, by his S125 ruling given twice, so stretching the box to fill
the column would have run the stroke out past the digits at both ends and
undone that ruling silently. Hugging the number and moving as a whole leaves the
stroke spanning exactly what it spanned before.

**One thing he may still want, named rather than assumed.** The two lines now
share one right edge, and their boxes are not the same width: the crossed-out
figure is the shorter of the two and sits at the right of the column. Stretching
it to fill the column literally is one line to change, and it is the line that
would pull his stroke past the digits, so it is his call and not Code's.

## 3. The three that lost are deleted

They are in the theme repository's history at v0.588.0. **Badge** made the
percentage a filled chip; **Line** gave the saving a line of its own across the
foot above the price; **Bold** left it where it was and took it one step up the
type scale at the price's own weight.

**Not one of the four needed a line of markup,** because the card already
renders the anchor, the money saved and the percentage and the stylesheet
decides which is drawn. An earlier sitting built it that way for exactly this.
