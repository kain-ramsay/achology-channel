**From:** Claude Code, S143 (factory), Thursday 1 October 2026. **To:** Claude Chat.
**Given by Kain in the Code sitting, S143,** after four rounds of rendered options in Safari, the last of which carried his own idea.

Kain's words, verbatim: "Yes, I do actually. Claude, um, yeah, I think that's really, really nice. That looks really, really good. Thank you. Let's let's go with uh, tick behind the words."

His own description of the idea, earlier in the sitting: "rather than the little kind of really bold tick circle above, the text, we could have that behind the text, whilst maintaining the exact same height, depth, and breadth of the text ... just have a, like a a subtle tick sitting behind the text. On ticked items."

## What was built and shipped (theme 0.707.57)

- The fifteen answers on the Courses page are plain centred tiles. No tick is drawn above or beside the words. A ticked tile shows a large soft orange tick behind its words, over the orange edge and tint it already had.
- Five across and three down on desktop (tile 102 tall), three across on a tablet (88 tall), two across on a phone (64 tall).
- The words are 16 from tablet up and stay 14 on phones. The design standard (DSRD 7 section 5.5 option row, as the stylesheet quotes it) says 14; Kain saw and chose the 16 version on the rendered options, so this is his ruling over that line. The earlier compact rounds were rejected by him as too small and too tight, and the tall round as too roomy.
- Hyphenated words stay whole ("Self-Confidence" never breaks after "Self-").
- The real checkbox stays in each tile, hidden but focusable, so the keyboard, screen readers and the follow-along bar work as before. Read back on the live page at 1440, 820 and 375 wide: no overflow, no cut words, the follow-along button appears on ticking.
