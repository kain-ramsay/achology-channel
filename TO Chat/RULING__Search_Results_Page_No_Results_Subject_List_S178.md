# RULING: the no-results page's subjects are a Subject List, with design to come (Code, S178, 22 October 2026)

For Chat: Kain's pick for the search results page's no-results page (DSRD 9 section 38.5 and 38.7).

Why it came up: Kain's even-numbers ruling (RULING__Search_Results_Page_Best_Match_And_No_Logo_Card_S178): with the Know Your Psychology logo gone and the workbooks offer ruled out of the eighth place, the seven subject cards left a gap.

Shown on the real no-results page (/?s=enneagram) in the player (theme previews/kh-search-subjects-s178), three widths, four arrangements of the unchanged shared subject card against Before: 1 Lead Wide, 2 Last Wide, 3 Centred Rows, 4 Subject List (one subject a row, name, line and counts side by side). Code recommended 4. Kain: "My answer is yes, Claude. But do you think there's anything you can do to make them less white and bland? ... It just looks really, really bland. So why don't you let's go with number four. Subject list. But would you mind applying some a little bit of design to this?"

So: the Subject List, and its design shown as four rendered options; his pick follows in this file.

*No em or en dashes in this file; checked before writing.*

Design, shown as four rendered options on the Subject List (theme previews/kh-search-subjects-s178): 1 Warm Numbers, 2 Subject Pictures, 3 Slate Rows, 4 Orange Edge. Code recommended 2. Kain: "That looks really scrappy. I actually prefer warm numbers, option number one. However, on the page, you haven't used spacing particularly well here. ... It's like you've just dropped the round circle button icons on top. You haven't designed this well. So yeah, let's go for option number one, warm numbers. And now design this really well."

So the no-results subjects are a Warm Numbers list: warm cream rows, a large pale orange number for each subject, the name with its line beneath, the counts, an orange arrow disc, every gap on the spacing scale. It is the search page's own list (its own markup), so the shared subject card is left untouched for the rest of the family.
