## What changed

The header at the top of the hero's right column read "More Wise Quotes", which
was a placeholder Code held identical across the four designs so that the only
thing being judged was the design. It now carries his words.

**He typed it in capitals and the markup does not.** The label is written
`More Quotes` and the stylesheet puts it into capitals, so a reader sees exactly
what he asked for. That is the theme's own standing correction, already on the
theme queue for the featured card's overline: capitals typed into markup are read
out letter by letter by some screen readers, and this project's acronym check
reads them as unexpanded abbreviations. A styled label is styled, not shouted.

This is a mechanical application of a rule that already exists, not a wording
choice: the words are his, character for character, and only their storage
differs from their appearance.
