# RULING: the book note's side column puts navigation first, and the cover becomes a thumbnail

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** ruling, filed by Claude Code, Session 114, theme session. **Date:** Monday 14 September 2026.
**Filed under Harness Rule 14.**
**Shipped:** Theme 0.401.0, deployed and read back on both page types.
**Board card:** Book note page template.

---

## 1. Kain's words

> "the sidebar and the book notes, it doesn't work. The table of contents is the second half at the bottom of the side panel, and the book and the button consume the entire top half. This just isn't ... doesn't make any sense."

He also said, in the same message, that he would like Code to be more proactive and that he was having to do all the directing. **That is a fair reading of this sitting and it is recorded here rather than answered in the chat.** Almost every improvement to that column today started with him naming a symptom. The one piece of work that did not, the whole-column separation standard, only happened because he stopped the sitting and told Code to look at the thing as a whole.

## 2. What it measured, before anything was touched

| | |
|---|---|
| Panel height (the screen) | 880 |
| Contents list began at | **611, which is 69 percent down** |
| Cover | 384, or 44 percent of the whole column |

The cover is the same picture the hero showed at the same size a moment earlier. So two thirds of the column was spent before the reader reached the only thing in it that belongs to the page they are on.

**The hairline shipped at 0.399.0 earlier in this same sitting made it about 70 worse.** That separation was correct and it was applied to a stack that was already in the wrong order, which is what happens when a standard is applied without asking whether the thing it is ordering is right.

## 3. What now holds, on the book note only

The contents goes to the top. The cover shrinks to a 72 wide thumbnail beside the Amazon button, the two forming one short block under the hairline, which turns over with the order so the same line now sits beneath the contents with the ruled 48 on either side.

**Read back on the live page: the contents list now begins at 56, which is 6 percent down.** It was 69.

**The thumbnail still does the cover's real job.** It opens as the hero leaves the screen so a reader always knows which book they are in, and it does that for a fifth of the height.

**The article page is untouched and its selectors say so.** Its picture is a square crop rather than a 2:3 jacket, it carries no buy button beside it, and its contents list is shorter, so none of the arithmetic applies. Measured after the change: its contents still begins at 12 percent down, exactly as before.

## 4. One cost, named rather than hidden

**The button's label now wraps to two lines.** The column is 256 wide, the thumbnail and its gap take 88, and "View the Book on Amazon" does not fit the 168 that remain on one line at the button's own type size. It is legible and centred, and no padding change makes it fit; only a shorter label or a smaller thumbnail would. Left as it is because the label is Kain's, ruled twice today, and the wrap is a smaller cost than either alternative. **His to overturn on the rendered page.**

OWED BACK: nothing. Section 4 is a named cost rather than a question.
