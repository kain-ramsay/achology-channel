**From:** Claude Code, session S123, Thursday 17 September 2026. **To:** Claude Chat.

Given by Kain in the sitting, on the rendered page at `achologytest.com/previews/pricing.html`. Built and deployed at theme v0.508.0.

## The ruling

**His words, S123:** "I'm just looking at the hairlines on this page ... I've just noticed that the hairline extends to the edge of the block. Could I ask that you just shorten these hairlines to match and to align with the text container."

**So the separator between two blocks now starts and ends where the blocks' words start and end.** Measured after the change, at all four widths: every separator on the page begins on the same vertical line as every heading, 216 at 1440, 96 at 1024, 80 at 768 and 44 at 390.

## What it reverses, which is why this file exists

**DSRD 7 section 4.4a carries the opposite in its own words:** "The container edge belongs to panels and to the separators between blocks, and to nothing else."

That sentence is Kain's own ruling from S121. He was shown four rendered answers to the two-text-edges fault on this page, one of which was **the separators inset to the text line**, and he chose the one that keeps the container edge. The three that lost were deleted at theme v0.459.0 and the option he has now asked for is the one he rejected then.

**He is entitled to change it and this is the record of him doing so.** It is filed rather than applied quietly because a page that disagrees with a written standard is the drift the harnesses exist to catch, and because the next person reading section 4.4a would otherwise find a rule the site no longer keeps.

## What is not changed

**DSRD 7 section 4.3 is untouched.** It governs a separator's presence and its spacing, 48 above and 48 below and 32 on a phone, and says nothing at all about its width. That silence is what made the width his to rule rather than a departure from anything.

The rest of section 4.4a stands: every block's words still begin on one vertical line, and the container edge still belongs to panels.

## One thing for Chat to decide rather than Code

**Section 4.4a is a site-wide rule and this is one page.** Whether the inset separator travels to every other built page is a sweep, and a sweep needs its own signed brief naming its pages (Harness Rule 3). Until that happens the pricing page and the rest of the site will differ on this one line, which is named here so it is a known state rather than a discovery.
