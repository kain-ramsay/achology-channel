**From:** Claude Code, theme session S178, 21 October 2026. **Ruled by:** Kain, on the rendered page in Safari (the real template in a local Playground on the Karpman record, test content only; no workbook is published on the build site). **Board card:** Workbook page template. **Branch:** theme/workbook-s178, not yet deployed.

## Kain's rulings in this sitting, in his words, in the order made

1. **Tablet band** (Kain: "I don't know why you wouldn't use a similar heading structure as what you've used on computer in tablet ... that's a terrible use of space"): on a tablet the title and subtitle on the left, the book on its shelf on the right from the breadcrumb's foot to the subtitle's foot, the email box and privacy line the band's full width under both. The phone keeps its stack.
2. **The close shows both paths** (Kain: "Ready for your learning. That's not finished. There's only one block there. There should be two"): the course path is the workbook's own source course.
3. **The right-hand column must not run over or under the full-width parts** (Kain: "The right-hand side column that should stick goes over and under other components in the page as it scrolls"): it is the S161 round ten column, the cover with the email box under it, held at the screen's foot and stopping at the column's end. Browse More and the close then run the full 984.
4. **No privacy line under the right-hand column's button** (Kain: "I want it removed. No arguments, just take it out ... Visually, it adds nothing. Users don't care"). The band and the dark email card keep theirs.
5. **The In short note and the Used For notes** (Kain: "Is there some way you could dress them really subtly? Just to make them look a bit less bland?"): four dressings shown, tabbed (Ruled Paper, Washi Tape, Folded Corner, Sticky Note).
6. **Kain chose Ruled Paper** ("I actually really like ruled paper"), with two changes on all five notes: **the pin sits on the paper**, not on its top edge ("Any real note would have the pin on the actual bit of paper. So the pin needs to be inside of the actual bit of paper"); and **each Used For note reads as one sentence after its number**, "1." then the text on the same line, to cut the white space ("keep the number one, two, three, and four, and then put a dot ... and then put the text following on from that"). The In short note's words are unchanged ("the top card is just spot on").
