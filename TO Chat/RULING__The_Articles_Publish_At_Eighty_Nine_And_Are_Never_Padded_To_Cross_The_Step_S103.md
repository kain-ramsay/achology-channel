## 2. What he was told before he ruled, so the record shows it was a decision and not a shrug

**That the score is a plugin's own opinion.** Google never reads it and no reader sees it. It is a discipline we set ourselves to stop thin pages going out, and here it has done its job: it reported that these articles are sound.

**That the writing would be padding.** These articles are already inside the length band their own records gate them to, at the lower end of it. The only thing at 2,000 words is where Rank Math steps its grading. Asked to add 400 words to a finished article, the honest outcome is padding, which makes the number better and the article worse.
