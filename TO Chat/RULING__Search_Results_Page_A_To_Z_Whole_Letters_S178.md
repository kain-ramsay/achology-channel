# RULING: the search results page's A to Z is Whole Letters (Code, S178, 22 October 2026)

For Chat: Kain's pick for the A to Z on the search results page, for the record in DSRD 9 section 38.7.

Why it came up: walking the page before sign-off, Kain saw "some real voids". Code marked them on pictures of the page (theme previews/kh-search-s178/voids); the largest was the A to Z, whose first column (A to D) ran far longer than the other four, leaving empty patches under them on every search.

Shown on the real page in the player (theme previews/kh-search-az-s178), four layouts plus Before: 1 Even Columns (a letter may carry on into the next column), 2 Six a Letter, 3 Letter Rows, 4 Pill Wall. Kain liked Even Columns ("I really like how the full container space is now used") but asked for no carryover: "so that there's just like the whole top of it is all letters at the top". Code added 5 Whole Letters to his words. Kain's pick, in his words: "I would be inclined to say five. I think five is nice, to be honest with you."

What Whole Letters is: no letter carried over; every column starts on a letter; four columns on a computer, three on a tablet, two on a phone; the letters split where the columns end most level (worked out again by the page whenever the word list grows). Without the page's script it falls back to the columns as built at S178.

Also asked by Kain in the same turn: "a little bit more space between the word and the number". Shown on the page at four sizes against Before (one ordinary space): 1 a touch more, 2 a little more (one step of the spacing scale, 8), 3 more (12), 4 the most (16). Code recommended 2; Kain, asked "Shall I build in 2?": "Go ahead, building too, Claude. Thank you." Whole Letters shipped as theme 0.707.445; the space ships next.
