## What was put to him

Having ruled the artwork card, Kain named two things wrong inside it. First, that a row of course names does not read as courses: "it's not clear that the five courses are actually courses." Second, the three facts under the school name: "the five courses, number of hours, twelve month membership included, that on one line, you know, stretched over onto two."

Five treatments were built into the theme and rendered on the whole page, one on screen at a time, every one checked at 1440, 1024, 768 and 390 before he saw any of them. Not one word, figure or price differed between them. Four changed how a course row says it is a course: a tick, a graduation cap, a counted numeral, or the courses in a quiet panel of their own. The fifth made each course a ruled row the width of the card.

Code recommended the graduation cap. Kain ruled otherwise, for a reason Code had not weighed: consistency with the block above rather than literal meaning in the row.

## His ruling, in his words

> "I am thinking that we go with Rows on this one ... let's lock in Rows for now. Um, these hairline rows, kinda... I think this allows us to follow on with the general kind of rule with the page, because we've got rows separating the courses in the panel above. So I think this is appropriate."

**One word of his was heard through dictation and is recorded honestly:** he said "Rose" throughout, and the treatment is named Rows. His own reasoning names it beyond doubt, since he describes hairline rows separating the courses in the panel above, which no other treatment does. Code acted on Rows and told him so plainly in the same turn so a mishearing could be corrected in one word.

## The second thing, which was not a choice

The three facts were fixed the same way in all five, so his ruling settles it too. They have come out of the card's head, where they read in the two thirds of the card the artwork leaves and broke onto a second line, and now sit under the head on the card's full width, on one line. Each fact is its own span and the middots between them are drawn by the stylesheet rather than typed inside the words, so the sentence is exactly the sentence already approved. **No copy changed.**

## What was done with it in the same session

- The ruled rows stopped being a treatment and became the card. The four that lost were deleted rather than parked; they are in the theme repository's history at v0.513.1, and the tick and graduation cap markup they needed came out with them, so no card carries an element nothing draws.
- Shipped and deployed as theme **v0.514.0**. Local, the server and the zip were each measured and agree.
- The approved state was re-exported as the pricing page's prototype under Rule 14's fold-back.

## One standards note, so nobody corrects it later

These hairlines sit inside a component and keep the component's own values rather than DSRD 7 section 4.3's 48 above and 48 below. That section's own last paragraph says so: "it does not govern rules inside a DSRD 8 component (a card footer, a card's stats row ...), which keep their own specified values."
