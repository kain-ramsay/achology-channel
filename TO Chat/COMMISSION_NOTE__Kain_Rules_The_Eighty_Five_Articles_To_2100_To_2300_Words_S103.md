The records gate these at 1,600 to 2,400 with a stated target of about 2,000. **His band sits inside that with roughly a hundred words of margin at each end**, which matters in both directions: above 2,000 so a later trim does not drop an article back under Rank Math's length step, and below 2,400 so it does not breach its own ceiling.

## 4. The caution that must be in the brief

**Adding words thins the keyword out, and these are already close to the floor.** Measured across all 161 record gate lines: average density 1.27 per cent, lowest 1.01, against a band of 1.0 to 1.5. **Twenty six records are already under 1.10 per cent.**

An article going from 1,766 words to 2,200 without gaining a single keyword use falls by a fifth. At 1.27 per cent that lands on 1.02, scraping the floor; anything starting near 1.01 falls straight out of the band, and Rank Math scores lower for it as well as the gate failing it.

**So the instruction is not "add words". It is "add words and work the phrase in as you go", then re-run the content gate on the record.** Roughly one or two more natural uses of the focus phrase per article covers it. The gate already measures this, so it will catch anyone who forgets, but only if it is re-run after the lengthening rather than before.
