## The standard now

**One component on all four, `.ach-listen-bar`, under the page title and above
the writing, with its rule under it.** The facts run in one order: date, writer,
words, reading time. The listen control and the narrator close the line at the
far end. **A page with no writer or no recording draws neither**, the way a
missing cover draws nothing rather than a fallback.
