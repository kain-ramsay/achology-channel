## Decision 3. The space between two paragraphs

**Shown on:** the book note, `/learn/helping-people/book-notes/the-skilled-helper/`, at his ruled body and column.

**What was in front of him.** The article page and the book note page leave different amounts of air between paragraphs, both approved by him this week. Three options: the book note's own gap, the article's tighter gap, and a more open one.

**His first answer was the book note's own gap.** He was told before it was written down that the gap was not on the site's short list of spacing steps, and that keeping it was fine but would be recorded as a deliberate exception so nobody tidied it away later.

**His ruling, in his words:** *"OK, lets stay with the 'A little tighter' option"*.

**So the paragraph gap is 16px, site-wide, and the book note comes onto it.** 16 is a step on the spacing scale (DSRD 7 section 4), so **no exception is created and nothing needs a carve-out.** The article page was already right and the book note was the page out of step.

**Worth recording, because it is the second time this sitting the same shape appeared:** two pages he approved in the same week answered one question two ways, and neither was wrong when it was built. That is what this sitting is for, and it is the argument for the gate check that refuses an off-scale spacing value.

**For Chat to write:** DSRD 7 section 4, or DSRD 9 section 22 where the block standard lives, whichever owns the paragraph gap: the gap between two body paragraphs is 16px.
