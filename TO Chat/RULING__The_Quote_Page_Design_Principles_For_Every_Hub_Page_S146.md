# RULING: the Quote page design principles, for every Knowledge Hub page (S146, theme session)

Kain, closing the Quote page sitting: "the quote page is finished ... we've simplified this ... a very, very, very neat page. I think there are some principles and standards that you can document from this ... transfer them over now into the book notes page ... look at all the decisions that we've made ... what is transferable." Every principle below comes from a ruling he gave in the S146 sitting; the detail and measurements are in RULING__The_Quote_Page_Sitting_S146 and RULING__The_Contents_Rail_S146.

## A. General rules (any page)

1. **A picture never breaches its container.** It stays inside its band or box and inside that box's own padding; no bleed, overlap or break-out. Kain: "the image itself has to stay within the container ... that's just not good design practice."
2. **The quote page pictures are meant to be blurry.** Never flag their softness as a fault.
3. **One way to do one thing.** Two blocks offering the same action side by side conflict; keep one. Kain removed Related Further Reading from the quote page because Explore More Quote Articles sits beside it: "we're giving people two opportunities to do the same thing which doesn't make any sense."
4. **One heading per block.** A link set in the heading's own style directly under the heading reads as a second heading; a secondary line takes the description line's setting.
5. **One hover rule for links in a list: colour only.** Underlines belong to links inside running text (the S356 in-body link standard, still to be ruled in Safari).
6. **Lines run across, they are not stretched.** Where a hairline continues into another column, the continuation is drawn on its own; a hairline that is the edge of a box is never widened, because the box's contents widen with it (the course cards widened at 0.707.79 and were put right at 0.707.81).
7. **Things that sit side by side share lines.** A side block ends where something in the main column ends (a row, a hairline), never at an invisible edge; where two columns hold rows, the rows share their lines or the side has no lines at all.
8. **Air, not boxes.** In a narrow column the space between links separates them; a rule under every link turns the space into empty boxes (Kain's S112 rule for the side list, restated S146).
9. **Every space and hairline on the 48 rhythm** (existing rule), and every boundary measured after every change, on the whole page, not only the part changed.

## B. The side panel (shared by articles, book notes and quote pages)

10. **The table of contents travels the whole article** and stays with the reader to the end; the sticky part comes to rest with its last item 48 above the first hairline it meets that closes the reading, measured from its last item, never its screen-high box.
11. **Every contents highlight is the same width**, the section names still folding by the S081 rule.
12. **The column's vertical edge carries on down** to the hairline the panel rests on.
13. **The side panel's square picture shares the cover column**, so the hero picture and the contents card share one right edge.

## C. The quote page only

14. Hero picture: a square on the cover column, from just above the label to the date line, three Achology bubbles behind it (large, medium, small), desktop only.
15. A short mid-grey hairline between Download, Share and Copy.
16. No Related Further Reading; the panel floats to the hairline closing Explore More Quote Articles, and that hairline runs on across the right column.
