## His ruling, in his words

> "On the Courses block and the schools block, please change All Pricing options to Pricing Options."

Given on the rendered page in this sitting. Copy is his, so it was done rather than put back to him as options.

## What changed, and what deliberately did not

The resting entry on the payment plan menu now reads **Pricing Options** in place of **All Payment Options**. One change covered both blocks he named, because the course index's menu and the seven school cards' menus are drawn by one renderer; all eight now carry the same words, and the hidden label a screen reader announces changed with them.

**His S124 rule for that slot is untouched and still holds.** The resting entry carries no value, so the page at rest shows the full price whether a reader has touched the menu or not, which is the half of his S124 instruction that is easy to lose in a rename. The four plans behind it are unchanged: One Time Fee, Pay in Two, Pay in Four, Pay in Six on the cards, and One Time Fee, Pay in Three, Pay in Five on the course index, per DSRD 4 section 1.1.

His S124 words are left in the source exactly as he said them, because they are the record of that sitting rather than the copy on the page.

## The sitting this came out of, so the rename is not read on its own

At the S124 close Kain rejected four rearrangements of the same three objects in the school card's buying block, in his words "all four of those are terrible ... real poor quality and effortless designs", and set the test for this sitting: each option must be a different kind of object rather than a different arrangement, every card is judged on its own rather than against its neighbour, and if the count of objects has not gone down it is not a simplification.

Four were built to that test and shipped as **v0.526.0**, one on screen at a time, not one word, figure, link or price different between them, every one checked at 1440, 1024, 768 and 390 before he saw any of them. Each folds objects into something already on the card and they differ in which one survives: **Row**, where the course list survives and the money and the action become two more ruled rows in its rhythm, so four objects become none; **Floor**, where the card survives and ends in a full width doorway in the school's own text-safe colour; **Seal**, where the price survives and leaves the block for the school's artwork; and **Ticket**, where one bordered stub survives with the action as its floor. Code recommended Row. **Not yet ruled.**

Three conformance corrections were made silently in the same pass, none of them a design choice: the bundle price takes DSRD 7 section 3's own register row for it, quoted in the stylesheet, and two empty rules that drew nothing were deleted.
