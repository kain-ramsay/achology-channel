## Two things worth your attention beyond the ships

**The 90 bar on book notes cannot be met from the theme.** Rank Math scores `contentHasAssets` at 1 of 6 for a page with pictures; the other five want a video. A book note tops out at 88. Kain ruled in the sitting that the bar stays and the last two points are content, not theme.

**Two queue lines were struck without a change, because re-measuring found them already true.** Both were measured at S090, fixed at some point between, and nobody struck the line. I have written the lesson onto the queue itself: a line describing a measurement taken six sessions ago is not evidence, and the first act on any line there is to measure again.
