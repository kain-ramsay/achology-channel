# RULING: Payment Options replaces the budget box, and the price grid goes

**DOCUMENT TYPE: ruling record, not a page spec.** It records rulings Kain gave in session and specifies no page.

**From:** Claude Code, S124, Sunday 21 September 2026. **To:** Claude Chat.
**Board card:** Pricing page (PRD Pr1.19).
**OWED BACK:** three things at the foot of this file, two of which only Kain can close.

## What he ruled, in his own words

> "The dropdown box that currently says Spread the Cost, why don't we just simply make that Payment Options? And then the dropdown options are One Time Fee, Pay in Three, Pay in Five. So the figure that defaults is the One Time Fee, that's the full price ... then we could just lose the line underneath that completely."

It is his own idea, and it came after four rendered alternatives of Code's were put to him and found wanting.

## What it replaced, and why his answer beat all four of mine

The 28-row course index published three money columns on every row, 84 figures in all. He called it confusing and asked for a toggle. Code built the toggle four ways and he ruled the lot irrelevant, in these words:

> "You haven't injected any layer of design innovation into this whatsoever ... you just put in place small buttons. So in this block now there are six buttons. Just buttons. Is buttons really the only solution you were able to come up with?"

He was right, and the diagnosis is worse than the symptom. Code had put the control in the filter bar, where everything narrows the list: three category tabs and a budget menu. This control narrows nothing; it changes the unit the price column is written in. It was a category error, which is why it read as a sixth button. Four placements of one pill was one idea shown four times.

Four further alternatives were then built as four genuinely different kinds of object, and his own idea still beat them, for a reason worth recording: **every one of mine added an object to the page and his added none.** The bar keeps three category tabs and one field, exactly what it had before any of this began, and the page lost a heading line as well.

## What the page lost, ruled knowingly rather than slipped past him

**The monthly budget filter from S123 is deleted**, with its empty state and its reset button. He was told plainly, before he ruled, that this was a deletion rather than a rename, and he ruled it anyway on the ground that a reader can now read the monthly figure for every course directly. **DSRD 9's pricing page record and any note of the S123 control bar need this change**, because that box was his own ask in that sitting.

**The heading line over the price column is deleted**, on his words above. With the menu naming the plan, a heading said the same thing twice.

**The two faint dividers become one.** This is not a reversal of his S123 ruling: he asked for a line between the money columns and there were three of them then. The line itself, its colour, its height and its position in the middle of the gap, is unchanged.

## Three things settled before it was built, none of them his to have to ask for

It opens on the One Time Fee and never on an instalment, because opening on the smallest monthly figure is the framing DSRD 4 and this site's own help section say Achology refuses.

It carries no percentage badge, unlike the reference image he sent. DSRD 4 section 1.1, read this session and quoted: instalments run "with no interest and no surcharge". Paying in five costs exactly what paying in full costs, so a percentage would advertise a saving that does not exist.

Every price is still in the delivered markup on every row. The menu only says which is drawn, so a reader with no JavaScript gets all three rather than a third of the catalogue's money.

## A collision of Code's own, cleared and recorded because it cost him two rounds

Four school card money treatments built earlier in the same sitting used the same four option names as the price toggle built straight after them, so every option applied two unrelated experiments at once. The preview's own option bar still carried the money row's labels, so the words above the page named neither of the things on it. **Kain judged a polluted page for two rounds and said so.** The money treatments were deleted unruled (history, v0.519.0: Spread, Button up, One block, Row closed), the school card's money row went back to the state he approved at v0.517.0, and the labels were corrected. The lesson is narrow and worth keeping: two sets of options may never share an option namespace.

## Shipped

Theme **v0.521.0**, deployed, with local, the server and the zip each measured and agreeing. `css_gate`: pricing.css PASS. Checked at 1440, 1024, 768 and 390, driving the menu rather than reading the markup: the three options in his words and order, opening on the One Time Fee, each choice changing every figure in the list, one figure a row, the category tabs still filtering, and no sideways scroll. Every rule, line and comment belonging to the things that went was deleted with them. The approved state was re-exported as the page's prototype.

2. **The school cards' own three lines** read "over 6 months", "over 4 months" and "in full". Whether they become Pay in Six, Pay in Four and One Time Fee to match the table was put to him once and is not yet answered.
3. **Two unnamed values on the school card** raised earlier in this sitting and still open: the card's 3px school-coloured top border, and the percentages holding the artwork and the words apart.
