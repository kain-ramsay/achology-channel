# Six Mental Health search descriptions, second draft (S420)

Each line now says what the reader will find in the article. Nothing is changed in the records yet.


## 1. Can Mental Health Affect Your Body, Sleep and Energy?

**New (143):** How does mental health affect sleep? What research finds about the link, and three questions to test the thought that keeps you awake at night.


## 2. How Do You Improve Your Mental Wellbeing, Day to Day?

**New (141):** How to improve mental wellbeing beyond a list of habits: catch the next feeling, name it, check its thought and choose one small thing to do.


## 3. Is Mental Health Genetic, or Is It Shaped by How You Live?

**New (138):** Is mental health genetic? What the NIMH and WHO say about genes and family history, and a simple way to check a worry you have always had.


## 4. Can Mental Health Improve Without Therapy or Medication?

**New (135):** Mental health without therapy or medication: what the WHO says, what Achology adds, and a one-sentence exercise for a worry that loops.


## 5. Should Mental Health Be Taught in Schools, and How?

**New (148):** Should mental health be taught in schools? Three large reviews found gains, one trial of mindfulness lessons did not. Plus what a lesson could hold.


## 6. Why Does Mental Health Matter So Much in Everyday Life?

**New (138):** Why mental health is important even when nothing is wrong: what the WHO means by it, and a test for the next message that makes you tense.
