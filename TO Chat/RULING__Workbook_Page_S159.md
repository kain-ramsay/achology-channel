## 5. The rules that make every workbook link to its own course (Kain: "make sure ... all the rules documented that will allow every resource to link through to its appropriate course")

1. **One source for the course.** Each workbook names its course once, in its own `associated_course` field (an existing field on the workbook post). The thank-you page reads that field and nothing else for: the course name in the "One Part of a Larger Learning Journey" line, the course name on the first path card, and the first card's button, which goes to that course's own page (the theme's course address helper, DSRD 1 section 2.3). The course name and the address can never disagree because they come from one record.
2. **No guessing.** Where a workbook's `associated_course` is empty, the page leaves the course sentence and the course path out rather than filling in a likely course. Today the Ladder of Inference workbook's field is empty (read from the install, 12 October 2026); it must be set to the Diploma Course in Modern Applied Psychology (DiMAP) before the page is used.
3. **The workbook theme.** "{workbook theme}" in the first course point is the workbook's idea in plain words (for the Ladder: "the Ladder of Inference"), read from the workbook record's searched name, never typed per page.
4. **The address.** One page serves every workbook at `/learn/workbooks/thank-you/{workbook-slug}/`; the Kit form for each workbook redirects there with its own slug. The page reads the cover (the workbook's featured image), the course and the theme from that workbook. A bare `/learn/workbooks/thank-you/` shows the page without a cover or course line. Noindex, follow (DSRD 1).
5. **Fixed for every workbook:** the community card and its button (to the pricing page's membership plans), the banners, the steps, the close.
6. **The gate (owed to the factory session):** a check that fails a workbook whose `associated_course` is empty, or names a different course from the workbook page's "Where This Workbook Comes From" section.
