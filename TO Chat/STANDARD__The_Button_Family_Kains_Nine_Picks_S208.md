# STANDARD: the button family, Kain's nine picks (buttons design side session, S208, 2 November 2026)

**Why.** In the course page hero sitting Kain saw buttons and tabs drawn many different ways and asked for one universal standard. ## The nine picks

1. **Direction for the main button:** a shortlist of four (Ember, Arrow Disc, Chamber, Ember Edge), then **Ember Disc** (pick 2): a lit, glossy pill; the arrow rides in a white 32 disc turned 45 degrees that turns to point the way on hover; it lifts 2 and a sheen crosses it; pressed, it sinks. 2. **Colour: Glowing Ember** (pick 3). Brand orange light #F3843D at the top, the action orange #C85015 held flat from 34 to 66 per cent behind the words (white words keep 4.55), deep ember #A9400E at the foot, a 1.5 brand orange rim, a warm glow. Kain's reason: the logo's orange (sampled #ED6922) had been undermined on the site.
3. **Second choice button: Fire Rim, stripped to Frame and Disc** (picks 4 and 5). White pill, 46, a 2px fire gradient frame (#FFC08F, #ED6922, #A6380B), brand dark words, the arrow in a plain action orange disc. On hover it lifts and the disc turns; nothing else moves. On a dark band the body is the brand dark (Ember Edge). Kain stripped it because "four or five things happening in every button" was too much.
4. **Text and arrow links: Mini Disc** (pick 6). Brand dark Como 14/600 words, the arrow in a 24 action orange disc that turns on hover; replaces today's four text link colours.
5. **Tabs: Fire Slide** (pick 7). One hairline pill track; the chosen tab is marked by a white pill in the fire frame that slides to it. Never wraps; slides sideways on a phone.
6. **Pills and chips: Fire Ring** (pick 8). Hairline pills 36 tall; the chosen pill wears the fire frame, exactly as the chosen tab.
7. **Icon buttons: Ring to Fire** (pick 9). Hairline 40 circles (play 64), brand dark icon; on hover or keyboard the fire frame appears and the icon turns orange.

**The one rule across the family:** the orange disc points the way; the fire frame marks what is chosen or reached for.

8. **Links in the writing: Faint Line that Firms** (pick 12). Every link inside a sentence on every page: plain AA orange words (#B8460F, #FFC08F on dark), a 1px underline in the same orange at 25 per cent that turns solid when pointed at or focused. No wash, no motion. Kain chose it plain on purpose ("I don't want this to be a feature"); it **replaces the link hover wash** of DSRD 8 section 43 for links in the writing.

9. **Navigation links: Line that Firms** (pick 13). The header menu, breadcrumbs, article contents and footer columns keep their sizes and colours; pointed at or focused, a 1px orange line appears under the words, and the page you are on keeps it. With pick 12, every link on the site answers one way.

10. **The one exception: school colours** (pick 14; Kain: "Please don't contradict this one part"). Every line, link or control drawn in a school's own colour today keeps that school's colour. The family's shape and behaviour still apply there, but its orange becomes that school's colour on that school's parts. Nothing in the rollout may override a school colour with the family's orange.
