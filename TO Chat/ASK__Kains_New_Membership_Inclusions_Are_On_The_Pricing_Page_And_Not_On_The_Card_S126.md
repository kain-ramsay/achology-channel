# ASK: Kain's new membership inclusions are on the pricing page and not on the membership card

## What happened

Kain gave new wording for what Achology membership includes, in session, on the
rendered pricing page. Eight lines where the old list had seven, and every one
of the seven reworded. His words, typed as he gave them:

> See What Your Membership Includes:
>
> Access seven Achology courses, available immediately on-demand
> Participate in unlimited LIVE online personal development sessions
> Join weekly and monthly mentorship groups led by expert mentors
> Apply for FREE one-to-one coaching with senior Achology members
> Join weekly discussion groups for collaboration and accountability
> Build valuable skills through focused training events and workshops
> Global networking with other community members and practitioners
> Opportunity to partake in Achology in-house accreditation process

## Why this is an ASK and not just a report

**The seven lines he replaced are shared.** They live in one function that the
pricing page and the membership commerce card both read. So his new wording
could not be put in without either changing a component nobody asked to change,
or leaving two lists on the site saying different things about one product.

**Code took the second, deliberately, and it is the reversible one.** The
membership card's content is owned by DSRD 8 sections 9 and 10, and its copy is
settled one row at a time across all five commerce cards by the signed S279
brief and its addendum. Rewriting that from a pricing page sitting would have
put the code ahead of the document that governs it, which The Shared Rules
section 4 forbids: prototype, then sheet, then code, and the lower one is
corrected.

So the pricing page has its own list, named as temporary in the code beside it,
and the difference travels here.

## What is owed back

1. **Do the membership commerce cards take the same eight lines?** If yes, it
   is one small change in one function and Code makes it on your word; the
   pricing page's own copy is then deleted and both read one list again.
2. **DSRD 8 sections 9 and 10 need Kain's wording written in**, whichever way
   question 1 goes, because the document and the page disagree today.
3. **One thing for Kain rather than for Chat, if it matters commercially:** the
   old line said "Unlock seven complete courses" and his says "Access seven
   Achology courses". The count of seven is unchanged and is DSRD 4 section 13's
   figure, so nothing about what is sold has moved. Flagged only so nobody
   reads the rewording as a change to the product.

**The cost of leaving it:** two lists about one product, on one site, for as
long as it stands. It is small and it is named in three places, but it is real.
