# RULING: four options minimum before any design change Code proposes

**From:** Claude Code, S121, Tuesday 16 September 2026. **To:** Claude Chat.
**Filed under Harness Rule 14:** Kain's ruling in a Code session is authority at
once, and this file is the record so Chat writes it into the document that owns
it. **It is not a pricing page rule. It governs every design change on the site.**

**DOCUMENT TYPE: not a page spec.** It files a standing rule and specifies no page.

## Kain's words, whole

> "From here on in, there's a strict rule. You will never on your own merit spend
> time changing a prototype or a design without presenting me with a minimum of
> four different options I can choose from. Because when you screw up, you waste
> my time, and you also waste my tokens. The tokens I have to pay for, that
> Anthropic will not return me or give me a return on."

And immediately before it, in the same sitting, after a change he rejected:

> "From here on in we're gonna have to insist that you explain exactly what
> you're about to do before you do it. Because when you are left to just do
> whatever you want, you waste time, and you also waste my tokens."

## What it binds Code to, as Code has read it

1. **A design change Code proposes is never made first.** It is presented as at
   least four options, rendered, and Kain picks one.
2. **Before anything is touched, Code says exactly what it intends to change**,
   in plain words, and waits.
3. **It does not touch work Kain has directed.** A change he asks for, or copy he
   hands over, is executed, not put back to him as four options. The rule
   governs Code's own initiative, which is what "on your own merit" names.
4. The four are rendered whole pages, one on screen at a time, which is the
   render standard already in force.

## What produced it

Code changed the pricing page's opening band without asking: the artwork was
taken out of its column and bled past the card's edges. Kain, on the rendered
page: "Revert that ... the image is pushing itself right out of the card. It's
ridiculous." It was reverted in the same sitting at theme v0.453.0, by checkout
rather than by hand, so nothing else in the file moved.

**The cost is the point, and it is his, not Code's.** A rejected change spends his
minutes and his tokens, and neither comes back. Four options spend Code's effort
before his, which is the right way round.
