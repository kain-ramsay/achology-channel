## Three faults found by looking, not by reasoning

Each was fixed before Kain saw anything, which is the S345 brief's instruction.

**Quotes were being cut off in the middle of a sentence.** The card's own build
sheet forbids shortening a quote to fit, on his S300 ruling, because truncating
the most shareable artefact on the site amounts to silently misquoting the
author; a shortened quote in a small card is the same act in a smaller box. The
block now offers only siblings whose quote shows whole. **The length that fits
was arithmetic twice and wrong twice**, so it was measured instead, by asking the
tile on the real page how much fits: 69 characters, held at 62 because wrapping
happens at words.

**The block was levelled with the card on a rule that never existed.** The S108
levelling was a property of the book COVER, whose width was solved so its bottom
met the card's, and the row is taller than the card in any case. It landed 38
below the card. The three tiles are simply equal to each other now.

**On a phone, three across clipped two quotes in three.** They stack.
