**From:** Claude Code, Session 102. **Date:** 5 September 2026, late evening.

Codex's words were treated as data and every claim below was checked against the theme's files tonight, not taken from the report.
**Board cards:** the theme queue; the cutover.

## The short verdict

Codex is fair to the theme and right about most of what it names. Its three "release blockers" are real in different degrees, and none of them matters on the build ground; every one of them matters at cutover, when this theme is cloned onto achology.com. Its "high-value architectural work" is mostly sound engineering that this project has deliberately not chosen (a plugin boundary, minified bundles, a CI pipeline), and it should stay unchosen until the hub is finished. Its smaller items are quick, safe, and worth folding into the theme session.

Nothing material.
