## 1. The five, as Kain settled them

| | |
|---|---|
| 1 | The Argument at the Heart of the Book |
| 2 | The Background the Author Comes From |
| 3 | Practical Applications Beyond the Book |
| 4 | What this Might Possibly Mean for You |
| 5 | What Your Next Learning Step Could Be |

**They replace:** What this Book is Actually Saying; Where the Author is Coming From; What Could this Mean for Society?; What You Can Take From the Book; What are Your Next Learning Steps?

## 2. Why, in his words

> "some of them just read a little bit arrogant ... a little bit presumptuous rather than inviting. I'd like them to read just in a slightly more inviting and broader, yet more compelling frame."

He arrived at these over four passes, with Code measuring each set in the rendered contents panel and reading the wording back. **Measured on the live page: all five sit on two lines in the 256 panel, exactly as the current five do.** The working limit is about 38 characters at that width, not the 33 Code first estimated; the longest of his is 38.

## 3. The question mark is gone, and that is settled

The fourth arrived as "What this Might Possibly Mean for You?", which made it the only one of the five ending in a question mark. Put to Kain in the sitting, he ruled: **"drop the question mark".** All five are statements. **Nothing here is left open.**

## 4. Two readings Code put to him that he heard and did not take

Recorded so nobody reopens them as though they were missed.

- **"Might Possibly" in the fourth is the same hedge twice.** Raised three times, kept each time. It is his.
- **"Learning Step" in the fifth** carries the assumption that the reader is on a course, which was one of the three faults he originally named. He put it back deliberately in his final pass.

## 5. What has to happen, in order, and none of it is the theme first

1. **99 record bodies rewritten** to carry the new five in this order. Cowork's work, Chat's to sequence.
2. **Then** the five strings change in `single-book_note.php` and in `content_gate_standards.json`.
3. **Then** the re-import or in-place update, which is Code's.

**The order is not a preference.** `content_gate` refuses a book note whose body does not carry exactly these five in exactly this order, so changing the theme first would fail every book note on the install.

OWED BACK: the sequencing for the 99. Nothing else; the wording is closed.
