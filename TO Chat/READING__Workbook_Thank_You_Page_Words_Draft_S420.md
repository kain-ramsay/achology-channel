## Block 4. What a workbook cannot do

**Heading:** What a workbook can and cannot do

A workbook can show you an idea and ask you good questions about your own life. What it cannot do is answer back. It cannot notice what you missed, show you how the same idea looks from another person's side, or keep you practising when the week gets busy. An idea can make complete sense on the page and fade by the following Friday. That says nothing about you. Learning something well takes practice, depth and other people.
