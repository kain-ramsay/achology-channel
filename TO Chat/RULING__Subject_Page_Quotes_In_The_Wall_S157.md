**Kain's words, on the built pages:** "there isn't any quotes incorporated into this ... We've got articles and book notes, but no quotes ... is there a reason why there are no quotes whatsoever? In any of the 7 walls?" **Why the wall had none.** The approved prototype was drawn on Psychology, which holds no quotes of its own, so the wall was designed with two tile kinds only and the Quotes filter hides when its kind is empty. The build sheet's "empty kinds are hidden" was correct for Psychology and wrong for Mental Wellness (204 quotes), Helping People (75) and Motivation (13). Code also hid the Quotes filter on every category at S157's first build, which was Code's call and was wrong where quotes exist.

**What was built at 0.707.161 (commit on main):**
- A category's wall shows the quotes filed in that category plus every quote that shares one of the category's own topic tags. The five content attribute tags (DSRD 1 section 5.4) never count. **No quote is moved and no address changes**: a quote shown on another category's wall opens at its own page, with its own category's breadcrumb.
- Each quote is drawn by the site's signed quote card (DSRD 8 section 6.3), unchanged.
- At most one quote a row: 12 quotes in the 48, spread through the whole grid in a fixed rotation (second, fourth, first, third place); articles and book notes mix in the other 36 as at 0.707.160.
- The Quotes filter is back, with the pool's count; the band's Quotes link opens the wall on quotes where there are some.
- All seven walls measured: 12 quotes, 48 cards, column feet level, on every category.

**Two things for Chat or Kain to know.** The pool counts are large (141 to 255) because the topic tags are broad, so the same newest quotes can appear on more than one wall; "for now" as Kain said. The quote card carries no word count or reading time, so DSRD 8's card rule (S154) is not met by it; a ruling on the quote card is Chat's.

**A true reassignment, if it is ever wanted,** starts in the content records (a quote's address contains its category, so each moved quote needs a 301 redirect row and the record's category changed, or the next import puts it back). Not done, not recommended for this.
