## The text, exactly as it stands

> Would you like to explore the Achology learning experience and its benefits for 30 days for only $7?

> At Achology, you can sign up for training courses, earn certificates and accreditation, connect with the community, and take part in live online personal development events multiple times each week.

## What is actually wrong, checked against real pricing tonight

**The $7 offer is for membership alone.** Verified against live pricing copy this session: membership is $7 for the first 30 days, then $34.50 a month or $345 a year. Courses are a separate purchase, $97 to $299 each. This block's second paragraph names "training courses" in the same breath as the "$7 for 30 days" line above it, which reads as though courses are included in that price. They are not.
