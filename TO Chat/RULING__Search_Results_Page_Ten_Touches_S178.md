# RULING: the search results page's ten touches, Kain's pick (Code, S178, 22 October 2026)

For Chat: Kain's pick at stage 4 of the search page's sign-off (PROCESS__Search_Results_Page_Sign_Off_S178, in the Search Results Page design folder), for the record in DSRD 9 section 38.7.

Shown to Kain in the workbook player on the real page (theme previews/kh-search-touches-s178, built as 0.707.435), each touch Before and After at three widths. Card arrival was left out on purpose: it is the shared Rise in turn (BRIEF__Rise_In_Turn_On_Every_Knowledge_Hub_Wall_S440).

Code recommended nine of the ten, leaving out touch 8 (the search words turning orange under the pointer), because with touch 7 two orange things would light on one result at once. Kain's words, asked "Shall I build in all ten except 8?": "yes, please do Claude".

Taken, nine, all on the page's own parts (the field, the count, the result's insides, the A to Z), none on a shared part:
1. The number of results counts up as the page opens.
2. Your search words get a soft highlighter stroke, one result after another.
3. The search box glows softly orange when you click into it.
4. When you type new words, the Search button glows ready.
5. When you press Search, the magnifier sweeps while the next page loads.
6. A result's picture moves a little closer under the pointer.
7. An orange line appears under a result's title under the pointer.
9. A book turns to face you under the pointer.
10. When you jump to a letter in the A to Z, its orange line draws in.

Left out: 8.

Built in as theme 0.707.436 (deployed, checked in WebKit at 1280, 834 and 390: every touch plays, no console error, no sideways scroll; under reduced motion nothing moves and the count simply shows). The preview player came back off the page with it. Every movement stays still under reduced motion; pointer looks only where there is a pointer.
