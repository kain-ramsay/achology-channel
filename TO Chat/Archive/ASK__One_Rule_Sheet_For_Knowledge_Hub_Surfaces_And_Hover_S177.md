## The draft rules
1. **Two kinds of surface.** A *pattern surface* is a topic's own card (the topic tile, the pointer card): it shows its icon pattern at rest, faintly. A *plain surface* is everything else that opens a page (the doors, the kinds buttons, the logo card, the Knowledge Hub's other cards): white at rest, no pattern.
2. **One hover for everything that opens a page.** It lifts 2 pixels and takes the one warm hover shadow, over 0.3 seconds.
3. **One pattern movement.** On hover the pattern rises and drifts: on a plain surface from nothing to 0.08, on a pattern surface from 0.14 to 0.22, drifting 16 pixels over 1.2 seconds, the same everywhere.
4. **One arrow.** Every card or button that opens a page ends in the arrow; on hover it slides 3 pixels. (The doors gain one, beside their topic count.)
5. **Colour belongs to families.** A family's colour is used only on that family's own parts (its door, heading, quilt squares, pointer card, tiles). Anything that is not a family (the kinds buttons, View topic, the topic counts, the logo card) stays brand orange.
6. **One icon holder per surface.** A white disc on a pattern surface; a tinted circle on a plain surface.
7. **Small things stay simple.** Quilt squares lift 2 and turn white; the key's names turn orange. No patterns on anything smaller than a card.
8. **The keyboard sees what the mouse sees.** Focus shows the hover plus the 2 pixel orange ring.
9. **Less motion when asked.** Where a visitor's device asks for reduced motion: no lift and no drift, colour changes only.
10. **One timing.** 0.3 seconds for everything that moves; 1.2 seconds for a pattern's drift; a pointer card opens at once and closes 0.16 seconds after the mouse leaves.
