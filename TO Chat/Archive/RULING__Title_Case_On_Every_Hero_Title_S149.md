# RULING: title case on every Knowledge Hub title (Kain, S149, theme session)

**Kain's words.** "The use of capitalisation is inconsistent across all three types of article title in the hero banners." Shown four rendered options in Safari (title case; first word only; every word; as now), he chose: "Yes, Claude, I definitely want um, option one, title case, please."

**And on book notes:** "on the book notes, I think I would quite like for everything that comes after the colon to to be as it is now, it's like a lighter ... lesser weight of ... font." The book note title already draws the part after the colon in a lighter weight (the hero's title-rest span); the preview flattened it by mistake. Book notes stay exactly as they are live.

**What title case means here (the option he chose, as rendered):** the big words capitalised, the small words not (a, an, and, as, at, but, by, for, in, nor, of, on, or, the, to, with), the first and last word always capitalised, and a name or book title keeping its own capitals. Examples from the preview: "What Does It Mean to Be Present, and Why Is It So Hard?"; "Why Trust Is the Key Ingredient of a Coaching Relationship".

**What it touches.** The Content Standard says nothing on capitals today, and DSRD 2 section 3.3 asks quote H1s for "every word capitalised", which is why the types drifted.
