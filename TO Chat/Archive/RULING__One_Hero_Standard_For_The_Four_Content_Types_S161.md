## 1. One length rule for all four types (Kain: "yes")

Kain, S161: "the titles, the subtitles are all completely different lengths. There's no consistent standard here." Measured in WebKit (Safari's engine) with real fonts at the hero's 640 measure: a title fills exactly two lines from about 40 to 78 characters; a subtitle fills exactly three lines from about 130 to 194. The rule sits inside both, clear of the edges:

- **Title: 50 to 60 characters, counting spaces, on every type.** Two well-filled lines in the band, and inside what search results show in full (the page heading and the search title are the same words, "one title, not two").
- **Subtitle: 150 to 170 characters, counting spaces, on every type.** Three full lines. The same range Kain approved for workbook subtitles earlier in S161 (RULING__Workbook_Page_Title_Subtitle_And_Layout_S161), now for all four.
- **Each type keeps its own pattern** inside the ranges: articles and quotes a question; book notes "[Book] by [Author]: Summary and Key Ideas"; workbooks "[Name] Workbook: Free Worksheet and Exercise" (DSRD 2 section 1.7's endings).
- **Measured today, out of range:** article (Jung's Shadow) title 73, subtitle 148; book note (Authentic Happiness) title 61, subtitle 224 (four lines); quote (A Good Coach) title 43 (one line without the measure), subtitle 110 (two lines); workbook (Karpman) title 60, subtitle 121.
- **Owed:** the rule in The Achology Content Standard and in each type's owning section; the content gate checks both counts on every page (factory); the lanes bring existing pages into range, the words theirs and Kain's.
