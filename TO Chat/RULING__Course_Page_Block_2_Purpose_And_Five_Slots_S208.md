## The ruling

**Purpose.** Block 2 has one job (BLOCK_MAP.md, pick 38, change 2): to tell the visitor what they will understand and be able to do by the end, and whether the course is for someone like them. Nothing else.

**The introduction video leaves block 2.** It goes back to block 4, Try the Teaching, beside the six free lessons, where the agreed block map placed it. This reverses PICKS.md pick 48's floating video panel.

**The five slots, the same on all 28 course pages.**

1. **Heading:** "What You Will Learn", fixed, the same on every page.
2. **Supporting line:** the course's promise in one sentence, 12 to 25 words (DSRD 7 section 3.3, rules 2 and 3).
3. **Four paragraphs, 45 to 65 words each, each with a set job, in this order:**
   - what the course is, and the question it answers;
   - the ground it covers;
   - what the reader will be able to do by the end;
   - who it is for, and what they need before they start.
4. **New: "By the end of this course, you'll be able to"**, a list of six outcomes, each opening with a doing word, 6 to 12 words each. It sits in the column the video held. Why: most visitors arrive on a phone and skim, so this gives them the whole promise at a glance; search engines and AI assistants read such a list as the course's outcomes.
5. **Every line from the course's own facts:** nothing promised that its lessons do not teach. Each outcome traces to the course's lesson file.
