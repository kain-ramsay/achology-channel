## What is being added, and why it was missed

**The author signature block.** The card at the foot of every article body carrying the writer's avatar, name and one-line bio.

**It is the only block on the article page that has never been through anything.** It is built, it is live, DSRD 9 section 22.7 specifies it, and it has **no section in DSRD 8, no folder, no prototype and no data file.** Every other block on that page is either approved with a record, or named as open with a sitting waiting for it. This one is in neither state, which is how it went unlooked at.

**Found at S303** while mapping every component on the article page against its owning document, on Kain's instruction after a design session that had gone badly for exactly this reason: work being done without the specifications being read first.

## Where it sits in the order

**Fifth.** After the footer, not before it. The four ahead of it each reach 18 to 29 templates and gate the eleven Knowledge Hub pages; this one reaches the article page and, once they exist, the other content templates. It does not block anything, so it does not jump the queue.

Sitting 1, the header, closed this morning. Four remain.

## What it is, measured rather than recalled

**I have not measured it in the theme, and I am not going to guess.** Chat does not read the theme; you do. What I hold is the specification side:

- **DSRD 9 section 22.7** specifies it: off-white #F3F4F4 card, 10px corner, 20px padding, flex row with 16px gap, 32px below the last body element. Avatar 52px circle with a brand dark gradient and initials in Como 18/600 white as the fallback. Name Como 15/600 brand dark linking to `/about/instructors/{slug}/`, brand orange on hover. Bio line Source Sans 3 13/400 soft grey, one line.
- **The bio is read from the people registry in the theme.** Never written into a page, never carried in an upload file. That rule is already ruled twice, for this block at DSRD 9 section 22.7 and for the book note page at DSRD 2 section 1.6.
- **The byline is the Achology pen name or instructor who wrote the piece**, which DSRD 1 section 3 distinguishes from the source author, a different person with a different page.
- **The theme carries an `.author-card__*` class family** including a monogram fallback, a role line in the AA-safe orange, and an ellipsised line. I have seen those rules; I have not counted them or checked how many templates they reach. **Measure it before the sitting, the way you measured the other four.**

## The three questions, unchanged

The same three the sheet already sets for every sitting. Does it hold as it is. Where a value has never been ruled, what is it. And, for this one, one extra worth putting to him:

**Does the article page need this block at all, given the byline already names the writer in the meta line a few centimetres above it?** DSRD 9 section 22.12 decision D8 answers it as ruled, that the author appears in two positions deliberately, meta line for quick attribution and signature block for identity and bio. So it is settled on paper. It is worth his eye anyway, because it is the same objection he raised at S268 about the instructor source variant, and he cancelled that one.
