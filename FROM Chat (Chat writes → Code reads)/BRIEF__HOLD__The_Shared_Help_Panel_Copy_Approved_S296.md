## A recorded exception you need to know about

Chat raised the two card lines as failing chapter 1's front-door rule, on the grounds that "Achology membership" and "members" are never identified. **Kain ruled that a call-to-action block does not carry term identifications, and that ruling stands.**

The block's job is to be short enough to act on. An identification inside an eleven-word card would be most of the card, and a reader who came for a next step would meet a definition instead.

**The exception covers this panel's card lines and card names only**, on all three help page types. It does not reach article bodies, category descriptions or any other page copy, where chapter 1 applies in full. It is written into the records as a recorded exception approved by Kain, not as a skipped line.
