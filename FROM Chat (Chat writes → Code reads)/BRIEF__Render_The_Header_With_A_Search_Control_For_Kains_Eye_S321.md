## Where this came from

Chat ran DSRD 6 chapter 8 on the /about/ page this session, from the S079 captures you returned through the channel. The record is written into the About Landing Page folder.

The chapter's trunk test asks whether a stranger dropped onto a page from a search result can answer five questions. This page answers four and fails the fifth: **how do I search.** There is no search control on the page at any width. The desktop header carries six navigation items and a sign in button; the phone header carries the mark and the menu control.

**It is not a fault of that page.** The header is a locked component, so the same fail lands on every page on the site, and one change fixes all of them.

## Why it is worth doing rather than recording an exception

Three reasons, and the third is the one that decides it.

The trunk test names finding search as one of five things a stranded visitor must be able to do, and this is the only one of the five the site fails.

A Knowledge Hub of thousands of pages cannot be navigated by menu alone. The menu can reach categories; it cannot reach a piece.

**And the site is about to have a real search engine and still not expose it.** SearchWP is bought, the token is on Kain's Desktop, and your own install list ends with excluding the search results page from the dynamic cache. So the destination this control needs already exists in your plan. The control is the front door to the page you are already building.

## What to render

Options, tabbed, one on screen at a time in the same position, on the same page, with only the one variable changed. Standing rule 16's render standard in full: real chrome, real page behind it, at desktop, tablet and phone width, because the phone header is the tightest and is where this decision is actually made.

Chat is not specifying the design, because that is exactly the decision Kain takes by eye and Chat has no business pre-empting it. What the options should differ on is the thing he is actually choosing between: how much room the control takes in a header that is already full. A mark that opens a field, and a field that is always there, are the two ends of that, and anything between them that you judge worth his look.

**Two things hold whatever he picks.**

The control posts to the site's own search results page. It is the front end of SearchWP, not a second search of its own.

Every registered chrome string comes from DSRD 8 section 18, read this session. If the option needs a label or placeholder wording that section does not hold, that wording is a new registered string and it is Kain's, so bring it as part of the same sitting rather than choosing one.

## What happens after he rules

You export the approved state as the header prototype's next version and update its build sheet, per the fold-back in standing rule 16. File the RULING through the channel and Chat writes it into DSRD 8 at close.

**One consequence to carry with it.** The trunk test finding is recorded on the /about/ record as a site-wide chrome fault. When the header ships with search, that line closes on every page's record, not just the one it was found on. Say so in your report so Chat closes them together rather than one at a time.

## Not in this brief

The search results page's own design, its filtering, and how SearchWP's two engines are configured. Those are the install, and they are already on your list.
