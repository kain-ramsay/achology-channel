## Change 1: `policies-content/manifesto.php`

The first sentence of the second paragraph in the "Read Aloud, Every Single Day of the Week"
section, which carries the page's first use of the word member.

**Current:**

> Every day, between three and five [live events](/free-events/) run inside the Achology community: workshops, group discussions, practice sessions and mentorship groups. Each one opens the same way. Before the work begins, a member reads the manifesto aloud to the room.

**Replace the first sentence only with:**

> Every day, three to five live online events take place within the Achology community at community.achology.com, where members can practise skills from their courses through workshops, group discussions, practice sessions, and mentorship groups.

**The two sentences after it are unchanged**, including "a member reads the manifesto aloud
to the room", which is now a second use and correct.

**Keep the existing link.** The sentence being replaced carries a link to `/free-events/` on
the words "live events". Put it on "live online events" in the new sentence, per DSRD 1
§6.4's rule that a link sits on words already in the sentence. **Mechanical carry-over, not
a copy decision: do not drop the link, and do not move it to different words.**

**One letter differs from Kain's pasted wording: practise, not practice**, the verb form in
UK English, under his S295 ruling on the same word in the register entry. The noun "practice
sessions" later in the same sentence is correct as written and stays.

## Change 2: `policies-content/code-of-ethics.php`

The third paragraph of the "What the Framework Is and Why It Matters" section, which carries
that page's first use of the word member.

**Current:**

> Achology members are encouraged to read the Ethical Framework regularly, familiarising themselves with its core principles before working with clients. Embedding these standards into your everyday practice is far more valuable than referring to them only when difficulties or challenges arise.

**Replace the first sentence only with:**

> Achology members who have joined the private learning community at community.achology.com are encouraged to regularly review the Ethical Framework and become familiar with its core principles before engaging in any form of professional practice or working with clients.

**The second sentence is unchanged.**
