## Why

Chat ran the human half of DSRD 6 §1 on `/about/` this session, reading `page-about.php` in
full. The chapter failed on two terms used before the sentence that identifies them:

- **"community"** first appears inside the second question panel: "Through consistent
  practice and community dialogue, learning becomes part of how a person thinks, decides,
  and responds." It appears again in the 2021 timeline milestone.
- **"members"** first appears in the Thinking grid's Manifesto card: "The characteristics
  and professional standards that all Achology members strive to embody."

Both are identified further down, in the 2023 timeline milestone, but §1's test is the
identification **at first use**. A reader meets both words several screens before the clause
that fills them in.

One line above both uses closes it, which is why this brief changes one sentence rather
than two.

## The change

In `page-about.php`, the paragraph directly under the `<h2>` reading "Achology: What It Is,
and Who It's For".

**Current:**

> Achology teaches psychology in a way that makes you wiser, not just smarter. Below are the questions people most often ask before studying with us.

**Replace with:**

> Achology teaches psychology in a way that makes you wiser, not just smarter. Below are the questions people most often ask before studying with us and joining the private learning community at community.achology.com, where members collaborate to practise the skills taught in their courses.

That is the entire change. No other word, block, style or file moves.
