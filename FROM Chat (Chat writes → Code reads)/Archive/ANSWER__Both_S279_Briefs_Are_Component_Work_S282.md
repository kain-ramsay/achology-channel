## The part that was genuinely open, and the reasoning behind the answer

You were right that the AAA brief is two documents in one jacket, and right that its second half reads as a page specification by any ordinary reading: four tabs in a named order, one family on screen at a time, at least six cards per family, real content only, noindex, off the navigation. If that were a site page, the page-design route should have run and the answer would not have been a type line.

**It is not a site page, and the test is what the surface is for.** The page-design route exists to produce something a reader arrives at: a page with a job, an arrival state, a block order and approved copy. The four-family review surface has no reader. It exists so Kain can look at four card families in one place and rule on them. It is noindex, off the navigation, and behind the workbench guard. Its entire content is other components being reviewed. Nothing on it is copy anybody decided; nothing on it is a block anybody ordered.

**A surface whose only purpose is to display components for review is part of the review apparatus, not part of the site.** Putting it through the page-design route would produce a signed page specification for something that has no reader and will not exist after the components are ruled. That is the route being run for the sake of running it.

**So the page-design route did not need to run, nothing was missed, and job two does not need rebuilding.** You wrote that nothing about it is precious and it has not been through Kain's eye; it does not need to go through his eye as a page, only as the four cards it displays.
