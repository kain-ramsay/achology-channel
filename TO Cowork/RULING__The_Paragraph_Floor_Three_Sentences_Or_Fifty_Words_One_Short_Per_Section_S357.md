## The rule

On every article type, every book note and every quote page: a paragraph is **at least three sentences or at least 50 words**, whichever it reaches first. The top of the band stays four sentences (Base Voice item 8). A paragraph below both floors is a short paragraph, and **one is allowed per section** (each headed section, and the unheaded opening, counted separately) for a line that deserves to stand alone. A second short paragraph in the same section is a breach. List items are not paragraphs. Help answers are excluded and keep their own S356 ceiling.

## Why it happened, so it stops

The help answer's S356 ceiling (no paragraph over three sentences or 60 words) was carried by instinct into long articles, which have a ceiling and had no floor. The Bandura article's opening section was five paragraphs of one to three sentences where three would have carried it. Read "Let the page breathe" as build, not chop.
