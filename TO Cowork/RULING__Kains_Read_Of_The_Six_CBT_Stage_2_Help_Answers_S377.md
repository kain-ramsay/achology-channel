### 3. nhs-routes-into-cbt: YES, with three fixes

Kain said yes on S377. The first fix is a factual correction, not a style one.

1. **Correct the order claim throughout.** The record says the two routes run in a fixed order and that skipping straight to high intensity "isn't how the system works". That is wrong. Chat checked live trainee high intensity job adverts on 23 September 2026 (for example beta.jobs.nhs.uk C9364-24-1154, Essex Partnership University NHS Foundation Trust; findajob.dwp.gov.uk 15929107): the posts are open to psychological wellbeing practitioners with at least two years' post-qualification experience, and also to people already holding a BABCP recognised core profession (mental health nursing, clinical or counselling psychology, medicine, social work, occupational therapy, counselling, psychotherapy and others), and to people meeting the BABCP's Knowledge, Skills and Attitudes (KSA) portfolio route. Rewrite the opening, "What Is the High-Intensity Route?", "Why Does the NHS Split CBT This Way?" and "So, What Are the NHS Routes Into CBT, Overall?" so they say this: the wellbeing practitioner route is one way in; people who already hold a core mental health profession can go straight to high intensity training. Update the Search and Citation Brief claim and the sourcing record to match, citing a primary source read live (the NHS Health Careers high intensity therapist page, or the BABCP's own core professions page), not the adverts.
2. Cut the sentence "Since 2022, everyone in these roles has also had to register with a recognised professional body, which keeps a floor under the training's quality throughout." Chat could not confirm it.
3. In "Where Can You Learn CBT With Achology?", cut the sentence "Achology teaches all three itself."

The 45 days, PWP and postgraduate certificate lines stand; check them against the same Health Careers page while it is open.
