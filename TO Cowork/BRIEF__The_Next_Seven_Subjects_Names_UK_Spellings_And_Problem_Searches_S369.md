## Cutting rules

**Name searches:** as in the S369 NLP and CBT brief. Keep buying phrases and every question or statement about the subject itself. Cut other meanings, place names (keep near me, online, usa, uk), other languages, routes for people already holding a clinical licence, and **a person looking to hire or book** a coach, counsellor or hypnotherapist (near me as a client, session cost, find a).

**Problem searches, which are new:** keep every question a person asks about the problem that a plain educational article could answer honestly: what it is, why it happens, how to understand it, how to handle it yourself, how to help someone else with it. Cut:

- medication, prescriptions, doctors, diagnosis tests and screening quizzes;
- booking a therapist, treatment centres, insurance, NHS or clinic services;
- client treatment searches (hypnosis for smoking, weight loss or phobias);
- **anything about suicide, self harm or crisis: cut entirely and do not quote it in the report;** Achology does not answer those, and the right answer is a crisis service, not an article;

For "anxiety", keep questions about understanding anxious thinking and emotion; cut symptom checkers, medication and treatment. Mark nothing yourself; Chat flags at the grouping step.
