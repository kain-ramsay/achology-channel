# BRIEF: the sourcing pass on the 30 Mental Health pieces (S405)

**From Chat, S405, on Kain's yes.** Run it end to end, then hold. Read harness Version 31 and the Content Standard Version 9 first; both changed this session.

## Why

Your S403 check found facts with no source behind them on 18 of the 30 pieces, the largest group of real faults. Before the 30 are fixed and checked again, every fact needs its source found and read. This pass does that and nothing else. It holds whatever is decided about wording, so it can run now. Harness Version 31's new Rule 4 makes the same step part of every future draft.

## The pieces

The 29 Mental Health records and the pilot: the same 30 your S403 check covered (20 Help answers in `Content Records/help-answer/`, the 9 hub question articles and the pilot in `Content Records/hub-question-article/`). Your S403 check sheets beside each record name the facts the checkers could not support; start there, then cover every other fact in the piece too.

## The job, for each piece

1. List every factual claim in the body: anything stated as true about the world, a body, a study, a figure, a provider, a law or a person. Opinions labelled as Achology's are not facts and are not listed.
2. For each fact, find its source under Standard Part 7 (rule 12, with its S405 allowance for a provider's own page about itself) and open it with the fetch tool.
3. Copy the exact supporting line into the record's claims table, as the fetch tool returned it, with the source's name, link and the date read. Say "as returned by the fetch tool" once at the head of the table.
4. A fact whose line cannot be found is marked **not supported**, with one line saying what the source does say, and the nearest true wording written beside it. A source that cannot be read is **not supported: source unreadable**.
5. Change no word of the body. Change only the claims table (the sourcing record beneath the body).

## What comes back, in one DONE file in FROM Cowork

- Per piece: facts listed, supported, not supported, unreadable.
- A list of every not-supported fact: piece, the sentence, what the source says, the nearest true wording.
- Anything you could not do, and why; and a cold read of harness Version 31 and Standard Version 9: any line you read two ways.
