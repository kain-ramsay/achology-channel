## 1. What is being asked for

**One short spoken script per course, twenty eight in all.** Each one becomes a video of Kain speaking to camera about that course.

**The scripts that already exist do not count.** Sixteen were exported from Evernote and Kain has ruled them a starting point rather than finished work: they do not accurately describe the courses. **Do not edit them and do not read them as a model.** This is a fresh write from the real source below.

## 2. The source, and it is the same shape for all twenty eight

**Every course's first lecture is its own course explanation, delivered by Kain.** That lecture's transcript is the source for that course's script.

The transcript bank is in the project folder: the Vimeo Exports folder, then `output`, then `transcripts`, then one folder per course numbered 001 to 028. Read the `.corrected.txt` file, not the raw `.txt` and not the `.vtt`.

**The job is to boil it down.** A twenty minute spoken introduction becomes a ninety second spoken script that says the same true things faster and better. Nothing is invented; the material is already in his mouth.

**Where the opening lecture is thin, read the course's section titles and lecture questions too**, from the section index and lesson index in the Course + Lesson Data folder. Those are Achology's own descriptions of what the course teaches. Never fill a gap from imagination or from the old website's marketing copy.

## 3. The two jobs each script has to do, and the second one shapes everything

**Job one:** the explainer video on that course's page. A visitor who is interested watches it and understands what the course actually is.

**Job two, and this is Kain's ruling at S355:** the same video runs as a **YouTube advert**. That changes the writing, in three ways.

- **The first ten seconds carry the whole thing.** On YouTube the viewer can skip, and most do. The opening line has to earn the next line. Not "in this course we will explore", which is the sound of a video about to be skipped.
- **It has to be enticing to somebody who has never heard of Achology.** No inside vocabulary, no assumed context, no course numbers, no talk of modules and sections.
- **It ends on one clear next step**, and only one.

## 4. Length, and the reason

**250 to 350 words. About ninety seconds to two minutes spoken.**

Chat's call, named so Kain can overturn it. The old scripts ran 310 to 670 words, which is three to five minutes. **A four minute video is not an advert.** Ninety seconds is long enough to say what a course is and short enough that a stranger stays. Where a course genuinely needs more, say so in the record rather than running long quietly.

## 5. Voice

**Kain speaks these, so they are written in his voice**: the Kain Ramsay voice profile in the vault, `02-Achology/Editorial Voices/`. Read it before drafting. Spoken register, not written: short sentences, said out loud, contractions where he would use them.

## 6. The hard limits

**Course facts come from DSRD 5 and nowhere else.** Student numbers, ratings, hours, lesson counts, the canonical course name. If a figure is not in DSRD 5, it does not go in the script.

**No overclaiming.** Nothing that promises an outcome, a qualification, a professional standing or a mental health result the course does not deliver. This is PRD Criterion 8 and it is a launch blocker for the whole site, not a style preference.

**The vault note `Achology Teaches The Mind, Not The Brain` binds any script touching mental health.** Read it if the course goes near diagnosis or wellbeing.
