## 1. The category is Psychology

`kh_category` is **Psychology**, one of the seven canonical categories (DSRD 1 section 4).

**Why, read against the category's own confirmed description (DSRD 2 section 1.3.1).** Psychology "explores how people think, feel, behave, and make sense of the world", and its content runs "from classic psychological theories to practical everyday insight". A 24-article series on the history and validity of psychiatric classification is a series about psychological theory and how it was built. It sits there.

**Why not Mental Wellness, which is the obvious other candidate.** That category is about "the habits, perspectives, and practices that support emotional balance and resilience", and its job is practical self-care. This series is not that. Filing it there would put a critical examination of the DSM among pages about managing stress, which serves neither reader.

## 2. The address

`/learn/psychology/articles/{slug}/`

The address segment is the category, so section 1 decides it. Nothing further is chosen. Article addresses take this shape by DSRD 1 section 1.

## 3. The destination school follows automatically, and no override fires

DSRD 1 section 4.1 routes the Psychology category to **The School of Cognitive Behavioural Psychology (CBP)**.

The subject-override rule in that section fires only where a reader finishing the piece would find the destination **plainly** odd, not merely arguable. It does not fire here: CBP is the school built on experimental, evidence-based psychology, which is the exact ground this series argues from. **No override is recorded.**

## 4. One constraint that binds every one of the 24, and it is not optional

The vault note **Achology Teaches The Mind, Not The Brain** binds every page touching diagnosis, mental health or wellbeing, in any voice. Read it before drafting.

What it permits: a label is a description, not an explanation.

What it forbids: that conditions do not exist; that no diagnosis has empirical backing; that psychiatry is not medicine. All three are out, in every article, however tempting the source material makes them.

The PRD's Criterion 8 risk is the reason: over-claiming about mental health outcomes is a launch blocker for the whole site.

## 5. The sourcing register governs every claim

`claude_DSM_Validity_Series_-_Ch1-2_Sourcing_Register.md` in the project files holds 27 verified claims. **Nothing is carried into an article unless it is marked CONFIRMED or CONFIRMED-WITH-CORRECTION, and where a correction exists only the corrected version is used.**

Three are marked DO NOT CARRY and are named here so nobody re-finds them: the "only 1 of 157 DSM-5 disorders" statistic, the "Ms T" case study, which appears to be invented, and the April 2021 "state of emergency" phrasing.
