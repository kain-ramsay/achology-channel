## 3. What a hub guide is, in one paragraph

A hub is a long guide article on one searched subject, marked Pillar Content in Rank Math. It is not a tag page, a course page or a school page, and it is not a new page type: it publishes on the ordinary article template with a contents list added at the top and a gathering block added at the foot. **The guide covers the whole subject; the spokes go deep.** Every other CBT piece on the site points up to it, and it points back down to them.
