## 1. The SEO title is no longer yours to choose

The S341 brief told you to write each note's SEO title to the step 3 bounds around the keyword the check finds. Kain has since fixed the shape. **Every book note's Rank Math SEO title is `{Book}: Summary and Key Ideas | Achology`**, the book's name first because it is the keyword and the title must carry it verbatim. Two tiers: where that runs past 60 characters, drop `| Achology`; where even `{Book}: Summary and Key Ideas` passes 60, use `{Book}: Summary`. Where the book carries a long subtitle, the `{Book}` slot carries the keyword's own form of the name.

Code has already written this title onto 62 of the 65 live pages at their source records, the master and the install. **Do not write a different SEO title on any row.** Your correction table's SEO title column carries the pattern, computed from the keyword, and nothing else.

## 2. What the check still decides

Everything else in section 2 of the S341 brief: the evidence line, the focus keyword (the short phrase, claimed in the register, next best phrase where claimed), the H1 (the question people ask, honest, no gimmick), and the description (keyword in the first 120 characters, under 155, a plain answer). Where the evidence proves people search the bare book name, the keyword is the bare book name and the `{Book}` slot is that. Where the evidence points elsewhere, the keyword is what the evidence supports, the `{Book}` slot carries that keyword's form of the name, and the row is marked for Chat exactly as the brief says.

## 3. Three rows whose keywords are already decided

Three of the 65 collide on their bare names with other pages, which is why their keywords carried the author's surname since S338: `free-will-sam-harris`, `emotional-leonard-mlodinow`, `nature-emerson`. A title cannot carry `free will sam harris` verbatim and read as a title anyone would choose, so Chat ruled at S342 (the keyword register is Chat's): **their keywords take the form `{book} by {author}`**: `free will by sam harris`, `emotional by leonard mlodinow`, `nature by emerson`. Their SEO titles then read `Free Will by Sam Harris: Summary and Key Ideas | Achology` (57), `Emotional by Leonard Mlodinow: Summary and Key Ideas` (52, second tier), `Nature by Emerson: Summary and Key Ideas | Achology` (51). Run their demand check as for every other row and record the evidence, but the keyword is fixed unless the evidence is against the `by` form outright, in which case mark the row for Chat rather than choosing. Their records' opening paragraphs must carry the new phrase verbatim; that is a one-sentence edit each, inside this run, and is the only body edit this addendum authorises.
