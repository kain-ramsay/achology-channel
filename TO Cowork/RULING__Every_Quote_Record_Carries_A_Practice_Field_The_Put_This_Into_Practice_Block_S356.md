## The block

Every quote page, article and book note carries one labelled block, "Put this into practice": one exercise a reader can do today, drawn from the page's own teaching and never a new idea, 40 to 60 words, written to you, in the fixed shape when, do, notice. One per page, no list, no button, no link. It is a field on the record, `practice`, added as a row in the page fields table, and the theme renders it between the second heading's prose and the third heading; you write nothing into the body for it.

The exemplar's, on Q07026, so you have the shape: "The next time you catch yourself measuring your life against someone else's, pause before the comparison finishes. Ask whose rulebook you are using to keep score, and notice whether you ever agreed to it. If you did not, you have just found one rule you are free to rewrite." Fifty-five words. Its exercise comes from the page's own second part, not from anywhere else.
