## The four rules, in one breath each

1. The link text names the thing the reader gets: "our book note on *The Ultimate Life Coaching Handbook*", "the UK Register of Learning Providers". Never "here", "click here", "read more", "learn more", "this page", "this article".
2. The important words come first, because people read the first two words of a link.
3. On one page, the same words always go to the same place, and two different destinations never share their words.
4. A link is a promise: it goes where its words say.
