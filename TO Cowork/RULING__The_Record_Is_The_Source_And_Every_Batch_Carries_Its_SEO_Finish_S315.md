## What changes for Cowork

0. Before any title is set, run stage 0: a live web search on the seed phrase (Google autocomplete, People Also Ask, one AI assistant asked the question), title the piece in the words real people use, paste the evidence into the record's notes block, and claim the keyword in `KEYWORD_REGISTER.csv` beside the pipeline document. No evidence, no title. Never state a Rank Math score; only Code can read one.
1. The record you produce is the only source of truth for its piece. Every field in section 3 of the process file is filled at drafting, including `author_website_url` (from the author's own site; empty where none exists), the three Rank Math fields, and the image filenames and alt text.
2. The body carries only the ruled sections for the page type. Gate printouts and notes sit outside the body, never inside it.
3. The SEO finish in section 5 is written at drafting: keyword placement, one internal and one followed external link to the original, no paragraph over 120 words, alt text carrying the keyword, and a unique keyword per page. The target is 90 on Rank Math. The three title gimmick tests are refused; do not write power words, numbers or emotional hooks into titles to please them.
4. Batches of five. The content gate runs on each record and its printout goes into the record. One batch report to FROM Cowork per batch, naming every record and its result.
