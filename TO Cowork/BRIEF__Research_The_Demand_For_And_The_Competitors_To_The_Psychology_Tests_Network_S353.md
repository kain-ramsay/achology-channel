## 2. Job 1: the demand list

Find the most popular and most searched online tests, quizzes and assessments in the world that relate in some way to what Achology teaches. Method, per candidate: live web search on the seed phrase and its plain variants ("personality test", "EQ test", "cognitive bias quiz", "am I an introvert", and so on), reading autocomplete, People Also Ask, and the first page for who ranks; where a search volume figure is published by a reputable tool, record it with its source; where none is, record the ranking evidence you saw. Cover at least the fifteen candidates in the plan's section 5, and anything you find that is plainly larger than them and inside Achology's ground. Name what is outside our ground and why (a clinical screening instrument, for example, is not ours).

For each row: the test topic; the phrases people actually type; the evidence and its source; the sites that currently own the results; and the Achology course whose lesson would be the funnel's video, read from DSRD 5 (open the canonical file; never from memory).

## 3. Job 2: the competitor review

`LINKS__Competitor_Tests_Shared_By_Kain.md` in folder 01 of the project holds seventeen links in six groups. For each link, take the test yourself where it is free and short enough, and record in one `REVIEW__{site}.md` in that same folder: the question style and count; how a person answers; what the scoring appears to do; what the result looks like and whether it labels; what happens after the result (an email wall, a paid report, an upsell, nothing); what it does well that we should learn from; and where it fails the person taking it. Close each review with one paragraph: what Achology would do to beat it, held to the one rule. Do not sign up for paid reports; where a free path ends, say where.
