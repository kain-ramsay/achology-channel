# RULING: every Achology article is descriptive and full; short-form, shorthand writing is banned

**From:** Claude Cowork, run of Monday 28 September 2026. **Given by Kain,** in the Cowork session, in two turns, after reading Part 2 of the Seven Beliefs series (`PART_02__can-people-change.md`, Content Records, seven-beliefs-series). This file replaces the narrower version filed earlier the same run, which covered the series only.

## Kain's words
First turn: "Some of the sentences that you've included in this just kind of end really abruptly and dryly. What I'd really hoped for was that you would draft this in a descriptive and full way, without short, sharp sentences, assuming and hoping that the reader is going to know what we mean by our generalized vagueness. I don't want vagueness to be incorporated into these articles. I want these articles to be descriptive, informative, and enlightening."

Second turn: "I'm not just giving you instructions for this one article. I want you to apply this standard to all of our articles. I'm finding now that I'm having to give you instructions like this on multiple occasions because you're essentially not recording or building into your memory how it is that I want you to write. I want these articles descriptive, informative, enticing, not shorthand. I want you from here on in to put a ban on all short form, shorthand writing. It is not okay, not acceptable in these Achology articles."

## The standard
- **Scope:** every Achology article, of every type, in every series, from now on.
- **Every sentence is complete.** It states the whole thought and explains what it means. No clipped endings, no sentence that stops where the explanation should begin.
- **Nothing vague is left for the reader to decode.** A figure of speech, a summary line or a general claim is always followed through into plain, specific explanation.
- **Short, sharp sentences are banned as a style.** They are never used for rhythm or punch.
- **The aim, in Kain's words:** descriptive, informative, enlightening and enticing.

## Examples of the fault, from Part 2
- "Becoming yourself is something you have to will." It never says what that looks like in a day.
- "What survives them is the shape." It gives an image with no explanation.
- "Treating one number as the last word on a person closes a door that was never locked." It is a metaphor left unexplained.
- "Can people change overnight? Almost never, and anyone who promises you that is selling something." It stops where it should explain why.

## Why it keeps happening: the written rules push the other way
These lines currently instruct the very writing Kain has now banned. Each needs changing or retiring, or the fault will return in every new session:
1. **The Achology Base Voice, item 3:** "Vary the rhythm. Follow a long sentence with a short one."
2. **The Achology Base Voice, item 8:** the allowance of a deliberate single-line paragraph as "a rhythm device" (S299, narrowed S357).
3. **The Flesch reading-ease band of 60 to 70** (Base Voice item 10, `content_gate_standards.json`, Recipe 9). It rewards short sentences. Fuller sentences can still land in the band with plain words, but the band must never be met by clipping. Recipe 9 and the base voice should say which wins if they meet.
4. **Recipe 9, "How every part sounds":** it has no line against shorthand. It needs this ruling added.

`house-copy-standards` should gain the ban as a checklist line, so every content-type skill inherits it.
