# Ruling: Kain signed the Achology mindfulness frame

Session S389. Kain read the page "What Achology Means By Mindfulness" and signed it, saying it is much better and adding four points, all now in the page.

1. Mindfulness at Achology exists to give people the self-knowledge and self-understanding to take a greater degree of mental, emotional and behavioural responsibility for their lives.
2. This is Achology's primary purpose in teaching mindfulness the way it does, and it is what sets Achology apart from most mindfulness teaching, which is mostly experiential.
3. Breathing is not taught: people have breathed since birth, and it is not a technique or a skill.
4. The way Achology teaches mindfulness must be presented accurately, because accuracy is the differentiator.
