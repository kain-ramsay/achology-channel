## The script

Most coaching training starts with goal-setting, straight out of the gate. This one deliberately doesn't. Because it really doesn't matter how good a goal looks on paper, if it's sitting on top of a belief that's quietly working against it the whole time.

I'm Kain Ramsay, and this blueprint walks you through limiting beliefs and core values first, so that by the time you actually reach goal-setting, you're clearing the way ahead rather than setting someone up to fail before they've even started. You'll get both our own branded resources and unbranded ones too, so you can take everything you learn here and use it with your own future clients.

Over seventeen thousand people have already taken this course, and it's rated 4.58 out of 5, because people find it genuinely more complete than the courses that jump straight to goals.

If you're ready to learn the complete process, not just the goal-setting part of it, Life Coaching Blueprint: The Complete Process and Practices is open right now, for you to start today.
