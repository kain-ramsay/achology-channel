## The script

Have you ever noticed how two people can live through the exact same situation, and come away with two completely different reactions to it? That gap, between what actually happens to us and how we choose to respond to it, is where cognitive behavioural therapy really lives.

I'm Kain Ramsay, and this toolkit is my own introduction to the ideas Aaron Beck first connected all those years ago, the link between what happens inside your head and what you go on to say and do out in the world. It's not about diagnosing anyone, and it's not about putting labels on people. It's a set of practical principles you can use to understand yourself, and the people around you, a good deal more clearly.

Over four thousand eight hundred people have already taken this course, and it's rated 4.48 out of 5, because people find it genuinely useful in their everyday relationships, not just in theory.

If you're ready to understand that connection for yourself, The CBT Toolkit: Core Principles and Real-World Applications is open right now, for you to start today.
