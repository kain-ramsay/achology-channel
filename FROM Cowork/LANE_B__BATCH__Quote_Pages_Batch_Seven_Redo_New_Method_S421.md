## Which quote word or idea the Look turned on

- CQ001-145-1, Why Connection Determines Your Peace: the word "depth" (not length, not intensity, but how much of what's real gets said and received), and the one-way arrow from connection to peace.
- CQ001-147-1, Why Clear Requests Protect Boundaries: the words "at least", the line's modest promise of clarity and nothing about the reply (with "honest and humble" and "requests" taken first).
- CQ001-148-1, Why You Can Manage Prejudice: the word "manage" (you manage something that is still there), with "discipline ourselves not to discriminate" putting the work on the act and not the opinion.
- CQ001-149-1, Why Understanding Beats Labeling People: the word "assigning" (a label is assigned from outside, like a sticker, where understanding can only come from the person), with "prioritized" read as an order and not a ban.
- CQ001-150-1, Why Normal Is Different for Everyone: the word "decided" (it says you took part in building your normal), set against "experienced" as the raw material.
- CQ001-151-1, Why People Judge You Less: the word "only" (the strongest word, and the one the page tests first), with "preferences" read as likes and dislikes, narrower than what someone notices or fears.
- CQ001-152-1, Why Understanding Builds Tolerance: the word "foundation" (laid first, out of sight, not the building), with "understanding" and "tolerance" taken before it.
- CQ001-153-1, Why Stress Is About Time: the word "equals" (changing one side must move the other), with "time" read as the time you believe is left and "limited space" read as the room you have to work in, marked as the page's own reading.
- CQ001-154-1, Why Trying to Prove Yourself Wrong Helps You Grow: the words "only when" and "go out of our way" (the claim is exclusive to one kind of growth, insight and understanding, and it will not happen by itself).
- CQ001-155-1, Why We Judge Others: "the things that they do and for the things that they say" (the visible part of a person, which is all a verdict has to go on), with "let us" and "stop" taken first.
- CQ001-156-1, Why We Put Others on Pedestals: "at the same time" (the lifting and the lowering are one act), with "unconsciously" (the second half goes unseen).
- CQ001-157-1, Why Judging Others Is Really a Defense Mechanism: "nothing more than" (a protective job is the whole story, which is stronger than the evidence).
- CQ001-158-1, What We Dislike About Other People: "attitude style" (a way of going about things, which is not a character word), with "the most" as the limit of the claim.
- CQ001-159-1, Why We Struggle to Collaborate: "adamantly" (the grip, not the preference, is what blocks building), with "never going to" as the strength of the claim.
- CQ001-160-1, Why We Feel Threatened by Others: the word "aren't trusting" (a thing you do, which can stop, not a trait you are), the word "tend", and the order the line assumes, trust first and threat second.
- CQ001-161-1, Why We Jump to Conclusions About People: "this process" (the quick business of deciding what someone's behaviour says about them) and the two things the quote asks for, "time or space", with "even" making the pause the smallest possible request.
- CQ001-162-1, Why Conformity Keeps Us Small: the word "resist" (you can only resist what you can feel pulling, so the first step is noticing) and the phrase "social obedience" read as doing what is expected without having decided, which is not the same as agreeing.
- CQ001-163-1, Why Authority Cannot Be Given Away: the word "non-transferable" (a key moves from hand to hand unchanged, while trust and respect have to form in the person who holds them) and "authority" as an agreement about whose word carries weight, which lives in minds as much as on paper.
- CQ001-164-1, Why You Stop Appreciating What You Expect: the word "never", read as a claim about expectation and not about the thing (you can appreciate the thing itself, but not as something you expect), then "appreciate" as an act of noticing that needs something to land on, and "expect" as the switch that stops treating a thing as news.
