## The script

If you're managing or leading other people day to day, it's not always realistic to escape off to a retreat every single time things get difficult at work. So how exactly do you stay grounded in the middle of a genuinely demanding week?

Over seven thousand eight hundred people have already taken this course, and it's rated 4.67 out of 5, because managers and leaders find it genuinely applies on a Monday morning, not just on retreat.

If you're ready to lead from a place of genuine self-awareness rather than just reacting to whatever the day throws at you, Mindfulness for Highly Efficient Management and Leadership is open right now, for you to start today.
