## The script

About fifteen years ago, I was genuinely homeless, broke, and living in someone else's flat on the other side of the world, with absolutely no real direction for my life at all. So when I talk to you about goal-setting, I promise you I'm not talking theory.

I'm Kain Ramsay, and I built this masterclass around a hard lesson I had to learn the difficult way: a goal that doesn't genuinely inspire you is really just hot air, and nothing more. We'll work through what actually makes a goal compelling, how to keep your life properly in balance while you pursue it, and how to adapt when things inevitably don't go to plan.

Over eight thousand six hundred people have already taken this course, and it's rated 4.67 out of 5, because people find these goals actually get them out of bed in the morning.

If you're ready to set goals that actually get you out of bed in the morning, The Strategic Goal Setting and Action Planning Masterclass is open right now, for you to start today.
