## The script

Have you ever known someone who spent years climbing a particular mountain in life, only to reach the very top and realise it was completely the wrong one? That's exactly what happens when we chase goals without ever getting clear on what we actually value first.

I'm Kain Ramsay, and this masterclass deliberately isn't a goal-setting course at all. It's everything that genuinely needs to happen before goal-setting actually works: understanding your values, your real priorities, and the gap between where you are today and where you're actually trying to go, so that when you finally do set goals, they're the right ones for you.

Over nine thousand six hundred people have already taken this course, and it's rated 4.79 out of 5, because people come away with real clarity, not just another list of goals.

If you're ready to get clear before you get moving, The Clarity, Purpose and Personal Effectiveness Masterclass is open right now, for you to start today.
