`qualities-of-a-true-leader.md` as a nearest-match guess, flagged as unconfirmed. This session,
`content_gate_standards.json`'s `shared.locked_tags` was read directly: 25 outcome-or-problem tags, 5

**2. A gate mechanic worth carrying into every future instructor-article: the H1 title counts toward
keyword density and can silently satisfy or break the "keyword in a subheading" and density checks
depending on whether it happens to contain the exact phrase.** Row 6's title happened to carry the keyword
and counted as a hit without being planned for; two other rows needed a heading rewritten mid-process because
a natural gerund form ("meeting" instead of "meet") looked like a match to a human reader but is not an
exact-string match to the gate. Worth a line in the `rank-math-90` or `instructor-article` skill so future
drafters plan for it rather than discover it by trial and error.

**3. A deliberate content boundary, applied consistently across all six and named rather than silently
decided.** Course 027 is An Essential Guide to Healthy Marriage and Long-Term Relationships, and several
lessons used as source material carry material considerably more personal than the brand's public marketing
content should repurpose: a miscarriage, a near-separation, a criminal record, mentions of other people's
infidelity and a friend's death by overdose, family religious detail. None of that appears in any of the six
records. Only the generalisable interpersonal principle each lesson teaches, and Karen's own stated
reflections on herself, were drawn on. This was Cowork's own editorial judgement, not put to Kain mid-flow,
on the reasoning that repurposing a specific vulnerable disclosure into unrelated SEO content is a different
decision from the couple's original choice to sell it as paid course material, and the boundary was
sufficiently obvious not to need a ruling. Flagged here so it is visible rather than invisible.
