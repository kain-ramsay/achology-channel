## The script

Have you ever caught yourself reacting to something in exactly the same way you always do, and then wondered afterwards why on earth you did that? Most of us go through life running on a kind of autopilot, following patterns we picked up so long ago we don't even notice them anymore. Neuro-linguistic programming is simply a way of bringing some structure and order to that thinking, so you can finally see what's actually driving you.

I'm Kain Ramsay, and I built this course as a starting point, not some technique to fix yourself or fix anyone else, because NLP isn't really a tangible thing you can hold in your hand. It's a body of ideas, drawn from applied psychology, that helps you understand the assumptions you're making about your own life, often completely without realising it. Once you can see those assumptions clearly, laid out in front of you, you get to decide for yourself whether they're actually serving you or quietly holding you back.

Over fourteen thousand people have already taken this course, and it's rated 4.58 out of 5, because it gives people a genuinely useful starting point, not just another list of techniques.

If you're ready to do a proper check-up from the neck up, A Beginner's Guide to Neuro-Linguistic Programming (NLP) is open right now, for you to start today.
