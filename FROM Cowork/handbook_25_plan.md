## Two swaps made, and why (flag to Kain)

Two of the original 31 shortlisted candidates were dropped after a live search, not because they fail DSRD 2's selection standard (both are verbatim, standalone, one-sentence, no em-dash, Kain's own words), but because each one closely echoes a famous quote attributed to someone else:

- "Discipline is the bridge that spans the gap between setting goals and achieving them." Nearly identical in structure and wording to Jim Rohn's well-known "Discipline is the bridge between goals and accomplishment" (Goodreads, BrainyQuote, Facebook/Jim Rohn official page all carry it verbatim).
- "The secret of getting ahead in life is simply getting started." Nearly identical to the line widely (if disputedly) attributed to Mark Twain, "The secret of getting ahead is getting started" (Quote Investigator, BrainyQuote, Goodreads).

Both would risk a reader, or an AI answer engine, flagging an Achology "Kain Ramsay quote" page as unoriginal on sight. Swapped for two other verified, non-colliding candidates (now Q07033 and Q07036 above). This is a call taken, not asked, because the standard for the swap is mechanical rather than a matter of taste. Open to restoring either original line if Kain would rather keep it and accept the resemblance.

## Duplicates avoided (same manuscript paragraph, only one used)

- "Discipline is the bridge..." / "Discipline is choosing between what you want now and what you want most." (both line 314, same paragraph) — moot now the first is swapped out.
- "A life coaching breakthrough session is about..." / "The purpose of a breakthrough session is to help facilitate..." (lines 840/836, same subsection) — kept 840, dropped 836.
- "Vision is always future oriented..." / "Vision, in preparation for taking action, is as important..." (lines 1685/1686, consecutive) — kept 1685, dropped 1686.

## Lesson learned on keyword length (matters for Q07035 onward)

A quote-page keyword longer than about 5 words cannot pass the density band (1.0 to 1.5 percent) at a 650 to 825 word body, because the fixed structure quotes the line twice (opening paragraph, provenance paragraph), which alone supplies 2 verbatim hits. Keep each remaining keyword to 4 to 6 words, and make sure the record's post_name/address slug contains the same words as rm_focus_keyword (the gate checks both match).
