## The script

Why is it that some people bounce back from a setback within a day or two, while other people carry the very same setback with them for years afterwards? Cognitive behavioural therapy gives us one of the most useful frameworks there is for understanding that difference, both in ourselves and in everyone around us.

I'm Kain Ramsay, and this practitioner course takes you from the foundational thinkers, Aaron Beck and Albert Ellis, right through self-awareness, limiting beliefs, and core values, and on into practical communication and goal-setting skills you can start using immediately. It comes from real teaching experience built up over many years, not a textbook written for the sake of it.

Over one hundred and sixty-nine thousand people have already taken this course, and it's rated 4.58 out of 5, not because the course is easy, but because it gives people a framework that actually holds up in real life.

If you're ready to properly understand why people, including yourself, do the things that they do, the Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) Practitioner Course is open right now, for you to start today.
