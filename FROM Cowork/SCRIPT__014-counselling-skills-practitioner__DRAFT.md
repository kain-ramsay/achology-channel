## The script

There's an old saying that's always worth sitting with: hurt people hurt people. Almost nobody ever comes looking for a counsellor without having felt judged, dismissed, or rejected somewhere along the way in their life.

I'm Kain Ramsay, and this course is built around real people skills, real listening skills, and a genuine appreciation for difference, not academic theory delivered for its own sake. Alongside Karen, I demonstrate the entire counselling process live, including seven full hours working with two real clients and their real concerns, so you see exactly how it's done, not just hear about it secondhand.

Over forty thousand people have already taken this course, and it's rated 4.79 out of 5, because people finish it able to actually listen, not just talk about listening.

If you're ready to learn how to truly listen to people, the Counselling Skills Practitioner Course (Beginner to Advanced) is open right now, for you to start today.
