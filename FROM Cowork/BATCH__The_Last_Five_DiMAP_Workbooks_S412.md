## The Reflect questions, for Kain to read

**Transference and Countertransference Workbook**

1. Recall someone you disliked or distrusted within moments of meeting them, and who later turned out to be quite different from your first impression. What did you first notice about them, and what changed your mind about the kind of person they were?
2. Think of a time when someone reacted to you with far more emotion than the situation seemed to call for. How did their reaction change what you said or did, and what did you want from that person at that particular point in the conversation?
3. Recall a time when you listened to someone's difficulty and found your own story rising in you as they spoke. Whose story did you end up responding to, and what do you wish you had done instead in that relationship?
4. When have you felt pulled to rescue, calm or take charge of someone who was upset with you or with their situation? What did you want for yourself in that moment, and what did the pull ask you to forget?

**Who Am I Workbook**

1. Think of a time when a job, a role or a whole season of your life came to an end, such as leaving a team or finishing a course. What did you find yourself saying about who you were afterwards, and what, if anything, still felt true of you underneath?
2. Recall one particular word that you or somebody else used to describe you over an extended period, such as clever, shy or reliable. What did that description make easier for you, and what did it discourage you from attempting?
3. Think of a day when an achievement or a compliment raised your opinion of yourself above its usual level. For how long did that feeling last, and what happened to it the next time something went less well?
4. Consider who in your life treats you as more than your achievements or your emotional state. What do they appreciate in you that you tend to overlook, and when did you last allow yourself to notice it?

**Stages of Human Connection Workbook**

1. Think of a relationship, with a friend or a relative, that progressed more quickly than the trust underneath it. What happened when the closeness developed ahead of the trust, and what would you have wanted the relationship to be like instead?
2. Recall someone whose first impression of you changed as they gradually came to know you better. What did they discover about you that you had not expected, and how did it change the way you behaved with them afterwards?
3. Think of a time when you made peace with something about another person that had troubled you for a considerable time. What did you accept, and what changed afterwards, either in them or in you?
4. Which of the eight stages do you find most natural in your own relationships, and which do you tend to rush through or avoid altogether? What do you think you are protecting yourself from by doing so?

**Six Domains of Self-Concept Workbook**

1. Remember a time when you felt entirely comfortable among other people, and another when you felt out of place. What distinguished the two situations, and what does that difference suggest about how you see yourself in company?
2. Recall something you once found difficult to learn or manage, and how you saw yourself after you had succeeded. What did that experience change about what you believed you were capable of?
3. Think of a moment when a strong feeling rose in you and you noticed it before acting on it. What did you notice, and what made it possible for you to notice it in time?
4. Of the six areas, which can you picture most clearly as a future, and which is hardest to imagine? What do you think accounts for the difference?

**Human Givens Emotional Needs Workbook**

1. When did you last feel settled and well in yourself, even briefly? Which of the nine needs were being met then, and which people or circumstances were helping you meet them?
2. Which of the nine needs do you meet most easily on your own, and which can you meet only through other people? What does that pattern show about where you've put your effort over the years?
3. When did you last give another person your complete attention and expect nothing in return? What changed in the conversation, and what changed in you afterwards?
4. What did you last make, mend or finish that you were glad to have done? Which of the nine needs did it feed, and did anyone notice?
