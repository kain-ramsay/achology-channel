## What the off-topic material is (same few things in nearly every answer)
1. Achology's definition of a practitioner and the quality-of-life teaching.
2. The goals and positive-outlook teaching.
3. A tour of lesson titles (mind, emotions, attitude, perception, goals, helping).
4. The "not a diagnosis, not a licence, not a substitute for a doctor" lines.
5. The crisis-line note.
6. Course facts repeated (130 lessons, 38+ hours, seven sections).
7. A scene or "picture someone..." opener, and a "try it on yourself" closer.

## Cause
Items 1 to 6 are the rules written into the drafting briefs after Kain's S393 rulings (carry his definition in every answer; Achology-led; stance lines; 650-word floor). The 650-word floor with a short on-topic answer is what pushes the rest in as padding. This is a conflict between those rules and relevance, and it is Kain's call, not a drafter fault.
