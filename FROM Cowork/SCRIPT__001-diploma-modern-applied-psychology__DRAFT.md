## The script

Why do you do the things you do? Are you simply born that way, or is it what life has made of you? That's the nature-nurture debate, and it's driven psychology for two hundred years.

I'm Kain Ramsay. For years, I've taught applied psychology to people who want more than theory copied from a textbook, and I built this course to be different from the psychology courses you might have sat through before.

Most of us meet a new idea, hold it up against what we already believe, and simply decide whether we agree or disagree. It feels like thinking, but it isn't, because nothing changes. You finish exactly where you started. This course asks something harder of you. Every idea you meet here gets linked to the next, until you're not just collecting information, you're building a bigger picture, one that stretches how you see things rather than just confirming it.

That's the real difference. This isn't about being able to recite theories at a dinner party. It's about seeing your own life, and the people in it, more clearly than you did before, because you let the ideas actually change how you think rather than just adding to what you already knew.

Over fifty-six thousand people have already taken this diploma. It's rated 4.78 out of 5 by more than ten thousand of them, not because it's easy, but because it does exactly that.

If you're ready to stop guessing why people, including yourself, do what they do, the Diploma Course in Modern Applied Psychology is open for you to start today.
