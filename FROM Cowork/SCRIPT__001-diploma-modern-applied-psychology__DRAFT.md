## The script

Why do you think the way you think, and do the things that you do? Are you simply born one predetermined way without any ability to change or become different? Or is who you are just a consequence of what your upbringing, society, and life, in general, have made you? That's the nature-nurture debate, and it's driven psychology for well over two hundred years now.

I'm Kain Ramsay, and I built this course because I wanted it to work differently to most of the psychology courses out there. Most courses just hand you theories and expect you to remember them. This one doesn't do that. Every idea you come across gets linked to the next, until, before too long, you're not just collecting information, you're building a much bigger picture, and that's a picture that helps you understand yourself, and the people around you, a whole lot better.

Over fifty-six thousand people, and counting, have already taken this diploma, and it's currently rated 4.78 out of 5, not because it's easy, but because it genuinely changes how people think, not just what they happen to know.

So if you're ready to stop guessing why people, and that includes yourself, do the things that they do, the Diploma Course in Modern Applied Psychology is open right now, for you to start today.
