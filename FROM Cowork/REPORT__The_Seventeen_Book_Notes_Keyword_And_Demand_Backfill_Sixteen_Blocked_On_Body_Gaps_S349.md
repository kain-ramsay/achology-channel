## Why sixteen of the seventeen still show GATE: FAIL

Two checks are left failing, and both are body content, not metadata:

**Keyword verbatim in the first 10% of the body**, fails on 15 of 17. Checked born-for-love directly rather than assuming: its opening section never states the book's title at all. This is what today's keyword swap exposes, not what it breaks. Job 1 is explicit that nothing but the keyword field moves, not the body, so no body text has been touched on any of the seventeen.

**External link to the source, 0 found**, fails on all 17 except have-a-little-faith. Same story: a citation gap in the body, outside what this brief authorises.

have-a-little-faith is the one clean PASS. It already carried both.

**yes-50-scientifically-proven-ways-to-be-persuasive carries a third, different problem.** Its title, and so its keyword, is 51 characters. DSRD 6 sets the keyword to the title exactly. RULING S345 requires the keyword to sit inside the SEO title's first 50 characters. A 51-character keyword cannot satisfy both rules at once, whatever surrounds it. Nothing written here closes this; it is a straight conflict between two standing rules, not a gap in this record.

**One more flag, not a failure.** multiple-intelligences-new-horizons and shyness-what-it-is-what-to-do-about-it both carry a post_title shorter than the book's actual full title (Multiple Intelligences: New Horizons in Theory and Practice; Shyness: What It Is, What to Do About It). Job 1 says set the keyword to post_title exactly, done, but both bare short forms are genuinely ambiguous: "Multiple Intelligences" returns Gardner's earlier, different book and the general theory ahead of this one in live search, and "Shyness" is a generic term this site may want for other content later. Named per the brief's own instruction rather than a longer phrase invented here.
