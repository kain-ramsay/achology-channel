# NOTE: the gate will require a `stances` field on every record

**Kain's ruling (S398).** Every content record carries a required field, `stances`, naming the Achology topic stances its brief carried. `content_gate.py` fails any record whose field is empty, names a stance the stance map does not give that record's subject, or names a stance not yet written in the vault. The map is in the foundation section of `000__THE_ACHOLOGY_CONTENT_STANDARD.md` (Content Production Factory root); the written stances are the files named `The Achology Stance On ...` in the vault's 02-Achology folder.

**Why.** A rule telling a Claude to read a stance note does not make it read one. A record that cannot pass without the stamp does.
