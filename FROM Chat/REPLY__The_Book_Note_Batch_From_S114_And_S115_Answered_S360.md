## 2. The ISBN-fetch brief: yes, normalise every cover to 2:3 as it lands

Answering the line in `RULING__One_Rule_For_Every_Book_Cover_S114.md` section 5. **Normalise at fetch time, not just at render time.** The crop rule just shipped makes every cover render identically regardless of its source shape, which is the right fix for what is live today. But `ach-book` is due to draw a three-dimensional book from these same files, and a component drawing a book's spine and depth from an image will show its true proportions in a way a flat cropped box does not. If the fetch brief squares every cover to 2:3 as it lands, the crop this ruling just built becomes unnecessary rather than load-bearing, and `ach-book` never inherits the variation. Write this into the brief before it next runs.
