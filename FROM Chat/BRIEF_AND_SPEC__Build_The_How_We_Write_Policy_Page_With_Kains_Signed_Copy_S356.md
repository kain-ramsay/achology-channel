# How we write

Every page on Achology.com is written for a person who arrived with a question, and every page is owned by our in-house editorial team. This page explains how the writing is done, so you can judge it for yourself.

## Everything we publish comes from what we teach

Every article Achology publishes is based on, and reflects, what we actually teach. The articles, the quotes, the ideas and the books we write about are all drawn from our training curriculum, built between 2012 and 2026: more than 1,000 hours of pre-recorded video training, across 2,146 videos in our current courses and 766 from earlier versions of them. Nothing here is made up to attract internet traffic. We use AI tools to help draft and edit that teaching into readable articles. Every article is approved by our in-house editorial team, to a standard our management team governs, before it is published.

We use those tools the way a publisher uses an assistant editor: to help shape and check the writing. The sources are always our own courses. We do not use them to publish at volume without a person reading what goes out.

## Our editorial team approves every page

No page is published because a machine finished it. Each piece is drafted to a written standard, checked by an automated gate for accuracy, sources and readability, and then read and approved by our in-house editorial team before it goes live. The Achology management team governs and decides the standard every page is written to, and Achology's in-house editorial team approves all written copy, editing and amending it where necessary.

## Facts are sourced, and figures belong to their owners

Where a page makes a claim, the source sits beside it. Course facts, prices and accreditation details are read from the pages that own them rather than retyped, so a number on an article is never out of step with the number at the checkout. Where a page names a study, a book or an organisation, it links to it.

## Two things we do not do

We do not publish content that no person has read. And we do not try to remove the invisible watermark that AI writing tools now place in the text they help produce; it is there so that anyone can ask how a piece of writing was made, and we would rather answer that question than hide it.

## Who approves what you read

Our written copy is prepared with the help of AI and approved by Achology's senior management team, so that it accurately reflects our policies, our learning processes, our accreditation practices and our organisational standards.

## If something on a page is wrong

Tell us. Every page can be corrected, and a correction is read and approved the same way the page was. Use the [enquiries](/enquiries/) page and name the page and the line.
