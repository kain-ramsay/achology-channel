## The ruling

1. **The quote page body ends on a third H2, "A Question Worthy of an Honest Answer".** Under it: one short paragraph of light, reflective context, then the reflection question as the page's last sentence. The question is body copy, not a component.
2. **The S355 `reflection_question` field is withdrawn for the quote page**, and with it the cream card at the foot that was to render it (the S303 ruling on the cream card is superseded by this one). Nothing at the foot after the body except the template's own blocks. If the column is already in the contract, strike it and say so in one line; if it was never built, nothing to do.
3. **The other two rulings from the same sitting, so the three travel together.** The H1 takes title case, every word capitalised. There is no pull-out quote block: the quote is carried inside one opening paragraph together with the sentence that introduces it, set in the larger opening type the article and book note pages use, directly under the H1. Whether that paragraph travels as its own field or as the body's first paragraph is yours to settle with Kain in the sitting; the words are the same either way.

## Why (so nobody re-derives it)

Kain read the drafted quote page and ruled the whole frame too cryptic for a first-time visitor. Every quote page now reads: title, the opening paragraph with the quote in it, his provenance paragraph ("This quote by [author] was taken from his book..."), then three headings: What the Quote Might Be Saying, What Can We Take Away From It?, A Question Worthy of an Honest Answer. The exemplar is being rewritten to that shape in the Chat sitting; the standard follows into DSRD 2 sections 1.1 and 3.3 and the quote-page skill once he approves it, and DSRD 10 loses the field at the same pass.

## What this changes for you now

Only the build in front of you: no cream card, and the body's last block is an H2 with prose under it. Nothing else in this file is a commission, except the one line below.
