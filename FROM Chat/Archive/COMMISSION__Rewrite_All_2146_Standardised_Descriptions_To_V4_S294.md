## What V4 changes, so you can see the size of it before you start

**1. Four parts, not three.** Question line, paragraph, paragraph, closing line naming what the lesson covers.

**2. No word ceiling.** V3's 90 to 120 band is gone. Kain's own example runs about 180 and the three rows he approved run 157, 168 and 185.

**3. No banned phrase list.** "In this video", "In this lesson" and "Kain discusses" are all unbanned. The ban was aimed at the wrong target: the fault in "Kain discusses several important concepts" is that it says nothing, not that it contains a phrase. V4 replaces the list with a test the closing line must pass, and the test is in the document.

**4. The teacher is never named.** New and absolute. No "Kain discusses", no "the lecturer explains", no "your instructor walks through". **This retires V3's speaker rule and its worked example**, which told you to write things like `Kain puts the question to Ian Blackie`. A named guest, interviewee or case subject is still named in full, because they are part of what the lesson covers. The person delivering the lecture is not.

**Plus a rule V3 half had and V4 makes explicit:** full names at first mention, for people and for concepts alike. Carl Rogers, not Rogers. Neuro-Linguistic Programming before NLP.
