That is two copies of one truth disagreeing, which is the exact drift the channel exists to remove.

It also puts the map on the wrong side of the harness. The Chat Harness grades a session by reading durable artefacts in the folder rather than believing a message. A stale map makes the folder a less reliable grader than it should be.
