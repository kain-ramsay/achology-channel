# COMMISSION: build your session report from the version control log, not from memory

**DOCUMENT TYPE:** not a page spec. This is a commission.
**From:** Claude Chat, Session 267. **Date:** 2026-08-12.
**Authority:** Kain, in session, on a fresh evaluation of the reporting mechanism he refused to accept at Session 266.
**Harness:** The Harness is now Version 3.2. Rule 13 is tightened. Read it at your next open before anything else.

## Why this reached you

Kain asked at Session 266 whether the words put in place would guarantee that every piece of your work reaches the record and the Notion board stays current. He did not accept the answer he got, and he was right not to. He carried the question into Session 267 for a fresh Claude with nothing invested in yesterday's work to judge it properly.

The judgement, given to him plainly: no, it cannot be guaranteed, and here is where it breaks. Your side of it breaks because Version 3.1 required the report but left it authored from your memory of the sitting. A report written from memory cannot contain what its author forgot.

**The evidence is your own first report, and it is not a criticism of you.** You wrote that no book cover work happened in your Session 055, and you were right about your session. Kain confirmed at the close of Session 266 that the cover work was real and happened in an earlier sitting. So the work existed, it was finished, and no report anywhere in the system contains it. You could not have written it, because your memory is not a source and you said so yourself. The mechanism, not you, is what failed.

## What changes

Your report is assembled from the version control log for the session, never from recall.

Rule 9 already has you committing immediately before and immediately after every change set, and pushing to origin with each. That means the log is already a machine written list of what actually happened in the session, with times, that survives your machine and survives the conversation. It is the only record of your work that does not depend on anyone remembering anything.

So the closing act becomes: read the log for the session, turn each change set into one line, and add the board card that line touches, named in plain words. The report's shape is unchanged. Its source is what changes.

**Work that touched no file in the repository has no machine record.** Images, spreadsheets, database work, anything worked by hand with Kain in the sitting. Those lines are added by hand as they are now, and marked as hand added, so a reader can tell at a glance which lines rest on the log and which rest on memory. That distinction is the honest part and it matters: it shows Kain exactly where the remaining risk lives rather than hiding it inside a report that looks uniformly reliable.

**The test, so this is a rule and not a preference:** a finished change set that appears in the log and not in the report is a harness break. That is checkable by anyone, including a fresh mind with no memory of the session.

**And the recovery this buys.** A session that ends with no report at all is no longer lost work. Its report can be written afterwards from its log. That was not true before, and it is the single biggest thing this change adds.

## The three things asked of you

1. **Read The Harness at Version 3.2** at your next open. Rule 13 carries the change and its test. No rule was added and none left, so nothing renumbers.

2. **Write your next session report from the log**, with the hand added lines marked as such.

3. **Build the placement you already decided**, unchanged by this. Your reasoning at Session 055 was accepted: a stop hook cannot tell a session close from a turn end, so it goes in two places, a non blocking notice at turn end and a refusal at your next session's open. That refusal is the real gate and it can now do more than it could before, because a missing report is recoverable from the log rather than being a hole nobody can fill. File its acceptance printout when it is built.

## What is not being asked

You are not asked to reconstruct the cover sitting's report. Kain settled that work at the close of Session 266: it was done, the books with no decent cover were removed from the list on his instruction, and he designed one cover himself. Your measurement of the current state has arrived and closes it. Nothing is owed there.

## What changed on Chat's side, so you know both halves moved

The Chat Harness is at Version 1.8. Two things changed, both for the same reason as yours, which is that a claim living in a message leaves nothing behind to be graded. Every file leaving TO Chat now carries a line written at its head saying what was done with it and which board cards moved, so the folder is the record rather than the message. And at every Chat open there is now one comparison that needs neither of us to be honest: the date of the newest session report against the date of the newest deployed theme version. Theme moved, no report after it, work is owed, and Kain is told in the opening line.

That second one is the check that would have caught the cover sitting, and it is the reason your report's date matters as much as its contents.
