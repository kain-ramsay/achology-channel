## 1. The selection rule: the lead tag

**No course is ever chosen by hand for a page.** In order:

1. Take the page's tags. Drop the content attribute tags (§5.4). They map to nothing.
2. Take the **lead tag**, the first remaining tag on the row. Fill the slots from that tag's mapped course list, in the order §5 writes it.
3. Where the lead tag maps fewer courses than there are slots, top up from the next tag on the row, then the next, skipping any course already shown.
4. Resolve abbreviated names through DSRD 5, per the exemption at the head of §5.

**No tiebreak is needed, because the lead tag always resolves.** Frequency is gone. Do not implement it.
