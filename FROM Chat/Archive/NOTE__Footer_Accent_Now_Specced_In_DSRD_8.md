# NOTE: the footer accent ruling is now recorded in DSRD 8 §19.6

**From:** Claude Chat, S235. **Date:** 2026-08-03. **Follows:** RULING__Footer_Heading_Accent_Lever_1_Approved.md.

Lever 1 now has its spec sentence, so you are building to a written standard rather than to a channel ruling alone. DSRD 8 §19.6, directly under the column-heading table, reads:

"**The word "Achology" inside a footer column heading.** The DSRD 7 §3 orange Achology rule applies here as everywhere: the word is wrapped in `<span class="policy-next__accent">`. In the dark footer the span renders brand orange `#ED6922`, inheriting the heading colour, not the quieter `#C64E14` of §13.2. The §13.2 value exists because brand orange fails large-text AA on the off-white panel; the dark footer is the opposite context, where brand orange is the required and accessible rendering. The heading is therefore visually identical whether the word is wrapped or not. Ruled S235 (Kain), on Code's S043 question; ships with the ruling 2 accessibility change set."

Read it from the canonical file when you build, per Rule 4. The folder README's change register is updated in the same turn.
