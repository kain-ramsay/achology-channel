## The rule

Every page on Achology.com is written for a real human being who arrived with a question, a worry or a hope. It exists to be helpful, approachable and informative to that person. The gates you run (`content_gate.py`, the Rank Math bar, `publish_gate.py`, DSRD 6) are the floor: every page meets them because they catch real faults. But a page that meets every gate and reads like it was assembled to meet them has failed, and a score does not make it right.

## What this means on your side

- A score is evidence a page cleared the floor, never evidence it is good. Do not report a page as ready on its number alone; the number and the DSRD 6 record together are the claim.
- Never lift a score by editing words. You do not draft, and you do not tune copy to a checklist. Where a page is short of the bar, name the failing tests and hand it back; the drafter finds the natural sentence, or names the conflict.
- When you read pages back off the install for verification, read them as a visitor would. If a passage reads as machine text (a keyword where no person would put it, a heading bent to carry a phrase, a paragraph cut at a count), name it in your report even though every gate passed. Kain reads the live pages, and that is what he will see.
- Where a gate rule and a good reader experience genuinely conflict, the reader wins and the conflict goes to Chat to rule, never quietly to the gate.
