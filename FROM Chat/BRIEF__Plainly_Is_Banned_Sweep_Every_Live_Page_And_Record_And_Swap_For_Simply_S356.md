## 2. What is asked

**Find every "plainly" a reader can see or a record carries, and remove it.**

Scope, all of it:
1. Every published page body on the install, every post type: Knowledge Hub articles, book notes, quotes, workbooks, the 250 help answers, the site pages (about, course, school, policy, every other). Titles, excerpts, SEO titles and SEO descriptions too, since a reader sees those in a search result.
2. Every record in `Content Records/` (all six folders), body and fields alike, so the record and the install stay one truth. The record is the source (S315 ruling); where a record and its live page both carry the word, both move.
3. Match on the whole word, case-insensitive. "Plain" and "plainly-spoken" are not in scope; "plainly" is.

**The swap rule, and it is not a blind find-and-replace.**
- Where "simply" keeps the sentence's meaning ("put plainly" becomes "put simply", "plainly, this is" becomes "simply, this is"), swap it.
- Where it does not, reword the sentence. Two shapes to watch: "said plainly" or "say so plainly" meaning frankly, and "plainly wrong" or "plainly visible" meaning obviously. "Simply wrong" says something different. Rewrite those so the meaning holds without the word.
- A swap or a reword never changes a focus keyword, an SEO title's keyword position, or a link. If one would, stop on that page and name it in the report rather than forcing it.

**Push both ways.** Records first, then the install, so a re-import never brings the word back.
