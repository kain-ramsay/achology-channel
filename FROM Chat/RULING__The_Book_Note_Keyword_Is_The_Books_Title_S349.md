## The decision

**The focus keyword on a book note is the book's title, not `{book} book summary`.**

It is not a new rule. **DSRD 6 section 5 item 11 already says it**, in the by-type list: "Book note: the book's title." The records carrying `{book} book summary` are the ones out of step, and they have been since before the title pattern was ruled.

**Every failing line you measured is that one mismatch.** Take `born-for-love`. Keyword `born for love book summary`. Address `/learn/psychology/book-notes/born-for-love/`. SEO title `Born for Love by Bruce Perry: Book Notes | Achology`. Alt `Book cover of Born for Love by Bruce D. Perry and Maia Szalavitz`. Opening sentence names the book. **The book's title is in all four. The phrase with "book summary" bolted on is in none of them.** Change the keyword to `Born for Love` and the address check, the alt check, the title check and the opening check all pass without a word of content moving.

**Nothing about the page changes.** Not the title Kain ruled at S102, not the address, not the body, not the cover. The focus keyword is an internal field; this is the record being corrected to the standard, not the page being rewritten to suit a plugin.

**And it should raise scores rather than risk them.** The live notes reach 85 to 88 today despite the mismatch. A keyword that sits in the address, the title, the alt and the opening is what those checks are looking for.
