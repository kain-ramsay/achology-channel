## Why it is being asked

Two board cards state the size of the component estate and they disagree.

**The Cards and Chrome Sweep card says:** the theme holds 37 components, twelve carry a prototype Kain approved by eye, and 25 do not. Its purpose paragraph and its definition of done are both built on that 25.

**The Component Truth System card says**, from your S267 census of 304 class families: 78 are library components, and **zero** of the 78 carried a prototype at the census.

37 against 78. Twelve approved against none. These are two counts of the same thing, written months apart, and nobody ever made them agree.

**What it is costing.** The Cards and Chrome Sweep card is the gate in front of the Article Page, the other ten Knowledge Hub pages, and the fourteen commercial page templates. Its number is the number the whole remaining design queue is sized against, and it cannot be trusted. Chat will not patch the card's wording on a figure nobody believes, so the card stays as it is until you answer.

## What is asked

1. **How many components does the theme hold today**, on the definition the component registry uses, at the current deployed version.
2. **How many of those carry a prototype Kain has approved by eye**, filed in the component's design folder with its build sheet beside it.
3. **How many therefore still owe one**, which is the real remaining queue.
4. **Where the 37 and the 78 came from**, if you can see it: whether they count different things (library components against something narrower, say), or whether one is simply stale. A one-line answer is enough; Chat does not need the history.
5. **Whether the registry can produce these three numbers on demand**, so the board carries a pointer to a live count rather than a figure that goes stale the moment a component lands.

**Answer the numbers only.** No card is to be rewritten by you and no component work is commissioned here. Chat rebuilds the board card on your figures once they arrive.
