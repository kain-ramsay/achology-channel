## The ruling

**The Cowork commission for the sixty five published book notes changes shape. It is a review of sixty five focus keywords that already exist, not sixty five pieces of writing from nothing.**

Kain's S096 ruling that book notes come first is untouched and still orders the work. What changes is what the work is.

## Why, in one paragraph

Every one of the sixty five carries a distinct focus keyword, SEO title and search description, written by Cowork at drafting and held in `Book_Note_Master.xlsx` ever since. They never reached the install because the importer's meta map threw them away until S087, and the sixty five were imported before that fix and never re-imported. They are on the install as of tonight. So the writing was done. What was never done is the judgement.

## The size of the review, measured rather than estimated

Every one of the sixty five keywords was tested against its own book's title. **Sixty two are the title plus a suffix and nothing else**: fifty six are `{title} book summary` or a close variant, and six add the author's name. The three the test set aside as carrying a real choice turn out on reading to be the title with its subtitle or its author attached, which is the same thing.

**So the honest figure is sixty five of sixty five, and not one keyword in the set was chosen against demand.** The test can only read shape; whether a phrase has demand behind it is stage 0's live check and is Cowork's, which is exactly why the review is needed rather than avoidable.

**What that buys, and it is the reason the job is still smaller than a write.** The failure is uniform. It is one shape repeated sixty five times, not sixty five separate problems, so the brief can carry one instruction and one worked example rather than sixty five briefs. And every piece starts from a real title, a real book and a real record instead of a blank page.

## What Chat writes into the brief

1. Run stage 0 on each of the sixty five, seed phrase the book's title and author, exactly as `BRIEF__Rewrite_The_Eighty_Earning_Books_As_Book_Notes_S319` already words it.
2. Keep the existing keyword where the evidence supports it, and say so in the record rather than leaving silence, so a kept keyword is a decision rather than an omission.
3. Replace it where the evidence does not, and claim the new one in `KEYWORD_REGISTER.csv`.
4. Do not retitle a book note away from its book. That rule already stands and this ruling does not touch it.
