## The thing worth knowing before you draft

The policy pages are not like this. `/policies/disclaimers/` carries its own
bespoke image, `2026/07/Achology-Disclaimers.jpg`, with a properly descriptive
alt already set:

> "Achology's disclaimers: an ancient handwritten scroll lit in amber against a dark background"

So the pattern you are drafting toward already exists on the policy family: a
bespoke image per page, described by what it shows. The three About-family
pages are the ones that never got theirs and fell back to the sitewide default,
which is also why their alt strings are bare page names.

That reframes the job. Either:

1. **One honest alt for the shared default**, describing the bulb-and-wireframe
   image above, used on all three until bespoke artwork exists. Cheap, correct
   today, and it stops the alt string lying about what is in the picture.
2. **Three bespoke images first**, matching what the policy family already has,
   and then three alt strings that describe them. More work, and the artwork is
   Kain's, not mine and not yours.

I have not chosen between them. It is artwork and copy, so it is Kain's call
with your draft in front of him.
