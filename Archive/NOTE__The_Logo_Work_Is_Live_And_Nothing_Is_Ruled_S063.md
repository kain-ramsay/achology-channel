## Where it has got to, so your handover is accurate

1. **Audited against the standards first, because he asked whether they even met them.** Three findings, all measured off the artwork. The KNOW block starts at #E96C22, brand orange to within rounding, then drifts lighter across itself, so it is a gradient where the token is flat. The YOUR block is a flat **#666666**, a pure neutral, and every grey in DSRD 7 section 1 is blue-leaning: that block has never been on our palette at all. White lettering on all seven school colours passes the bar that applies at that size.

2. **Rebuilt from the vector master.** `Know_Your_Psychology_Graphic.ai` turns out to be a nine-page PDF: the two generics and the seven school versions. They are rendered at 3x rather than edited as 2023 pixels, so the edges are drawn fresh.

3. **The school block now runs its secondary into its primary.** My first attempt hung the artwork's own shading around the primary, which was arithmetic of mine and met no standard. DSRD 7 section 2 gives every school a secondary that was sitting unused, so both ends are now approved values.

4. **The YOUR block is on soft grey #5E6B75**, which is Kain's ruling in session, replacing seven per-school tinted greys I had offered him. It is the only one of the five palette greys that works: the hairline and mid grey are too light to carry white lettering, brand dark would make a second dark slab, and soft grey is also nearest to what was there.

5. **All three blocks now share one gradient**, on his instruction, because one graded block beside two flats did not read as a family. The axis is the artwork's own, measured: purely left to right, darker left, no vertical component at all. Orange hover into brand orange, brand dark into soft grey, secondary into primary.

6. **The bulb that stands in for the O is lit**, again on his instruction: a white crown running down into a warm tone chosen per school. The scale is fixed to the orange-to-yellow band and the pick is arithmetic, being the tone furthest in hue from that school's own block, with a legibility floor that overrides it where a cool block would otherwise get a bulb too dark to read as lit.
