## What changed

Kain gave the wording directly in session and it is live at v0.42.5.

**Was, from the signed plan section 4:**

> "4,517 written reviews since 2014, from students in 216 countries. Every one
> of them real, including the critical ones."

**Is now, Kain's own words**, which he revised twice within the same session.
This is the third and final version, live at v0.42.7:

> "Since 2014, we've gathered thousands of reviews across our various courses
> from students in 216 countries. All reviews are real, including the handful of
> negative ones. This should help you decide whether studying with Achology is

No drafting was done by me. He typed all three, I placed them. The apostrophe is
set as `&rsquo;` to match the About hero, which is the same slot on the same
frame; that is typesetting, not a change to his words.

Two things changed across the three versions and both are worth the record.

**The lead gained a job.** The closing sentence tells the reader what the page
is for. Neither the plan's line nor the first revision did that, and it is the
single biggest improvement to the block.

**A factual seam opened and then closed.** The second version said "tens of
thousands of reviews" while the figures panel one screen below, inside the same
component, says 4,517 written reviews. That is two answers to one question on a
page whose entire claim is honesty. I reported it to Kain rather than editing
his words, and he settled it in the copy: "thousands" agrees with 4,517.

That is worth recording as a pattern rather than an incident. The hero and the
figures are the same component making the same claim at two scales, so a copy
change in one can contradict a specified figure in the other without anybody
touching the figure. Whatever the new build sheet ends up carrying for this
component, the review count is a value the sheet should hold once, so the two
places that state it cannot drift apart.

## The one thing worth checking, and it is not a copy question

**The page now says two different things about how many reviews there are.**

The hero says "tens of thousands of reviews". The figures panel a screen below,
inside the same component, says **4,517 written reviews**, which is the number
DSRD 4 and the plan both specify and which matches the 4,517 rows imported into
WordPress.

Both can be true of different things. DSRD 4 section 14.2 Variant 3 carries the
trust line "4.66 stars from 175,162 ratings", so tens of thousands is
comfortably right for **ratings**, and 4,517 is right for **written reviews**.
The hero does not make that distinction, and a reader who meets "tens of
thousands" and then "4,517 written reviews" on one screen has been given two
answers to one question.

This is Kain's call and I have not touched his words. But it is the kind of
thing the page's own claim of honesty is most exposed to, so it should be a
deliberate decision rather than something nobody noticed. Two obvious ways
through, both his: name the distinction in the hero (ratings versus written
reviews), or let the figure panel carry the larger ratings number alongside the
4,517.
