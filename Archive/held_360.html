So how do you get to Master Achologist? By progressing through all six statuses of the continuing professional development (CPD) pathway, from Achology Member through Certified Achologist, Achologist Adept, Senior Achologist and Senior Achologist (II), to Master Achologist, meeting each level's verified requirements in turn. Levels cannot be skipped, the totals are cumulative across the journey, and the pathway is self-paced: it is a long-term professional commitment, not a weekend certification.

What Level 5 requires

The Master Achologist level asks for, cumulatively: five Practitioner Certification Courses, which must include The Skilled Helper Practitioner Course (Advanced to Expert), plus three School of Personal Growth and Development courses; 50 Virtual Achologist Led Training Sessions (VALTS) across all six VALT types, attending 30 and hosting or co-hosting 20; 50 Peer-Peer Applied Learning Sessions (PALS) delivered as coach, of which 8 must come from the alternative types (the HOT Seat as coach, the Live Coaching Practice session as coach, or the Achologist-Led Tutorial as host); 30 one-to-one Focused Practice Sessions received as client; and 50 Competency Improvement Practice Sessions (CIPS), real coaching sessions offered to people outside the community, with a maximum of six counting from any one individual. Alongside these sit the compliance strands: the live Code of Ethics Training annually, and 8 or more of the 10 Code of Character and Conduct virtue sessions current within any rolling 12 months.

The status is maintained, not kept

Master Achologist is not a permanent designation. From 1 January of the first full calendar year after qualifying, annual maintenance applies: hosting a minimum of ten VALTS and eight PALS-as-coach sessions across the named formats, receiving six one-to-one sessions, offering eight CIPS, the annual ethics training, the rolling conduct-code currency.

Where to start

Wherever you are on the pathway, the route is the same: study, practise, contribute, and claim each activity through the self-report form for the Society of Modern Applied Psychology (SoMAP) verification. The practice pathway article gives the level-by-level shape, and the full requirements live in the Achology CPD Handbook.
