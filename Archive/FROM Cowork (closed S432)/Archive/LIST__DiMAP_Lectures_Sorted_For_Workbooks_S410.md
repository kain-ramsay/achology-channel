## My proposal for the proving batch (Kain stops me there)

Three lectures from three categories, each a clear named idea with a strong exercise and little to cut:

1. **001-054, The Johari Window** (Personal Growth). Four quadrants of what you and others know about you. Fully taught, a strong exercise on one relationship. One slip to correct (the lecture ties the unknown quadrant to Freud; that is the lecturer's bridge, not Luft and Ingham's model), and a marriage example to replace with a generic one.
