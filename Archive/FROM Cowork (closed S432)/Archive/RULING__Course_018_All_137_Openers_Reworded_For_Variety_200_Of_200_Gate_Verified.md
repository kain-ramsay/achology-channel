RULING: Course 018, All 137 Formulaic Openers Reworded For Variety, 200 Of 200 Still Gate Verified

Context. After the 200 of 200 CQ018 quote pages were completed and Kain approved commissioning Code's import and score pass, a routine review while surfacing next task candidates found that 137 of the 200 records opened on an identical "If you have ever..." stem. This does not breach the S356 ruling's letter, which bars a page's opener being copied from the exemplar rather than any particular words, but it plainly risks the same stamped effect at the seven thousand page site scale that ruling was written to prevent. Reported to Kain, who approved rewording all 137 before Code ever sees them, in these words: "Want the 137 openers reworded for variety before Code ever sees them? YES please, thats a great suggestion Claude!"

What was done. All 137 opening paragraphs were rewritten from scratch, one at a time, across 7 batches of up to 20 records each. Every new opening keeps the embedded verbatim quote unchanged, keeps the focus keyword phrase exactly where the gate requires it (verbatim in the first tenth of the body, density between 1.0 and 1.5 percent), keeps the page speaking to the reader as you, and clears every other content_gate.py check: paragraph length, reading ease, banned vocabulary, internal and external links, image alt text. No two of the 137 share the same opening construction or connective phrasing.

Method note, for Code or a future Cowork session to reuse. Before drafting a replacement opener, check whether the focus keyword phrase already appears verbatim inside quote_text. Where it does, the new lead can safely omit the keyword, since quote_text alone already carries the required first tenth of body hit. Where it does not, true especially of the longer "why (a full phrase)" title style keywords, the new lead must carry the keyword itself, usually through a natural "which is why (keyword)" construction. Drafting blind and fixing density failures afterward, the batch one approach, cost far more rework than checking quote_text first, which is what batches three through seven did.

One further instance found and corrected. CQ018-007-1 carried a second, mid body use of the same "If you have ever..." construction. This was not part of the 137 opening line count, since it sat mid body rather than at the top of the page. A full corpus search confirmed this was the only such instance anywhere in the other 199 records. It has been reworded and re verified. This was Claude's own call, not put to Kain first, since it was a one line fix continuing the instruction he had just approved, on a single isolated instance rather than a pattern.

Verification. All 200 CQ018 markdown records were re run through content_gate.py this session, in full, not only the 137 that were touched. Result: 200 of 200 GATE: PASS.
