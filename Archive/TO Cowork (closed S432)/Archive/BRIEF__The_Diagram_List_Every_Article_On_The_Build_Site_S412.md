## What happened

Kain approved a new component at S412: the Achology Diagram, one simple hand-sketched picture that shows a model's parts at a glance. Five shapes are approved: four boxes (the Johari Window), triangle (Karpman), ladder (the Ladder of Inference), stacked levels (Maslow, never a pyramid), and loop (Thoughts Feelings Behaviour). The approved drawing of all five is in the Achology Diagram folder in Component Design Prototypes; open it to see what each shape can carry. The content rule is **DSRD 2 section 3.11, The Diagram Block**: read it in full before you start. It is the standard for this job.

Kain ruled that the articles already on the build site get their diagrams first, before any new content does.

## The job

Read every article you hold, of every article type, that has a page on the build site. That includes the instructor articles, the field authority articles, the hub question articles, the hub guides, the Seven Beliefs series, the rescued older articles and the DSM articles, wherever their records live. Not book notes, quote pages, help answers, workbooks or author biographies. Where you cannot tell whether a record's page is on the build site, list it and mark it "not sure", rather than leave it out or guess.

For each article, decide: does it teach a model whose parts or steps fit one of the five shapes? An article may carry up to three diagrams, one per model it teaches. **Never force one.** A made up picture on an idea with no shape is worse than none, and most articles may well get none. An idea with a real shape that fits none of the five is listed as "needs a new shape", with the shape described in one line, and never drawn into one of the five.

## What you file

One list, `LIST__The_Diagram_List_Every_Article_On_The_Build_Site_S412.md`, in FROM Cowork. One row per article, these columns:

| Column | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Article | its title and slug |
| Diagrams | none, or 1, 2 or 3 |
| Shape | one of the five, or "needs a new shape" |
| The words | each part's label, one to four words, with an optional short line, in order, taken from the article's own teaching and its own terms |
| Shaded and arrows | which part is shaded, if any, and what any arrow shows |
| Caption | one short line |
| Where it sits | the first words of the paragraph it follows: the paragraph that first names the model's parts |
| File name | per DSRD 2 section 3.11: the page's focus keyword where the diagram shows the page's own subject, otherwise the model's name, then `-diagram`, lower case, hyphens |
| Alt text | one plain sentence naming the model and its parts, carrying the same phrase as the file name |
| Workbook | the workbook this idea shares a diagram with, if one exists or is planned |

At the top of the list: articles read, articles with at least one diagram, total diagrams, articles marked "not sure", and models that need a new shape.
