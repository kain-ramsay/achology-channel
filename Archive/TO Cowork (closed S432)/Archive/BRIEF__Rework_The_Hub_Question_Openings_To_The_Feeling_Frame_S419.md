## Rules that bind

- **Echoes.** The old scene is often reused further down: a later heading, the closing paragraphs, the practice block, the picture idea, a worked example in the middle. After you change an opening, search the whole body for every word of the old scene and change what pointed back to it. A scene kept deliberately as a worked example in the body may stay if it is introduced there cleanly. The Mental Health set missed this the first time and had to be fixed.
- **Only the opening and what points back to it change.** Do not touch the research, the claims, the sources, the Achology view, the course paragraph or the metadata. If an opening change would alter a fact, stop and note it.
- Every opening is new (Part 3 rule 9): no moment, scene or first line reused from another article, and not the same phrase across your own set. Vary the way in: not every opening may begin "Part of you" or "You've been".
- No clock times, places or objects in the moment. Plain words. No banned phrases (Part 3 rule 8). No dashes.
- Never touch Kain's own words in any record where he wrote the paragraph.
- Keep each piece's status honest: reworked pieces are not Ready until the fresh check below passes.
