## The job: four placements, on the pages the table names

For each help answer on Code's failing list, using its existing focus keyword, which is already chosen and evidenced and **is never changed here**:

1. **`keywordInSubheadings`.** The phrase appears in at least one subheading. Where the answer has no subheading and does not need one, say so in the report rather than inventing structure; a 350-word answer with a forced H2 is worse than three lost points.
2. **`keywordIn10Percent`.** The phrase appears verbatim in the opening tenth of the body. On a short answer that is the first sentence or two, which is where the reader's own words belong anyway.
3. **`keywordInContent`.** The phrase appears verbatim in the body at least once more, at the density the gate's own counter reports.
4. **`keywordInMetaDescription`.** The phrase appears in the description, within 155 characters.

**The rule that governs all four: the sentence has to be one a person would write.** If a placement can only be made by bending a sentence into something nobody would say aloud, leave it, name the page and the test in the report, and let the three points go. A help answer that reads worse to gain a point has failed at the only job it has.

**Nothing else moves.** Not the answer's substance, not its length, not its address (Code's), not its keyword.
