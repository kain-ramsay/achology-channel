## What changes, and what does not
**Changes, per workbook:**
- **Learn becomes three pages** (the example shows how). Page 1: five or six paragraphs on why the idea matters, opening on a feeling or moment the reader knows before the model is named. Page 2: the idea as a textbook explains it, under the material's own sub-headings, key terms defined, and four to six references at the foot. Page 3: how the idea is used, with one running illustration of named people who end by concluding something, the signals that show the idea at work, how to use it on your own part first, where the idea stops holding, and a closing question that leads into Reflect. Learn runs about 1,400 to 1,900 words in all; the whole workbook about 2,200 to 3,000. These are guides.
- **Landing page body** (350 to 450 words, hard) and **subtitle**, written to the Standard and `workbook-landing-page` skill. None exists yet. Put the body under a `## Landing page body` section in the record (the gate reads that shape), its headings at ###, and the subtitle beside it.
- **Search title and description.** The title ending follows the name-length table in DSRD 2 section 1.7 (names of 13 characters or fewer: "Free Worksheet and Learning Exercise"; 14 to 22: "Free Worksheet and Exercise"; 23 to 35: "Free Worksheet"; 36 to 46: "Free [Name] Workbook"). Six of the recorded titles did not follow it. The description follows the skill's pattern and names the business Achology.com, 155 characters at most.
- The record's Sources list gains the page 2 references (it was "none").
- **The Before you begin page takes one set wording (Kain approved it S421).** Standard Part 17.6, bullet "Before you begin", gives it in full, and the exemplar shows it. Replace the two stock lines ("This workbook is a standalone resource..." and "It asks you to write, not just read.") with the two new lines, filling only the idea's name in the first. Keep the workbook's own bold "Before you begin, choose one..." sentence and any caution of its own exactly as the record has them, but cut the old tail ("You'll carry that one ... through the exercise.") and put the fixed tail in its place. Add the line "Write it here, in a line or two." above the writing space. Leave the lecture and course line untouched. Copy the fixed lines from the Standard, never from memory.

**Does not change, word for word:** the front cover, Reflect, Apply, Discuss and its fixed lead-in, the back cover, and the title and H1. Kain said Reflect, Apply and Discuss stay as they are. ## Rules that bind every page you write
- **The words Hook, Book, Look and Took never appear in a workbook**, as headings, labels or copy (Kain, S421). Learn pages are headed by number; page 2's sub-headings are plain and the material's own. Scan for the four words before every check.
- **Voice:** warm, wise, easy to read, insightful. "You" and contractions. **No "I".** Not dry, clinical, clipped or robotic. Named people in an illustration are introduced with "imagine" or "picture" and are never presented as real. No invented stories about the writer, no invented credentials or quotations.
- **Facts:** page 2 is accurate as a textbook. Every fact traces to the record's sourcing, read this session. Where the lecture slips, state the correct fact (Part 7 rules 1 and 5). A claim you cannot verify is left out, not softened. In the example, the one unverified line is the Winner's Triangle attributed to Acey Choy; do not carry it over unless you verify it.
- **References:** open and read every one live, this session. Each must be relevant, not a shopping link and not a page that sells. The link sits on the title of the work, two to five words. A reference that will not open is left out and written on the workbook's Owed to a person list.
- **Page 1 is written fresh and is not the companion article reprinted.** The articles are not written yet; do not wait for them.
- "In Achology's view" and any phrase like it: once at most per workbook. "Truly" never; "actually" once at most. No dashes of any kind. No help line, and no sentence sending a reader to a doctor, therapist or helpline. The word "download" never appears; the workbook is emailed.
- Paragraphs none over 120 words, no sentence over 40 words, no list of facts in place of prose on page 1 or page 3.
