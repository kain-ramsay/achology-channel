## 2. Kain's locked decision — name display

**Reviewer names display as first name plus initial ("Sarah T.") everywhere on
the site.** Rationale: the Trustpilot/Amazon convention, credible and genuine,
without broadcasting real people's full identities under the Achology brand.
The full name stays in the store (private); the short form is derived at render
time. This unblocks publishing reviews as social proof. Please log to the DSRD
that owns review display (DSRD 4 §14 / DSRD 5), so the decision has a spec home.

## 3. Per-review country — DROPPED (integrity decision, Kain 2026-07-24)

Per-review country is **not** being added. We cannot get true per-student
country from Udemy (only a high-level aggregate map exists), and inferring a
country from a name and printing it next to a real, named person turns a guess
into an asserted fact about that individual. That is the one thing that would
undermine the trust the reviews exist to build, so we do not do it.

The global-reach story is carried honestly instead by the **aggregate country
map** we already hold, shown as the Global Impact Block (DSRD 4 §14.2 Variant 1:
world map + "students from 216 countries"). True at the aggregate level, no
guessed country on any individual. That is a future build item on the relevant
page, not part of the review store.

## 3b. Topic tags — APPROACH DECIDED (Kain 2026-07-24)

The mechanism is settled, so it is not left open for guesswork later:

- Each review carries one or more **theme tags** from a fixed vocabulary. Tags
  are an INTERNAL routing label, never shown to the reader (the reader sees only
  words, star rating, first name plus initial, and course). A wrong tag is a
  quality nudge, never a false claim, so there is no integrity risk.
- Tags are generated by a **one-time AI pass** over the displayable reviews, run
  when we build the display components (not now; the empty field is added to the
  ACF group at that point, no data rework needed).
- Each display page **declares its theme** and pulls matching reviews
  automatically, on top of the course and school matching we already have. That
  is the whole value: the right proof lands on the right page by itself.

**Proposed starting vocabulary (10, to finalise at build):** confidence and
self-belief; career and professional growth; relationships and communication;
self-awareness and personal growth; coaching and helping others; mental
wellbeing and resilience; practical real-world application; teaching quality and
clarity; purpose and life direction; value and beginner-friendliness. Kain
approved the approach; the exact list is confirmed when the AI pass is built.
