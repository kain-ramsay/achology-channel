## What done looks like

The governing text is DSRD 9 section 20.0h, written home from RULING__The_Knowledge_Hub_Wall_Standard_S179. Built on the build site, every wall in the Knowledge Hub:

1. **Columns:** 4 on a computer, 3 on a tablet, 2 on a phone. The front page and the topic pages change.
2. **Cards:** a full wall holds 48 (the front page and the subject pages); a paged list 24 a page with numbers; a topic page 6 of each kind with View all.
3. **Mixing:** the front page and subject pages mix the kinds; lists and topic pages hold one kind per wall.
4. **Spreading:** whichever kind is fewer on that wall is spread evenly so no two of it touch, whichever kind that is.
5. **Quotes on mixed walls:** at most one a row, at most two from any one person.
6. **The Know Your Psychology logo card** on every wall, one at a column foot where there is room, so every wall ends level; the front page gains it. The one exception is the search page (DSRD 9 section 38.9, item 5).
7. **Rise in turn and lift on a pointer**, as already built (BRIEF__Rise_In_Turn_On_Every_Knowledge_Hub_Wall_S440).
8. **One piece of code lays out every wall**, replacing the three scripts.
9. **The quote card family is option 2, The card itself** (Kain, S179): every quote card on every wall is his signed quote card and nothing around it, in three shapes (the wall card and the list card upright, the wide card at the signed 1200 by 630 proportions), as section 20.0h describes. The quote page's own card and the downloadable picture do not change.

**Reach:** the front page, the seven subject pages, the kind lists (all and per subject), the topic pages, and the quote page's Browse More cards, which join the family. **Not in reach:** the search results page, which stays as it is; whether it ever becomes a wall is a separate question for Kain, open in section 20.0h.
