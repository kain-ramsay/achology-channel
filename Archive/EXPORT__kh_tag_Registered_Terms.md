## The slug rule, so you can predict it rather than look it up

WordPress lowercases the name, replaces spaces with hyphens, and drops apostrophes and other punctuation. Every one of the 36 follows that, so a new term's slug is predictable. Two worth noticing because they are the cases where a guess goes wrong: a name already carrying a hyphen keeps exactly one (`Beginner-Friendly` gives `beginner-friendly`), and an ampersand disappears rather than becoming "and".

**If the master file's tag names do not match these names exactly, send me the mismatches rather than inventing terms.** A near-miss name creates a second term with almost the same slug, and then the tag layer has two pages for one idea, which is the failure this export exists to prevent.
