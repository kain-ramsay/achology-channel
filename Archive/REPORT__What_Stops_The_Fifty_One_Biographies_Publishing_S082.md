## The one thing that actually stops them, and it is not an SEO problem

**Q7 on the article page's build sheet: nobody has settled what address a biography lives at.** DSRD 1 §3.2 says a biography "renders in place" as the body of the Author Hub at `/learn/authors/{slug}/`. S298 and S302 put it on the article template, and every record's own `address` field reads `/learn/{category}/articles/{slug}/`, which is where they currently render.

Both cannot be true. **Publishing 51 pages at an address that then moves creates 51 redirects and 51 pieces of lost authority**, which is the exact thing DSRD 1 §11's redirect map exists to avoid having to do.

This is the decision that unblocks everything else, and it is not Code's.

## Three things owed to the record rather than to the reader

1. **No DSRD 6 record covers this page type.** The gate looks for one declaring `carl-jung` and finds none, so Harness Rule 6 refuses to call any of them done. DSRD 6 §0's volume rule applies here: the machine chapters run on all 51, the human chapters run on a signed exemplar. **Nobody has named which biography is the exemplar.** One line from Chat and Code builds the record.

2. **The titles are on the site and not in the records.** Kain ruled the pattern in the sitting and Code applied it to 50 drafts; the records still carry the bare name, so the source of truth and the site disagree and a rebuild would revert them. Filed as `RULING__The_Author_Biography_Title_Pattern_S082.md`.

3. **The alt text is Code's wording**, "A stylised portrait of {Name}", because the author-biography contract carries no alt column where the instructor one does. It is factual rather than authored, and it is there because 51 images with no alt fails DSRD 6 §7 outright. Chat overwrites one field if it wants different words.

## The GEO position

**Not measurable by machine, and that is by design.** DSRD 6 §6 names a human reader as its runner.
