## What this run is

Twelve first-person reflective articles in Karen Ramsay's own name and voice, written as one interconnected narrative in four movements. The argument, in Karen's own words to Chat: managing a business and directing people has less to do with skills or knowledge and more to do with the ability to connect with people on an authentic level, to meet people where they are, to treat people with the trust and respect you would want yourself, and to hold a growth-oriented mindset, which is central to all creativity, entrepreneurial work and problem solving.

**Content type:** instructor-attributed article, per the `instructor-article` skill: 1,200 to 1,500 words, first person, reflective, Hook-Book-Look-Took under reader-facing H2s, a close on a real course. Gate type `instructor-article` in `content_gate_standards.json`.

**Two departures from the skill, named here rather than left for a drafter to discover.**

1. **The source is not a book.** Kain's articles draw on The Ultimate Life Coaching Handbook and Gerard's on The Skilled Helper. Karen has no book. Her source is the environment she built and runs: the Achology community, the people in it, and what she has learned directing a business and its people day to day. The "Book" movement of each article draws on that, on the named framework or study the article's question rests on (linked to its original), and on her own profile page bio (`/about/instructors/karen-ramsay/`, live on the install). Where a claim rests on research, it is sourced beside itself as the house bar requires.
2. **There is no Karen Ramsay voice profile in the vault.** The two instructor profiles are Kain's and Gerard's. So this run drafts **one exemplar first** (row 1) on the strongest model tier, and stops. Kain and Karen read it. Their word on the voice becomes the profile the other eleven are drafted to, and Chat writes that profile into the vault before row 2 starts. Do not draft rows 2 to 12 until a RULING lands here.

**The close of every article** is a real course, read from DSRD 5 in the session with one true fact. Karen's own authority is the community, so the natural close is the Achology Gateway free tier or the monthly membership rather than a single course; the exemplar tries the membership close, and Kain rules on it with the voice.
