# RULING AND SHIP: the Wise Quotes mark on the quote card is one size, and the shared picture takes the page's

**From Code, S110. Date: Thursday 10 September 2026. Shipped at theme 0.259.0,
deployed and read back off both live drawings.**
**Ruled by Kain** live in this sitting, on two photographs of the same card taken
from the build ground. His words:

> "Yes, make the logo the same size please"

**Owning documents:** DSRD 7 sections 15.2 and 16.2, and the card's own build
sheet in the quote page's design folder.
**Board card:** Quote page template.

---

## What was wrong

The card is drawn once, by one function, so that the picture people share cannot
drift away from the page it came from. One part of it was still measured twice,
in two different systems: the Wise Quotes mark took a share of the card's width
in the baked picture and a fixed height in pixels on the page.

Measured on both live renders before the change: **5.3 per cent of the card's
width on the page, 3.6 per cent baked.** So the picture the whole internet sees
carried a smaller mark than the card Kain approved at S109, and nothing said so.

**Where the pixel height came from, because it was not careless.** It was added
at S108 on his own ruling, so the mark matched the share block that then sat on
the card beside it. The controls came off the card at S109 and the block it was
matching went with them; the rule stayed.

## What was built

The mark is stated once now, in the card's own units, as a HEIGHT rather than a
width, so a differently proportioned lockup file can replace it without the
number meaning something else. The reversed lockup for the dark card drops
straight in when it exists. Its distance from the foot is derived from the
closing quote mark rather than typed, so it still follows if either is resized.

The page's own override is deleted rather than switched off.

**Read back after deploying, on both drawings:** 5.3 per cent on the page and
5.3 per cent baked, the mark's centre on the closing mark's centre line in both,
every picture loaded in both. The page itself did not move.

## The fold-back, under Harness Rule 14

The card's prototype is at its next version, `PROTOTYPE__Quote_Card_S110_APPROVED`
in the quote page's design folder, HTML and picture, with the build sheet updated
to match and the S109 pair archived. The card's build sheet gains one line under
what the card must never do: **measure any part of itself in two units.** One
card drawn once is not enough on its own, which is the lesson this cost.
