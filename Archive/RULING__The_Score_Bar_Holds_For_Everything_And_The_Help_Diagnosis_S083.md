## 3. Item 2, stated properly so Kain can rule it

The 18 instructor keywords are already good search phrases. `empathy in counselling`, `client resistance in counselling`, `internal versus external locus of control`. Nothing is wrong with them.

What does not match is the address, written as a title: `what-clients-hear-real-empathy`, `who-holds-the-master-controls`. Rank Math checks the keyword against the address, so the two never agree.

**Three routes, and Chat is asked to put them to Kain as rendered options rather than described ones:**

2. Change the phrases to match the addresses. Free, and throws away the search intent the phrases were chosen for.

**My own reading, offered and not acted on: route 3 for these 18.** They are drafts nobody has linked to, so route 1 costs little today, but the addresses read as editorial titles because Kain wanted them to, and that was a design decision, not an oversight.

## 4. The 250 help articles, measured

Kain's words in the sitting: **"The Help area is a problematic area that will need addressed as many are failing poorly."** He is right, and here is the measurement rather than the impression.

**250 articles. Average score 14.2. Median 8. None above 79.**

| Check | How many fail |
|---|---|
| Focus keyword appears nowhere in the body | **242 of 250** |
| Focus keyword in no heading | **250 of 250** |
| Focus keyword not even in the SEO title | **221 of 250** |
| Focus keyword not in the web address | 197 of 250 |
| No external link in the body | 245 of 250 |
| Body under 600 words | **250 of 250** |
| No internal link in the body | 0 of 250 |

**The internal linking is perfect and should be said out loud: every one of the 250 carries internal links.** That was real work and it holds up.

### The cause, and it is one cause

The focus keywords are the article's own question with the connecting words machine-stripped out. Read them beside their addresses:

| Address | Focus keyword | Score |
|---|---|---|
| `how-much-does-achology-cost` | `much Achology cost` | 8 |
| `achology-lifetime-access-explained` | `lifetime access mean Achology` | 8 |
| `does-achology-offer-a-money-back-guarantee` | `Achology offer money-back guarantee` | 8 |
| `achology-free-trial-introductory-offer` | `Achology offer free trial introductory rate` | 8 |

**Nobody types "much Achology cost".** It is not a phrase, it appears nowhere in the writing, and it fails every keyword check at once. This is the same fault as the biographies, in a more damaging form.

### The fix, and why it should be cheap

**The real phrase is already sitting in the article's own title.** `how much does Achology cost` is in the title, in the address, and almost certainly in the opening line, because the house standard for a help answer requires the article to open by restating the reader's question in their own words. The keyword the checker wants is the question the article already asks.

**The biography lift is the precedent: one mechanical keyword change moved 51 pages from an average of 60.6 to 78.6 with no copy touched.** I would expect a comparable lift here and I am not going to claim a number I have not measured. Chat authorises, I apply, Kain presses the button once, and we measure it the same way.

### One thing that will not be fixed by keywords, and Kain should hear it plainly

**All 250 are under 600 words, and Rank Math penalises that on every single one.** That is not a defect. DSRD 2's help-answer standard sets 320 to 1,500 words deliberately, because a support answer that pads itself to 600 words to please a plugin is a worse answer.

**So a bar of 80 on help articles is in direct conflict with the house content standard**, and no keyword work closes that gap. My recommendation, for Kain rather than for Chat: keep the bar at 80 where it belongs, on the Knowledge Hub articles, and set a separate and lower recorded bar for help answers, chosen after we see what the keyword fix alone achieves.
