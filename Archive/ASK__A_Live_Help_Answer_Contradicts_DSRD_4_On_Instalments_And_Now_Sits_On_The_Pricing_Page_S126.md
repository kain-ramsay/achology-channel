# ASK: a live help answer contradicts DSRD 4 on instalments, and it now sits on the pricing page

**From Code, S126. Found on the render, checked against the canonical DSRD and against the answer's own body on the install.**
**OWED BACK: a corrected body and excerpt for that help answer, and a decision on whether anything else in Help carries the same retired figures.**

---

## The finding

The pricing page's questions block was rebuilt this session on Kain's
instruction. It no longer carries six written answers; it is the help article's
own closing card, listing six real help answers drawn from the two price
categories. So live Help wording now sits on the page where people decide what
to pay, which is what turns this from a Help section detail into something
worth stopping for.

**One of the six is wrong.** `pay-instalments-achology-courses`, post 341, "Can
I pay for Achology courses in instalments?".

**Its body says, read off the install this session:**

> "Depending on the purchase you can spread it over two, three or in some cases
> four monthly payments, or pay in full."

**Its excerpt, which is the line the pricing page now prints under the question:**

> "Yes: courses, school bundles and the Access All Areas Pass split into two,
> three or four monthly payments."

**DSRD 4 section 1.1 says, quoted from the canonical file this session:**

> "The plans are not uniform (read off the live checkout by Code, S090, on four
> products): a single course offers three or five monthly instalments, a school
> bundle two, four or six, and the Access All Areas Pass three, six or twelve."

The answer is wrong on all three products, in both its body and its excerpt.

## Why this one is not a judgement call

DSRD 4 section 1.1 records the correction itself, in its own words:

> "This paragraph said 'two, three, or in some cases four' until S333, which
> understated the till."

**The help answer is still carrying the exact sentence DSRD 4 was corrected away
from at S333.** It was not reworded when the document was. So this is a known
correction that never reached the Help section, rather than a new disagreement
between two sources.

## What is NOT wrong, checked so nobody checks it twice

- The same answer's line that every course purchase includes three months of
  complimentary community access is correct: DSRD 4 section 1.1 states it and
  section 5 states the auto-continue at $34.50 after it.
- Its advice that the checkout is the definitive place to see the options is
  sound, and is worth keeping in whatever replacement is written. DSRD 4's own
  instruction points the same way: "because the counts differ by product, no
  page names a number."

## What is owed back

1. **A corrected body and excerpt for post 341.** Code does not draft content
   (Harness Rule 8), and this is a rewrite rather than a mechanical correction:
   more than one wording is defensible, so it is not Code's. DSRD 4's own line
   is the likely shape of it, "pay in full or spread it over monthly
   instalments, shown at checkout", but that is Chat's to settle with Kain.
2. **Whether anything else in Help carries the retired figures.** Code can
   search the 250 help records for the old phrasing in one pass and report, on
   your word. It has not been done and this file does not assume the answer.
3. **Nothing is held up meanwhile.** The pricing page ships with the answer as
   it stands, because taking a live help answer off the page would hide the
   fault rather than fix it, and because the six rows are chosen by category
   rather than named one at a time.

## One thing this says about the method, worth a line

The block was rebuilt to give the site one questions panel instead of two. The
first thing that fell out of joining them was a four-figure error in a live
answer that had sat unread since S333. Pulling real content onto a commercial
page is a check on that content, not only a saving in build.
