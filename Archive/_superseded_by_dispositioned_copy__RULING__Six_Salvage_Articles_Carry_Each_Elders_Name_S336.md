# RULING: up to six Salvage articles carry each elder's own name

**From:** Claude Chat, Session 336. **Date:** 3 September 2026.
**Ruled by:** Kain, this session, in the sitting that raised it.
**Applies to:** the Salvage run, Recipe 3, from batch three onward. Batch two is already briefed and running and is not changed by this file.
**Nothing else in Recipe 3 moves.** The record shape, the gate, the sourcing standard, the destination school and the address rule are all unchanged.

---

## What was ruled

Achology has six eldership members. Each of their profile pages carries a section headed with their own name promising their writing. Rather than commission new writing from six volunteers, **up to six Salvage articles are assigned to each elder as their byline**, drafted by Achology and published under their name.

Kain has already told all six that this is coming and that the first drafts may not read exactly as they would have written them. **They are free to send back an edited version of any of their six at any time and it replaces what is published, without argument.** He meets them monthly. That is the consent, and it is real.

## The assigning rule

**Genuine fit only, and the fit has to be obvious.** A piece is assignable when its central subject sits inside one of the six territories above, plainly enough that a reader who knows that person would nod. Most of the Salvage set will not qualify: it is largely psychology history and named studies, and nobody's name goes on a rebuild of the Milgram obedience study or Piaget's four stages. **An unassignable piece keeps its pen name and nothing about it changes.**

**Up to six each, never more.** Where a territory already holds six, a later match stays unassigned.

**Erika's territory overlaps Alec's and Gaby's.** Where two elders could both claim a piece, it goes to whichever of them holds fewest so far, so the six piles fill evenly.

**Cowork proposes, Chat assigns.** A drafting agent that thinks a row belongs to an elder names it in the batch report with the elder and one line of reasoning. It does not set the byline itself. Chat confirms or declines the assignment when the batch lands. This keeps one pair of eyes across all six piles, which no single batch can have.

## What changes on an assigned piece, and only on an assigned piece

**The author field carries the elder's slug** from the table above, in place of the pen name.

**The voice is the Achology base voice with no pen-name accent.** An editorial pen name's accent belongs to that pen name. A piece going out under a real person's name is written on the house bar and nothing more.

**Nothing in the body may claim the elder's personal experience.** No first-person anecdote, no "in my own practice", no case from a client, no biographical detail. They did not write it, and inventing a life for a real person is the one thing this arrangement cannot survive. The piece teaches its subject in the ordinary way; the name at the top says who stands behind it, not who lived it.

## The replacement route, when an elder sends their versions back

Their words win. But the page is one that already earns from search, so the swap is done rather than pasted:

1. The body text is replaced with theirs.
2. **The address, the H1 title and the focus keyword stay exactly as they are.** These are what the redirect and the search standing rest on, and a rewrite that changes them silently loses the traffic the Salvage run exists to keep.
3. `content_gate.py` is re-run on the record before it goes back up, and anything it fails is fixed in conversation with the elder rather than around them.

That is Achology's job and no elder needs to know it happens.

## What holds publication

**No elder-bylined article publishes until that elder's profile page is live.** All six pages exist as drafts and wait on Kain publishing them. The byline links to the profile, and a byline linking to nothing is worse than no byline.

Records may be drafted and imported before then. Only publication waits.

## One honest finding, so nobody plans around a number that may not exist

Batch two's twenty rows were read against the six territories this session. **Roughly three of the twenty qualify** (Egan's skilled helper model, Carl Rogers on person-centred counselling, and essential character traits for personal growth). At that rate the whole Salvage set yields somewhere near thirty rather than thirty-six, and the piles will not fill evenly.

Six each is the ceiling, never a quota. **No piece is stretched into a territory to reach a number.** Where the set falls short, the shortfall is reported and topped up later from another content type, or the elder simply has fewer.
