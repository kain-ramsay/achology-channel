# RULING: one cut exists at a time. Unapproved video is never kept.

**From:** Claude Code, Session 097. **Date:** 3 September 2026. **Session type:** factory.
**Ruled by:** Kain, this session, in his own words: *"They MUST go, you can never allow unapproved video files to be saved again."*
**Board card:** Manifesto Audio + Brand Film, and it belongs in the standards beyond that card.

---

## What he found

Three suffixed cuts of the same film sitting side by side in the manifesto folder, plus three music comparison versions and two superseded review reels. **A hundred and fifty five megabytes of video that nobody had approved and nobody could tell apart.**

He asked what I had missed, which is how it surfaced. I had not noticed it while working in that folder all evening.

## Why it matters more than tidiness

**The one canonical file rule already forbade this**, and I read that rule at the open of every session. I broke it three times in one evening anyway. That is the finding worth recording: **a rule that is only remembered is not kept.**

Video is the worst place for it. The files are enormous, they all look finished, and a folder holding three of them makes it impossible to say which one is the work. Somebody opening that folder in two months, Kain included, would have had to play all three to find out.

## What the ruling is

**One cut exists at a time**, under a name that says what it is rather than which attempt it was, and it is replaced in place. A comparison version is a temporary file outside the deliverable folder and it is deleted the moment the choice is made. Keeping a version is Kain's decision to make and never an assumption.

## What was done, not merely written down

**The superseded files are deleted**, all seven, on his instruction.

**The rule is a gate rather than a note.** `build35.py` in the Film Build System folder now refuses to render at all while any other film sits in the folder it writes to, and names the file that is in the way. **Proved rather than claimed:** a stray file was put there deliberately and the build refused, then the stray was removed.

## What Chat may want to do with it

This is broader than one film. If the standards should carry a line about deliverable media generally, that is yours to place and word. **Nothing is asked for here**; the rule is already enforced where films are built.
