So what does Achology actually know about you? Achology collects the personal data needed to run your account, deliver your courses, process payments, and operate its learning community: contact details, account and registration information, transaction records, communications, technical usage data, and marketing preferences. It does not intentionally collect special category data such as health information, ethnicity, religious beliefs, or political opinions. The full list is set out in the Privacy Notice, and what Achology actually holds about you depends on how you use the platform.

The categories, in plain terms

The Privacy Notice lists the categories of personal data Achology may collect. Not every category applies to every person.

  • Contact information: your name, postal address, email address, and telephone number.
  • Account and registration details: login credentials, profile information, and account activity.
  • Transaction and service information: courses purchased, events attended, certifications awarded, and credits earned.
  • Communications: emails, support enquiries, and other correspondence with Achology.
  • Images and recordings: photographs, video, or audio from events where recording takes place and participants have been informed.
  • Technical and usage data: device and browser information collected through cookies, explained in the Cookie Policy.
  • Marketing preferences: your choices about receiving communications.
  • Financial information: payment details, handled securely and in most cases processed by third-party payment providers rather than stored by Achology.

Two further categories apply only in specific situations: recruitment information if you apply for a role, and business or professional information where it is relevant to the services provided.

Special category data is not collected

Achology does not intentionally collect what UK data protection law calls special categories of personal data: health information, racial or ethnic origin, religious or philosophical beliefs, political opinions, trade union membership, genetic or biometric data, or sexual orientation. If you volunteer something of that kind yourself, for example in a community discussion, it is not used for any specific purpose and is handled under the Privacy Notice.

Where this is set out

The Privacy Notice is the full statement of what is collected, why, and on what lawful basis. If you have a question about your own data, contact support@achology.com or use the contact form.
