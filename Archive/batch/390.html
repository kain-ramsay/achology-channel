So how do all these policy documents fit together? Achology publishes seven policy documents designed to be read as a single framework, with the Community Code of Conduct alongside them: each covers one dimension of the relationship between Achology and its learners, and each cross-references the others. The Disclaimers carry a map of how they fit together, and no document is meant to be read in isolation.

What each document covers

  • Terms and Conditions: the contractual mechanics, your order, access rules, cancellation rights, and liability.
  • Refunds Policy: the financial boundaries, including the 14-day money-back guarantee and its scope.
  • Privacy Notice: what personal data is collected, why, and your rights over it.
  • Cookie Policy: cookies and tracking on the website.
  • Trust Statement: the working philosophy on responsibility, boundaries, and who the environment is for.
  • Disclaimers: the scope limits, what Achology does not provide, and reliance boundaries.
  • Accessibility Statement: the access commitment.
  • Community Code of Conduct: the conduct standard for the learning community, named in the Terms as part of the entire agreement.

How they connect

The Terms are the contractual spine: their entire-agreement clause names the Trust Statement, Disclaimers, and Community Code of Conduct as part of the agreement, and the Refunds Policy forms part of the Terms directly. Where the Terms and the Refunds Policy are ever inconsistent, the Terms state that they prevail, except where consumer law requires otherwise. Your statutory rights sit above all of them, covered in What are my legal rights as an Achology customer?

Where to start reading

Start with the document that owns your question: money in the Refunds Policy, data in the Privacy Notice, conduct in the Community Code of Conduct, boundaries in the Disclaimers and Trust Statement. Each will point you to the others where they overlap.
