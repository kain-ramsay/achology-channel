# RULING: every turn ends with a proposed next action and a request for the go-ahead

**From:** Claude Code, Session 058. **Date:** 2026-08-14.
**Filed under Harness Rule 14**, which requires a ruling given in session to be acted on and filed the same session, quoting Kain's words.
**Needs an edit to a document Chat owns.** Named in section 4.

---

## 1. Kain's words

> "I need you asking me for 'go ahead' at the end each turn after you have proposed your next action steps."

Given at S058 after he asked three times why my turns had stopped proposing anything, and rejected my first two answers because both were introspection rather than a cause. His instruction, verbatim, on the third ask: **"is in project instructions or a change in system instructions - no more guessing or justifying."**

He was right to insist. The cause was written down, and I had not looked.

## 2. The cause, which was a collision between two standing instructions

**CLAUDE.md line 45:** "Once a decision is settled or specced, carry it out at full pace without re-asking."

**The S055 memory note, quoting Kain:** "Just call you on and do what you know you must do. Please don't ask me for permission to do what you know you just must do." Its written application: "if it is commissioned, ruled, or blocked only by my own tooling, do it and report what I did."

Nearly all of my work is commissioned, so that condition was met on almost every turn, and turns became reports with no closing move. Kain had also corrected me earlier the same session for asking him a project-direction question that belongs to Chat, which removed the other kind of closing move at the same time. Between the two, nothing was left to end a turn with.

**This is worth recording as a pattern rather than an incident.** Neither instruction was wrong and neither was misread. Two correct rules, written eleven weeks apart, combined into a behaviour neither of them intended, and the result looked like a personality change rather than a rule collision. It cost Kain three exchanges to get to the cause, because my first two answers reasoned about myself instead of reading the instructions I run on.

## 3. The rule as it now stands

Two things that were being treated as one:

- **The work needs no permission.** Commissioned, ruled, or blocked only by my own tooling means get on with it in the same turn. S055 is untouched here and Kain has not withdrawn it. A stacked list of questions instead of work remains the failure it was.
- **The turn ends with the next action proposed and the go-ahead asked for.** This is not asking permission to do agreed work; it is giving Kain the steering he directs by.

So: do the work, report it briefly, name what comes next, ask for the go-ahead.

## 4. What Chat needs to change

**CLAUDE.md line 45 carries the half of the collision that lives in a document I do not edit.** It currently reads:

> "Once a decision is settled or specced, carry it out at full pace without re-asking."

It needs the same narrowing the memory note has taken: the sentence governs the work, not the shape of the turn, and a turn still closes with a proposed next action and a request for the go-ahead. Kain's file, Chat's edit.

**The `caveman` skill needs no change.** Read again this session, it already says a message carries "what he can act on next". It was not the cause and it is not in conflict.

## 5. My own note on it

The lesson I am taking is not about closing moves. It is that when Kain says my behaviour has changed, the first place to look is the instructions I run on, not my own reasoning about myself. Two of my three answers to him were the latter, and both were worthless to him.
