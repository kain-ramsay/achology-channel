# RULING AND SHIP: Download, Share and Copy are one group beneath the card, with Download carrying the emphasis

**From Code, S110. Date: Thursday 10 September 2026. Shipped at theme 0.267.0,
deployed and read back on the live page at 1440 and 390.**
**Ruled by Kain** live in this sitting. His words:

> "Can you please group Download, Share and Copy neatly beneath the card,
> consider your use of spacing wisely, with Download receiving slightly more
> emphasis."

**Owning documents:** DSRD 7 section 4.3 for the spacing, and the quote page's
build sheet in its design folder.
**Board card:** Quote page template.

## The spacing is the grouping, and the four numbers are one decision

  - **8** from a mark to its own word
  - **24** between one control and the next
  - **24** down from the card to the row
  - **48** on from the row to the listen bar's line

**Eight against twenty four is three to one**, so a mark belongs to its own word
and not to the word before it. That ratio is the whole reason the row reads as
three things rather than six.

**Twenty four against forty eight is one to two**, so the row belongs to the card
above it rather than to the writing below. Nothing is drawn to say so.

**The 24 above was 16.** Under a card standing 336 tall the controls were almost
touching it, and the row read as stuck to the card's foot rather than sitting
beneath it.

## The emphasis is type, never a button

Download takes weight 700 in full white; Share and Copy take weight 500 at 72 per
cent white. **All three keep the same size**, because stepping Download up to the
16 of the type scale would make it taller than its neighbours, which breaks the
group he asked for in order to emphasise a member of it.

**A filled primary control is the obvious answer and is the one he has already
refused.** His S109 ruling on these same controls was "No box, with words",
because printed inside a border they made the card look like a control panel, and
the card is the thing people share. That ruling holds here.

## Two things found by measuring rather than by looking

**The three were not the same height.** Download is an anchor and the other two
are buttons, and a button brings its own line height from the browser. Centring
hid most of it, which is how it survived: the row was three things centred near
each other rather than one line. One line height is now stated for all three.

**The card's 68 exception to DSRD 7 section 4.3 is retired**, and this is where it
went rather than being quietly dropped. It existed because the card sat directly
above the listen bar's line with nothing between them, so the button on that line
ate the gap. The share row is between them now, and the line takes section 4.3's
plain 48. One registered exception fewer.

## One thing worth knowing about this row

**Share is not always there.** The control deletes itself where the browser has
no share sheet, which is every desktop browser except Safari, so most of my own
photographs of this page have shown two controls and Kain has been looking at
three. The test browser is now given the same capability his has, and the row is
built to read correctly at two and at three.

## The fold-back, under Harness Rule 14

The page's prototype is re-exported at 0.267.0. The build sheet gains a section
for these controls carrying all three of his rulings on them, and its spacing
table now holds the row's four numbers with the reasoning that ties them
together.
