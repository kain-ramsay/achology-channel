## What is ruled, in his own order

1. **The mark is rebuilt from the vector master, not edited as pixels.** Rendered at 3x from `Know_Your_Psychology_Graphic.ai`, whose nine pages are the two generics and the seven school versions.

2. **The YOUR block leaves #666666 for soft grey #5E6B75.** His words: "Lets replace the YOUR background colour with our accepted Grey." It had been a pure neutral, and every grey in DSRD 7 section 1 is blue-leaning, so that block had never been on our palette. Soft grey is the only one of the five that carries white lettering without becoming a second dark slab.

3. **All three blocks share one gradient**, on the axis the artwork already used, which measurement showed runs purely left to right with no vertical component at all.

4. **The direction runs dark on the left into full colour on the right**, on the orange and the school block. He asked for the opposite first, looked at it, and reversed it: "can we shift the tint so it goes from left (darker) to right (lighter)". **The grey block was deliberately left running the other way**, because he named two blocks and not three.

   | Block | Left | Right |
   |---|---|---|
   | KNOW | orange hover #D85A1B | brand orange #ED6922 |
   | YOUR | soft grey #5E6B75 | brand dark #354149 |
   | PSYCHOLOGY | that school's secondary | that school's primary |

5. **The bulb is lit, and its glow is the treatment.** Chosen from four rendered options against a literal warm ramp, a lit-glass version and a rays version: "Lets go with the glow."

6. **The glow is Soft**, chosen from three strengths, then **softened at its core** on a second look: the peak mix came down from 0.46 to 0.26 with the reach untouched, so the light carries as far but stops concentrating into a hot spot behind the glass.

7. **The halo is white, not warm.** His words: "could we change the background glow from yellow to white." Only the glass and filament stay warm. It is the truer reading of light and it keeps each block unmistakably its own colour, which matters more on these seven than anywhere, since colour is the only thing that distinguishes them.

8. **The block carries a contour pattern.** Chosen from four rendered options, against a literal fingerprint, a fingerprint whose core was the bulb, and ripples spreading from it: "contours looks great". Topographic lines at five per cent, drawn in the block's own colour lightened rather than in white, clipped to the block, never touching the lettering or the bulb, and fading before the rounded corners.

## And one thing he ruled by NOT ruling it

**The school colours stand.** You have that already in `CLOSED__The_School_Colour_System_Is_Not_Being_Reopened_S281`. Everything above is built on DSRD 7 section 2's current seven. The lifted palette I had rendered, chroma raised to 0.55 with the plum held at 0.45 on his word, is parked rather than lost, and the measurement behind it stands whatever happens to it.

## What this does NOT settle, and it is the important half

**These are still recoloured renders, not shipping artwork.** Nothing above can retire the 2023 files until the Illustrator master is recoloured to the same values, for the reason the retirement note gives: the next export from that file otherwise reinstates the old palette and we hold two truths with the wrong one as the source.

**So there is no prototype to export yet under Rule 14's fold-back**, and I am not manufacturing one from pixels. The signed record for this component is the recoloured master plus its exports, and it is owed by whoever holds the artwork. When it exists I will file it.

**Where the work is meanwhile.** `_proposed_S063` holds the seven at both sizes with the page he judged them on; `_bulb_options_S063` and `_pattern_options_S063` hold the option sets, because the rejected options are the record of why the chosen one won. All three sit beside the artwork in Website-Wide Assets rather than in temporary space, after the scratchpad was wiped mid-session and took the first build with it.

## For DSRD 8, when you fold this in

The values worth recording are the three gradient pairs at point 4, the glow's three numbers (reach 1.35 of the bulb's width, strength 0.26, falloff 1.7), the pattern's five per cent, and the rule that the bulb's warm tone is picked per school as the tone furthest in hue from that school's own block, with a legibility floor overriding it where a cool block would otherwise take a bulb too dark to read as lit.
