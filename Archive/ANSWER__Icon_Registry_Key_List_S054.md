## Three things the rewrite should carry, because they are traps

1. **`library`, `library-big` and `bookshelf` are three different drawings** under three near-identical names, and two of them were misnamed until S255. `library` is Lucide's Library (four leaning strokes), `library-big` is LibraryBig (the shelf, and since S262 it means the Knowledge Hub only), `bookshelf` is not Lucide at all.
2. **`circle-question-mark` and `message-circle-question` are both in use and are not the same mark.** One is a circled question mark, the other a speech bubble. Ten callers and one caller respectively.
3. **`quote` deliberately carries two jobs**, the written-reviews figure and the quote content type, ruled at S262 and explicitly not to be split.

## What is NOT in the registry, and is meant not to be

The three groups Kain kept out at S262: breadcrumb separators (3 sites), the footer social marks (6), and artwork (the About era chart). Plus the two filled play triangles, which are not outline glyphs. Plus `faq-icons.php`, a second generated registry for the FAQ category set, which Chat ruled stays separate. Plus ten recorded drift pairs left inline, listed in `REPORT__Icon_Registry_Sweep_S054.md` section 5.
