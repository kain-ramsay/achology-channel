## Why this follows from the heading

The block's new supporting line, his own words, reads "All course bundles grant lifetime access and include a 12-month Achology membership." The card's fact line said the same thing a second time, one line further down. He saw it immediately.

## His three rulings

**One. The third fact goes, from all seven cards.** In his words: "because the subheading now says all course bundles grant lifetime access and include a twelve month Achology membership, we don't need to repeat ourselves in the next line down ... we can lose the twelve months of membership included completely from all cards."

**Two. The two facts that remain move up under the school name, and take capitals.** "Move the number of courses and number of hours up underneath the main school name. All I would ask you to do is capitalize the C in Courses and capitalize the H in Hours ... and then we lose that line completely and therefore shorten the cards."

This reverses a change made earlier the same session for a reason that no longer holds. Three facts would not fit beside the artwork and broke onto a second row, which is why the line had been moved out of the head. Two fit at every width, so the line returns to the head, fills the empty space under the name, and stops costing the card a row of its own.

**Three. The list loses the rule above its first course and below its last, and the spacing is worked out rather than left where it fell.** "We can lose the hairline above the top course and beneath the bottom course. If you please calculate the spacing. Don't just remove and, you know, please calculate this and do this well."

## The calculation, since he asked for one

Every number is a step of DSRD 7 section 4's scale. Nothing is hand-typed and no new value is created.

Inside the list a course carries 8 above it and 8 below it, so two courses read 16 apart, with a rule drawn through the middle of that space. A boundary has to read as wider than the rhythm inside it or the list stops holding together as one group. The next step up the scale is 24.

24 is what the card already gives at both ends, with no new declaration at all: the card is a flex column with a 16 gap, and a row carries its own 8. So the banner's bottom edge to the first course reads 16 plus 8, and the last course to the price band's rule reads 8 plus 16. Equal at both ends, wider than the rhythm inside, and unbroken white against a rhythm that is split by a rule.

**Measured on the rendered page at 1440** rather than asserted: the head is 137 tall on all seven cards, both gaps are 16 box to box on every one of the seven lists, and the card stands about 35 shorter than before this change.

**The two rules that stay are not this list's.** The one above is the banner's own bottom edge and the one below is the price band's own top edge. Both are rules inside a component, which DSRD 7 section 4.3 leaves to the component: "it does not govern rules inside a DSRD 8 component (a card footer, a card's stats row ...), which keep their own specified values."
