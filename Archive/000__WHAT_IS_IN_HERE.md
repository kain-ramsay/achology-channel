# Archive (the channel's record)

## What this folder is for

This is where dispositioned channel traffic goes, and it is the one archive in the estate that gets a map, because it is the one archive anyone actually searches. Live rulings, verified facts, and Code's session records land in here after they are handled, and they get retrieved: a question about what was ruled, measured, or reported often has its answer in a file here. The specification names this exception deliberately.

## What belongs here, and what does not

**Belongs:** every channel file that has been read and dispositioned, from either direction, kept whole and never deleted.

**Does not belong:** unhandled files (they stay in TO Chat or FROM Chat until dispositioned), or any document's one home. A ruling archived here has already been written into the document that owns it; the copy here is the record of the exchange, not the live truth.
